We rate every Silverado 1500 year Avoid when it has the 5.3-liter or 6.2-liter V8, so there is no best used year for those engines. Engine is the top complaint part on every Silverado from 2019 to 2025, 458 of the 2021’s 866 complaints name it, and NHTSA’s open investigation EA25007 covers 6.2-liter V8 engine failures that Chevrolet’s recall 25V-274 leaves out; owners on Reddit report lifter and camshaft failures on the 5.3 too. The 2.7-liter turbo, 4.3-liter V6 and 3.0-liter diesel trucks aren’t covered by that call, but NHTSA counts complaints by part, not by engine, so this table can’t rate them on their own. Silverado generations: what changed

Third generation (K2XX) 2014–2018

The 2016 to 2018 model years are this truck, with a 4.3-liter V6 and 5.3- and 6.2-liter V8s and, from 2015, an eight-speed automatic on the 6.2 (Wikipedia). GM renamed it the Silverado LD for 2019, a double cab only with the 5.3, and sold it beside the new truck until December 2019; a 2022 Silverado Limited was a separate stop-gap.

Fourth generation (T1XX) 2019–2026

It went on sale in August 2018 as a 2019 model with a 2.7-liter turbo four, a 4.3-liter V6 through 2021, 5.3- and 6.2-liter V8s, and a 3.0-liter Duramax diesel added for 2020, with six-, eight- and 10-speed automatics (Wikipedia). The 2022 refresh brought a new front end and interior and dropped the V6, and the 2.7 was renamed TurboMax for 2024.

Common Silverado problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 6,569 Silverado complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Engine 2019–2025 model years1,868 complaints naming engine, 70 per 100,000 sold. Engine tops every year from 2019 to 2025: 431 on the 2019, 458 on the 2021. Recall 25V-274 covers 6.2-liter V8s from 2021 to 2024, where a connecting rod or crankshaft defect can lead to engine failure; dealers inspect and repair or replace the engine, free. NHTSA’s EA25007 counted 1,157 reports of 6.2-liter engine failure across GM trucks and SUVs when it opened, 173 of them outside the recall. Owners on Reddit also report lifter and camshaft failures tied to cylinder deactivation on 5.3 and 6.2 V8s.
  • Transmission and drivetrain 2016–2022 model years1,607 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 55 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s label spans the transmission, driveshaft and axles. Recall 20V-650 covers a driveshaft joint weld on 2019–2020 trucks that can separate the shaft and cut propulsion, and 24V-797 and 26V-083 cover diesel transmission control valve software on 2020–2022 trucks. Owners on Reddit report transmission and torque converter failures between 80,000 and 160,000 miles.
  • Brakes 2016–2017 model years487 complaints naming brakes, 55 per 100,000 sold. Brakes lead the 2016 (265) and rank second on the 2017 (222). Recall 19V-645 (2014–2018 trucks) has dealers reprogram the brake control module because the vacuum pump’s output can fall over time and lengthen stopping distances. NHTSA closed PE18012 on hard brake pedal from the vacuum pump on 2014 to 2016 trucks after GM added six-year, 72,000-mile special coverage in December 2018, which may have lapsed on an older truck.
  • Electrical system 2019–2022 model years570 complaints naming electrical system, 36 per 100,000 sold. Electrical is third on the 2019 (192) and the 2022 (143). NHTSA’s label names a part, not a failure. Recalls 19V-888 (2019–2020, a cable ring with excess glue that can make an intermittent alternator connection) and 19V-889 (2019, a brake control module fault that can disable stability control and ABS without a warning light) are the best-documented items.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Silverado: 506 mentions in 157 threads, 123 positive and 305 negative, a net of −34 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Silverado is 40 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/Silverado and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Chevrolet Silverado 1500 price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Owners on Reddit describe a 5.3 that runs past 150,000 miles with few repairs until its lifters fail, and report 6.2 failures even on trucks the recall covered, which fits NHTSA’s EA25007. The 2.7 turbo is called trouble free so far. The threads rarely name a model year, so they back the engine pattern in the table without confirming any one year.

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Engine connecting rod and crankshaft 2021–2024 6.2-literRecall 25V-274. Manufacturing defects in the connecting rod or crankshaft can lead to engine damage and failure. Dealers inspect the engine and repair or replace it as necessary; trucks that pass get higher-viscosity oil and a new filter. Repairs are free.
  • Front upper control arm welds 2016–2017Recall 16V-256. Inadequate welds near the control arm bushing can let the arm separate and compromise steering. Dealers replace both upper arms and realign the truck, free; GM told owners not to drive until repaired.
  • Brake vacuum pump 2016–2018Recall 19V-645. The vacuum pump’s output can drop over time, reducing brake assist and lengthening stopping distances. Dealers reprogram the brake control module, free.
  • Driveshaft joint weld 2019–2020Recall 20V-650. A driveshaft joint may not have been welded properly and can separate while moving, cutting propulsion. Dealers replace the driveshaft, free.
  • ESC and ABS software 2019Recall 19V-889. A software error can disable stability control and ABS without lighting their warning lamps. Dealers reprogram the brake control module, free.
  • Transmission control valve 2020–2022 dieselRecall 24V-797 and 26V-083. On diesel trucks the transmission control valve can fail and lock the rear wheels. 24V-797 updates the software and GM covers transmissions the software flags; 26V-083 re-inspects trucks that were repaired incorrectly under it.
  • Roof-rail air bag inflators 2016–2017Recall 26V-611. An inflator end cap can detach or the inflator can rupture and throw fragments into the cabin. Dealers replace both roof-rail modules, free; owner letters were mailed October 21, 2026.
  • Transfer case 2026 4WDRecall 26V-289. A missing component in the transfer case can lock the front or rear wheels without warning. GM advises owners not to drive an affected truck until a dealer inspects and, if needed, replaces the transfer case, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Loss of power from 6.2-liter engine failure 2019–2024EA25007, open. NHTSA opened the engineering analysis on October 23, 2025 after 1,157 reports of L87 6.2-liter engine bearing failure, 173 of them outside recall 25V-274’s scope. Its opening summary names 2019 to 2024 Silverado 1500s and GMC Sierra 1500s, among other GM trucks and SUVs.
  • Air bag warning light 2020–2021PE21010, closed. NHTSA closed it in August 2023. It looked at rust particles in the driver air bag connector that could light the air bag warning lamp and, rarely, stop the bag from deploying, on trucks built during a seven-month pandemic production gap, and found the failure rate low and easy to spot.
  • Brake vacuum pump 2014–2016PE18012, closed. NHTSA closed it in April 2022 after GM’s special coverage for the vacuum pump and the later recall 19V-645.

What to check before you buy a used Silverado

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Chevrolet dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The 6.2-liter V8 on a 2021 to 2024.Ask for the dealer’s record showing the 25V-274 inspection was done and how the truck passed. EA25007 is open on engines the recall doesn’t cover, so a clean recall record isn’t a clean bill.
  • The 5.3 and 6.2 lifters.Owners on Reddit report lifter and camshaft failures tied to cylinder deactivation. Ask for oil change records and have a shop scan for misfire codes.
  • The driveshaft and brake module on a 2019 or 2020.Confirm 20V-650 (driveshaft weld) and, on a 2019, 19V-889 (stability control and ABS software).
  • The brake vacuum pump on a 2016 to 2018.Confirm 19V-645 was done; GM’s special coverage ran six years or 72,000 miles from December 2018 and may be over.
  • The roof-rail air bags on a 2016 to 2018.Recall 26V-611 (2016–2017) was filed in September 2026 and 24V-756 covers the 2018; the VIN lookup shows whether they are open.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine for codes, the transmission for shift faults and the front suspension before you sign.

Common questions

What are the worst Chevrolet Silverado years?

With a 5.3-liter or 6.2-liter V8, every one: we rate 2016 to 2026 Avoid. By the numbers the 2019 draws the highest rate, 253 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold, and 458 of the 2021’s 866 complaints name the engine.

What is the best used Silverado year?

With a 5.3-liter or 6.2-liter V8 we don’t have one. The 2025 draws the lowest rate, 32 complaints per 100,000, and the 2020 draws 173, but engine is the top complaint part on both and we rate them Avoid like the rest. The 2.7-liter turbo, 4.3-liter V6 and 3.0-liter diesel trucks aren’t covered by that call, but NHTSA counts complaints by part, not by engine, so this table can’t rate them on their own.

Is the 2023 Silverado reliable?

It draws 113, which is low, but 182 of its 406 complaints name the engine and NHTSA’s EA25007 is open on 6.2-liter failures. We rate it Avoid with a 5.3-liter or 6.2-liter V8, and if you buy one anyway we’d want the engine and recall history in writing.

Which Silverado engine has the most problems?

NHTSA counts complaints by part, not by engine, so these figures can’t say. Engine is the top part on every year from 2019 to 2025, and the recall and the open investigation both concern the 6.2-liter V8.

How do I check a used Silverado for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that truck; recall repairs are free at a Chevrolet dealer.
