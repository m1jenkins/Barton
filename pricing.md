# Drive Right pricing

Updated: 2026-09-18

Drive Right offers two flat-fee car-buying service plans for buyers nationwide through remote support. Drive Right is a service-area business based in Austin, not a vehicle dealer or a storefront in every listed metro.

## Full Service

- Service fee: $295 USD one time
- Includes: New, used, and certified pre-owned inventory search; price negotiation and fee review; a dedicated advisor through the buying process; and documented vehicle recommendations
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#full-service

## Ultimate Concierge

- Service fee: $895 USD one time
- Includes: Everything in Full Service, plus searches across auctions, forums, and niche sources; priority communication and coordination; and delivery coordination where available
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#concierge

## Costs not included

Service fees do not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs. Inventory, seller participation, timing, financing, and purchase outcomes vary. The buyer reviews the vehicle, deal terms, and final paperwork and makes every purchase decision.

For the authoritative current checkout scope and terms, see https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html and https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/how-it-works.html.

The standalone AI Agent offer is retired for new sales. Historical paid engagements remain supported. This local candidate requires coordinated Stripe activation; see docs/seo-execution/release-candidate.md.
