# Drive Right pricing

Updated: 2026-09-14

Drive Right offers three flat-fee car-buying service plans for buyers across Texas. Drive Right is a service-area business based in Austin, not a vehicle dealer or a storefront in every listed metro.

## AI Agent Buying Service

- Service fee: $195 one time
- Includes: AI-assisted vehicle research, dealer outreach, email negotiation where available, tailored options organized in a deal sheet, and buyer review before any purchase decision
- Upgrade note: A $100 credit may apply toward Full Service; confirm current terms before booking
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#consultation

## Hire Drive Right

- Service fee: $495 one time
- Includes: New, used, and certified pre-owned inventory search; price negotiation and fee review; a dedicated advisor through the buying process; and documented vehicle recommendations
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#full-service

## Ultimate Concierge

- Service fee: $895 one time
- Includes: Everything in Hire Drive Right, plus searches across auctions, forums, and niche sources; priority communication and coordination; and delivery coordination where available
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#concierge

## Costs not included

Service fees do not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs. Inventory, seller participation, timing, financing, and purchase outcomes vary. The buyer reviews the vehicle, deal terms, and final paperwork and makes every purchase decision.

For the authoritative current checkout scope and terms, see https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html and https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/how-it-works.html.
