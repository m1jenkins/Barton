# Drive Right pricing

Updated: 2026-10-23

Drive Right offers two flat-fee car-buying service plans for buyers nationwide through remote support. Drive Right is a service-area business based in Austin, not a storefront in every listed metro.

## Drive Right

- Service fee: $395 USD one time
- Best for: Buyers who know the car they want
- Includes: One model, with any trims, colors, and packages within that model; new, used, and certified pre-owned dealer inventory; price negotiation and fee review; one dedicated advisor from start to finish; and written recommendations (what we found and what we would do)
- Not included: Switching to a different model
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#full-service

## Drive Right Pro

- Service fee: $695 USD one time
- Best for: Buyers torn between a few cars, such as a BMW X5 and a Mercedes GLE, or a RAV4 Hybrid and a CR-V Hybrid
- Includes: Everything in Drive Right, plus offers on up to three models priced side by side (a model is a make and model; trims, colors, and packages within a model do not count extra); dealers for each model played against each other's written offers; out-of-state dealers, with help coordinating shipping when the out-of-state dealer is open to it; first-in-line service; and our pick of the best deal, judged on out-the-door price plus resale and running costs, not just the sticker
- Current plan: https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html#concierge

## Both plans

- One dedicated advisor from start to finish
- Full refund if you cancel before negotiations begin.
- Dealers and lenders pay us nothing.

## Upgrading

A Drive Right client can upgrade to Drive Right Pro later by paying the $300 difference.

## Costs not included

Service fees do not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs. Inventory, seller participation, timing, financing, and purchase outcomes vary. The buyer reviews the vehicle, deal terms, and final paperwork and makes every purchase decision.

For the authoritative current checkout scope and terms, see https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/schedule.html and https://www.driverightcarbuying.com/how-it-works.html.
