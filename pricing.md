# Steer Me Right pricing

Updated: 2026-11-26

Steer Me Right offers three flat-fee car-buying service plans for buyers nationwide through remote support. Steer Me Right is a service-area business based in Austin, not a storefront in every listed metro.

## Steer Me Right

- Service fee: $395 USD one time
- Best for: Buyers whose car is within 200 miles
- Includes: One model, with any trims, colors, and packages within that model; dealers within 200 miles of the buyer’s ZIP code; new, used, and certified pre-owned dealer inventory; price negotiation and fee review; one dedicated advisor from start to finish; and written recommendations (what we found and what we would do)
- Not included: Switching to a different model; dealers more than 200 miles away
- Current plan: https://www.steermeright.com/#full-service

## Steer Me Right Pro

- Service fee: $695 USD one time
- Best for: Buyers who want the best deal anywhere in the country
- Includes: Everything in Steer Me Right, plus dealers nationwide, not just within 200 miles; shipping quotes on long-distance deals (the buyer pays the carrier); trade-in support (we compare offers on the trade-in from several areas and negotiate it with the deal); offers on up to three models priced side by side (a model is a make and model; trims, colors, and packages within a model do not count extra); and our pick of the best deal, judged on out-the-door price plus resale and running costs, not just the sticker
- Current plan: https://www.steermeright.com/#concierge

## Steer Me Right Private Client

- Service fee: $1,995 USD one time
- Best for: Buyers of cars with an MSRP of $100,000 or more, new or used
- Includes: Everything in Steer Me Right Pro, for as many models as the buyer likes; allocation and build-slot search, and factory orders, when the car is not on a lot; negotiation of market adjustments and dealer-installed add-ons; an independent inspection arranged on a used car (the buyer pays the shop); a lease and loan check (money factor, residual and rate markup); a review of the buyer’s order and finance-office products before signing; enclosed transport quotes (the buyer pays the carrier); a delivery-day checklist; and same-day replies from Mason on business days
- Capacity: Four Private Client searches at a time. A buyer who pays when all four are taken is told within one business day and may wait for the next opening or take a full refund
- Current plan: https://www.steermeright.com/#private-client

## All plans

- One dedicated advisor from start to finish
- Full refund if you cancel before negotiations begin.
- Dealers and lenders pay us nothing.

## Costs not included

Service fees do not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs. Inventory, seller participation, timing, financing, and purchase outcomes vary. The buyer reviews the vehicle, deal terms, and final paperwork and makes every purchase decision.

For the authoritative current checkout scope and terms, see https://www.steermeright.com/#plans and https://www.steermeright.com/how-it-works.html.
