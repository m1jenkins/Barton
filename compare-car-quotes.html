Your comparison

Incentives with unconfirmed eligibility are shown only as scenarios. If an unconfirmed incentive is already in the selling price, the first scenario adds it back. Both scenarios hold other entered charges fixed. Request a revised itemized quote when terms change, and confirm that incentives can be combined and remain valid through purchase.
