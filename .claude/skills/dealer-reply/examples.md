# Mason's dealer emails

Real replies Mason typed to dealers in November 2026, with names, cars, colors and numbers changed. Each starts with what the dealer had just said, then Mason's reply, then what happened. His line breaks, "--" and loose punctuation are his; keep the style, not the typos.

## Asking for the numbers

Dealer: "Can do $31.5k selling price, what day can you stop by?" (Itemized OTD came back in 13 minutes.)

```text
Can you send me an invoice/breakdown? I'd like to do as much online as possible so all I need to do is pick up the keys when I show up. Happy to do financing online/preapproval. But I need an invoice with OTD numbers first.
```

Dealer: heated seats aren't a standalone option; offers an in-stock car without them. (Itemized OTD in 6 minutes.)

```text
NBD -- I'll just skip the heated seats.

Could you send me over an invoice that reflects the ~$31k purchase price + whatever misc doc/dealer fees? I am leaning towards just doing that.
```

Dealer gives a rough fee total and asks what time he's coming. (Fee breakdown in 3 minutes.)

```text
Early afternoon. I'll get you an exact time soon.

What's the breakdown on those fees?
```

## Holding the email line

Dealer: "Sounds reasonable, when can you come in and put down a deposit?"

```text
I want to do basically 90% of this over email/e-docs before coming to the dealership. I'll do the financing approval process online and can send ID/docs. But I need a final invoice before committing to a drive/day at a dealer.
```

Same rep, second time: the first step is to come in and sit down. (The rep switched to email.)

```text
Hi Dan,

I am not interested in coming into the dealership without a concrete invoice and financing already secured.

If you are unable to handle these details via email first, I completely understand, but please let me know so I can move on to another dealership.

Best,
Mason
```

Dealer sends the OTD and asks him in today for a test drive.

```text
Already know I want the car. I've driven one before. Don't really need to come in for a test drive. How much of this can we do online? I really don't have that much interest in driving down to a dealership without financing secured and a signed agreement at a specific price.

Would love for this to be a very quick and painless process for the two of us.
```

## Countering

Out-of-state used car; dealer says "We can do 48 + TTL" and asks about transport. (Dealer came back at $47,500 + TTL, his original ask, in 3 minutes.)

```text
Undecided on transport at the moment. Not opposed to flying up and driving it back. Would you be willing to do 48 + transport to Dallas?
```

Rep: "If I can get my manager to do $500 off, can I get us a deal?"

```text
$1k off would be an easy decision. See if you can make it work -- will make this an easy sale and could have a deposit on it quickly.
```

Rep pitches a one-price guarantee on a car that's sat for months. (The rep offered $500 off.)

```text
Hi Kyle,

If you want to make a sale, let me know. I'm not interested in paying that for a car that's been on the lot for three months.

Mason
```

## Match or beat

Rep: "I have the vehicle on the lot, when do you plan on visiting?" (The dealer offered to match with a written quote.)

```text
I've got a few dealers willing to do 31.5 and 31,214 on XLEs in Texas. Both just have the port installed options but otherwise base cars. Are those deals you guys can beat? I am dropping my requirement for heated seats but it has to be Ocean Blue.
```

CRM: "I tried to call you, one call replaces 4-5 emails, what's your number?" (Screenshot of the rival quote attached.)

```text
Hi Sam,

I prefer to handle the purchase process over email so I can review documents at my own pace.

Another dealer came in with a fairly aggressive offer on the same car.

Is this something you guys could come under?

Thank you,

Mason
```

Dealer beats the price, but the spec sheet shows another color.

```text
Really appreciate you guys coming in under an already great deal -- but the spec sheet I got shows grey. Ocean Blue is a non negotiable for me.
```

## Add-ons and fees

Dealer itemizes an $899 doc fee and a $499 dealer protection package. (The package line came off.)

```text
We are not local. I looked on the website and there's nothing in the protection package that provides any value to us.

Willing to overlook the $900 doc fee.
```

## Locking it in

Dealer: the OTD is agreed and won't change, but financing has to be done in person and they can't hold the car.

```text
Okay -- that's fair. Any chance you could send me a purchase agreement just so I have something in writing before showing up? I know it's some extra work on your end but I'd enjoy the peace of mind.
```

## Closing out

Rep: his manager says to take the rival offer; they can't beat it.

```text
Totally fair -- I agree it's an aggressive offer. Thank you for your time and I appreciate the candid communication.
```

Dealer refuses the counter and says they don't work with brokers.

```text
No worries, all the best.
```
