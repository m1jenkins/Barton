Write the comparison for a blog post: 2026 Toyota RAV4 XLE Premium AWD vs 2027 Honda CR-V Sport Hybrid AWD. I drove both back to back on Saturday, about 40 miles each, same loop. My notes:

RAV4: $39,095 as tested. 236 hp hybrid. EPA 42 mpg combined, I saw 43.1 on the loop. Felt quick off the line. Seat was firm, my back noticed after an hour. Loud on the highway, tire roar mostly. Tons of little shelves and bins, deep one in the door handle. New 10.5 inch screen is fast, nav needs a subscription after the trial. Cargo 37.8 cu ft. Easy to see out of, low hood. Dashcam is standard which is cool.

CR-V: $38,950 as tested. 204 hp hybrid. EPA 37 combined, I saw 37.8. Slower but smoother, quieter at 70. Front seats are great, could sit all day. Rear legroom 41.0 in vs 37.8 in the Toyota, my 6'2" brother in law fit behind me fine. Cargo 39.3 cu ft. 9 inch screen looks dated, wireless CarPlay worked first try. Fewer cubbies. Rear doors open really wide, car seat went in easy.

Both: 3yr/36k basic, 5yr/60k powertrain. Around 600-800 words. Pick a winner.
