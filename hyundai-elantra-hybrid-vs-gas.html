Dealers and lenders pay us nothing.

Yes, even after Hyundai’s gas-car cash. An Elantra Hybrid Blue costs $1,700 more than a gas SEL Sport and is rated 54 mpg combined against 34, which is 163 fewer gallons a year at 15,000 miles. At the national average of $3.805 a gallon that is about $622 a year, and the premium is back in 2.7 years (3.4 years at 12,000 miles). Hyundai pays $2,000 on the gas car this month and $500 on the hybrid, which widens the real gap to about $3,200 and the payback to 5.1 years.

At 54 mpg the Blue also out-rates the Corolla Hybrid’s 50, and it starts at $25,650. The cost is power: the hybrid makes 139 hp against the gas car’s 147, and Edmunds timed a gas Elantra at 9.4 seconds to 60 mph (we found no hybrid time). Edmunds still scores the hybrid higher, 7.6 out of 10 against 6.8 for the gas car. Hyundai’s financing runs 3.9% on the hybrid against 3.49% on the gas car, a difference of about $280 in interest on a $25,000 loan (our arithmetic).

Buy the hybrid if…

  • You drive 12,000 miles a year or more. The Blue’s $1,700 premium is back in 3.4 years at 12,000 miles and 2.7 at 15,000, before Hyundai’s cash.
  • You want 54 mpg combined, against 34 for the gas SEL Sport and 50 for a Corolla Hybrid.
  • You do a lot of highway miles. The Blue is rated 58 mpg on the highway against 40 for the gas car.
  • You want a sunroof and leatherette seats with the hybrid. The Hybrid SEL Sport Plus has them for $2,850 over the gas SEL Sport Plus.

Buy the gas version if…

  • You want the cheapest Elantra. A gas SE starts at $22,825, $2,825 under the Hybrid Blue.
  • You’ll finance with Hyundai. The gas car gets $2,000 bonus cash and 3.49%; the hybrid gets $500 and 3.9%, so the hybrid’s payback roughly doubles.
  • You want the most power. The gas Elantra makes 147 hp against 139 for the hybrid, and the N Line turbo makes 201.
  • You drive fewer than about 6,000 miles a year. At that pace the Blue takes more than 6 years to earn back $1,700, and nearly 13 with Hyundai’s cash.

What the hybrid costs on like-for-like trims

Hyundai sells the hybrid on its own trims, so no pair matches exactly. The Hybrid Blue (proximity key, wireless Apple CarPlay, LED projector headlights) is the entry hybrid, and the SEL Sport (gloss-black 17-inch wheels, spoiler, leather-wrapped wheel) is the gas car it is closest to in price. The Hybrid SEL Sport Plus and the gas SEL Sport Plus both have a power sunroof and leatherette seats, so that pair is the closest on equipment.

EPA fuel economy, gas against hybrid

EPA rates the Hybrid Blue at 54 mpg combined (51 city, 58 highway) and the gas SEL Sport at 34 (30 city, 40 highway). The Hybrid SEL Sport Plus is rated 50 (49 city, 52 highway), so its sunroof and bigger wheels cost 4 mpg. The Blue leads by 21 mpg in the city and 18 on the highway, an unusually even gain, so a long commute pays about as well as city driving.

How many years until the hybrid pays for itself?

The Blue’s $1,700 premium is back in 2.6 to 3.4 years and the Hybrid SEL Sport Plus’s $2,850 in 5.0 to 6.6, depending on mileage and gas price. The Blue’s payback is short because the gas car is a 34-mpg compact and the hybrid is a 54-mpg one.

Do this month’s incentives favor one version?

This is the one pair where Hyundai’s offers favor the gas car, and the page’s verdict allows for it. Hyundai gives $2,000 bonus cash and 3.49% financing for up to 60 months on a gas Elantra, and $500 and 3.9% on the Hybrid, the same in all four regions we checked. Hyundai’s Dallas fine print says the gas car’s $2,000 requires Hyundai Motor Finance; the other three regions don’t say. On the hybrid, Hyundai’s fine print names the Blue in Chicago and New York and the SEL Sport in Dallas and Los Angeles, so ask which trims qualify for the $500. Leases are $239 a month for an SEL Sport and $269 for a Hybrid Blue, both for 36 months with $3,599 due. Everything ends November 30, 2026. Offers are from the Elantra price guide, read at Hyundai’s offers page on November 18, 2026.

Resale: does the hybrid hold its value?

iSeeCars counts three-year depreciation for the Elantra at 16.6%, against 12.1% for compact cars and 24.8% for the average vehicle. We found no separate figure for the Elantra Hybrid. The gas Elantra loses more than its class, so a hybrid that holds a little better would help its case, but we can’t say it does. The Elantra is also due for a redesign in 2027.

What owners on Reddit report about real-world mpg

Hybrid owners report about 50 mpg in everyday driving, below the Blue’s 54 but at the SEL Sport Plus’s 50 rating, and some mention a recall update they say cut highway mileage. We counted 68 opinions about the Elantra’s fuel economy in 191 Reddit threads about the car, posted from January 2025 to November 2026: 43 positive, 14 negative and 11 mixed. That is a net score of +49 on the same −100 to +100 scale as the Elantra price guide, and it covers every version of the car, not only the hybrid.

Common questions

How much more does the Elantra Hybrid cost than the gas Elantra?

$1,700 for a Hybrid Blue over an SEL Sport ($25,650 against $23,950), $2,850 for a Hybrid SEL Sport Plus over an SEL Sport Plus, and $2,825 for the Blue over the cheapest gas SE ($22,825). Prices are 2026 starting MSRPs without destination, from the Elantra price guide.

How much gas does the Elantra Hybrid save?

About 163 gallons a year at 15,000 miles for the Blue (278 against 441), which is $622 at the November 16 national average of $3.805 and $654 at EPA’s $4.00. At 12,000 miles it is about 131 gallons.

Does Hyundai’s gas-car cash change the answer?

It lengthens the payback but doesn’t reverse it. With $2,000 on the gas car and $500 on the hybrid, the Blue’s real premium is about $3,200 and it earns that back in 4.9 to 6.4 years. The offers end November 30, 2026.

Should I wait for the 2027 Elantra?

Only if you want the redesign. Kelley Blue Book reported the eighth-generation Elantra shown in South Korea in June 2026 and expected in U.S. showrooms in 2027, and Hyundai hasn’t announced U.S. prices. The 2026 is the last of this design and carries this month’s cash.
