What a car finder does

You give the finder a spec. Not just “a RAV4” but the trim, the colors you’d take, the packages you won’t go without, a mileage ceiling if it’s used. The finder comes back with the actual cars that match and where they are.

Plenty of finders stop there and hand you a list of links. We don’t. Finding the car is the first half of the job; the second half is getting each dealer that has it to put an out-the-door price in writing and then negotiating those prices against each other. A car you’ve found at a bad price isn’t much of a find.

Where we look

We search new, used and CPO dealer inventory for your model, including cars still in transit to the dealer. On the Steer Me Right plan ($395) that covers one model, with any trims, colors and packages within it, at dealers within 200 miles of you. Steer Me Right Pro ($695) covers dealers nationwide and up to three models. The wider reach matters most when the spec is rare where you live.

We do not search private sellers or auctions. Every car we bring you is at a dealer, with a dealer’s paperwork behind it.

Hard-to-find cars

A manual transmission, an unusual color, a trim the factory barely builds: these are the searches where a finder earns the fee, and also the ones that take longest. Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front.

If the only matching cars are more than 200 miles away, that’s a Steer Me Right Pro search. We get shipping quotes when the best deal is far away. You pay the carrier; shipping is not part of our fee. And sometimes the honest answer is that the car you described isn’t for sale anywhere this month. We’ll say so, and tell you what the nearest matches are.

How this differs from a listing site

Listing sites are free and good at showing you what’s advertised. They don’t tell you whether the car is still there, what the dealer adds to the advertised price, or what the same car costs at the store forty miles away once fees are in. Someone has to call and ask. You can do that yourself, and our free quote comparison worksheet will help you line up the answers. Or you can hand us the list of calls.

Common questions

What is a car finder service?

A service that locates a specific car for a buyer. Steer Me Right finds the car in dealer inventory and also negotiates the price and fees, for a flat $395 or $695.

Can you find a car in another state?

Yes. State lines don’t matter. The Steer Me Right plan ($395) covers dealers within 200 miles of you. Steer Me Right Pro ($695) covers dealers nationwide. On Steer Me Right Pro, we get shipping quotes when the best deal is far away. You pay the carrier; shipping is not part of our fee.

Do you find used cars?

Yes. Used and CPO cars from dealers are part of every plan. Private sellers and auctions are not. See our used car buying service page.

What if I’m not sure which car I want?

That is what Steer Me Right Pro is for: we price up to three models side by side and tell you which deal we’d take. Switching to a different model isn’t included on the Steer Me Right plan.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel before negotiations begin and you get a full refund. During negotiations, refund eligibility depends on the work already done. Read the refund policy.
