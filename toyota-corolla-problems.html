The best used Corollas are the 2022 to 2025 model years, which draw 48, 37, 32 and 18 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a Corolla yardstick of 48, and the 2016 to 2018 also rate Buy, at 59, 58 and 47. Each rate divides a model year’s complaints by the U.S. sales of the same calendar year. Model years and calendar years don’t line up, since a model year’s cars sell across two calendar years, so the rate compares years with each other. Sales come from Good Car Bad Car’s Corolla table, which our Corolla vs Elantra page also uses; its 2023 to 2025 total is one unit under Toyota’s year-end releases. Our verdicts use this car’s own yardstick, the median of 48 complaints per 100,000 across its 2019 to 2025 model years: Buy up to 1.25 times it, Check first up to twice it, Avoid above that. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

Corolla generations: what changed

Eleventh generation 2016–2019 sedan

The 2014 to 2019 Corolla sedan used a 1.8-liter four-cylinder (132 hp, and 140 hp on the LE Eco), with a CVT, a four-speed automatic on the base L and a six-speed manual on the L and S. A 2017 refresh brought a new front end, a 7-inch touchscreen and the SE and XSE trims (Wikipedia).

Twelfth generation 2019–2025

The hatchback arrived first, for 2019, and the sedan followed for 2020, with a 2.0-liter engine (169 hp) on SE and XSE, a 1.8-liter (139 hp) below them and a 121-hp 1.8-liter hybrid, the first Corolla hybrid sold here. A refresh for 2023 put the 2.0-liter and a CVT on every gas model, dropped the manual, added all-wheel drive to the hybrid and made Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 standard (Wikipedia).

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Corolla: 407 mentions in 246 threads, 340 positive and 45 negative, a net of +75 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Corolla is 67 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/corolla and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Toyota Corolla price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners report Corollas lasting 200,000 to 300,000 miles on routine maintenance, and say the hybrid and CVT drivetrains hold up when the fluid is changed on schedule. The complaints are older engines burning oil or losing a head gasket, CVT trouble on some newer cars and coolant bypass valve failures on twelfth-generation cars. NHTSA’s field doesn’t say whether the 2020’s engine complaints are about that valve.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report 200,000 to 300,000 miles with only routine maintenance
  • Hybrid and CVT drivetrains hold up when fluid is changed on schedule
  • Older generations are described as durable and cheap to keep running

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report older engines burning oil or suffering head gasket trouble
  • Some owners report CVT or transmission problems on newer cars
  • Owners report coolant bypass valve failures on 12th-generation cars

  • Noise and refinement. Owners report dash, blower and seat rattles on newer cars

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Air bag control unit 2011–2019Recall 20V-024. The air bag control unit may malfunction in certain crashes and keep the air bags or seat belt pretensioners from deploying. Dealers install a noise filter free.
  • In-tank fuel pump 2019–2020Recall 20V-012 and 20V-682. The low-pressure pump can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump assembly with an improved one, free.
  • Torque converter impeller 2019 hatchbackRecall 18V-901. The impeller blades can detach under high load and stall a CVT car. Dealers replace the CVT and torque converter free.
  • Passenger occupant sensor 2020–2021Recall 23V-865. A short circuit in the sensor can keep the front passenger air bag from deploying. Dealers inspect and replace it free.
  • Rear seat belt lock 2020Recall 19V-877. The rear seat belt webbing sensor may not lock as intended in a crash with several impacts. Dealers replace the assemblies as needed, free.
  • Steering column cable 2023Recall 23V-480 and 25V-040. A weak weld inside the spiral cable can separate and deactivate the driver’s air bag; 25V-040 covers cars repaired incorrectly under 23V-480. Dealers replace the cable free.
  • Steering intermediate shaft 2023–2024Recall 24V-878. Cracks from manufacturing can make the shaft fracture and detach from the steering column. Dealers replace it free.
  • Brake assist software 2023–2025 HybridRecall 24V-708. A software error can cut power brake assist when turning a corner. Dealers update the brake computer free.
  • Headlight aim markings 2026Recall 26V-110. The front headlights may lack aim markings, which can leave them aimed wrongly after service. Dealers replace both assemblies free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

NHTSA has no open investigation on the 2016 to 2026 Corolla as of November 21, 2026. The only item on its list that names the Corolla, EQ21001, is closed and covers replacement air bag modules in 2003 to 2008 cars.

What to check before you buy a used Corolla

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Toyota dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Stalling and fuel pump fixes on a 2019 or 2020.Confirm recalls 20V-012 and 20V-682 are closed, and ask whether Toyota’s stalling campaign 22TC07 was done on a 2020. It is not a recall, so the VIN lookup won’t show it.
  • CVT recall on a 2019 hatchback.Confirm 18V-901 is closed; the repair replaces the CVT and torque converter.
  • Air bag recalls on a 2016 to 2021.Confirm 20V-024 on a 2016 to 2019 and 23V-865 on a 2020 or 2021. Both are free at a Toyota dealer.
  • Steering recalls on a 2023 or 2024.Ask for proof that 24V-878 (steering shaft) is done and, on a 2023, that 23V-480 and 25V-040 are closed.
  • Coolant and oil on a 2020.Reddit owners report coolant bypass valve failures on twelfth-generation cars and older engines burning oil. Check the coolant level, look for leaks and read the oil level.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine and transmission before you sign, especially on a 2020.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Corolla has 0 complaints on NHTSA’s list and 1 recall so far, for headlight aim markings, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024 or 2025, the used cars carry steering shaft recall 24V-878 (2024) and Hybrid brake software recall 24V-708.

Common questions

What is the worst Toyota Corolla year?

The 2020, at 135 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold on 320 complaints, 112 of them naming the engine. It needed a software fix for stalling and a fuel pump recall.

What is the best used Corolla year?

The 2022 to 2025 model years, at 48, 37, 32 and 18 complaints per 100,000. The 2016 to 2018 also rate Buy, at 59, 58 and 47.

Is the Toyota Corolla reliable?

Reddit owners report 200,000 to 300,000 miles on routine maintenance. NHTSA’s counts rate seven of the ten model years from 2016 to 2025 Buy, against a Corolla yardstick of 48 complaints per 100,000.

Did Toyota recall the 2019 and 2020 Corolla for the fuel pump?

Yes. Recalls 20V-012 and 20V-682 cover an in-tank fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, and the repair is free at a Toyota dealer. A VIN lookup shows whether it is still open.

How do I check a used Corolla for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Toyota dealer.
