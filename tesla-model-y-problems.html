The best used Model Ys are the 2024 and 2025 model years, which draw 109 and 47 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a Model Y yardstick of 352.5. Skip the 2021: it draws 685 on 1,107 complaints, 584 of them about automatic emergency braking, and two open NHTSA evaluations name it, PE25010 on electronic door handles and PE26006 on front lateral links. The 2020, 2022 and 2023 are the years to ask about. PE26006 covers the 2021 to 2023, the 2023 drew 1,205 complaints, the most of any year, and the 2020’s rate of 713 rests on a partial first year of sales. No 2016 to 2019 Model Y was sold.

Tesla publishes no model-level U.S. sales, so the rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across every Model Y listing, by Good Car Bad Car’s estimates, the method our Tesla Model Y vs Toyota RAV4 page uses. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

Model Y generations: what changed

First generation 2020–2024

Tesla began building the Model Y in Fremont in January 2020 and added Austin in late 2021. A seven-seat option arrived in January 2021 and ran until the 2025 refresh. A Standard Range AWD built in Austin with 4680 cells and a structural battery pack went on sale to the public around April 2023 and left in September, and a Standard Range RWD returned in October 2023 before a Long Range RWD replaced it in May 2024 (Wikipedia).

Refreshed Model Y 2025–

The refresh reached the U.S. in March 2025. In October 2025 Tesla renamed the Long Range trims Premium and added a rear-wheel-drive Model Y Standard, and a six-seat Model Y L launched on July 2, 2026 (Wikipedia).

Common Model Y problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 3,944 Model Y complaints for the 2020 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Automatic emergency braking, 2020 to 2023 2020–2023 model years1,673 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 206 per 100,000 sold. The biggest part count on every year. NHTSA’s evaluation PE22002 looked at unexpected braking with adaptive cruise control on 2021 and 2022 Model Ys and Model 3s and closed it in June 2026, saying reports had faded and no collisions were identified. Reddit owners report Full Self-Driving braking abruptly for no obvious reason.
  • Suspension, 2020 to 2023 2020–2023 model years299 complaints naming suspension, 37 per 100,000 sold. Recalls cover upper control arm bolts (20V-709, 2020), lateral link fasteners (21V-835, 2020 and 2021, and 22V-895, 2023) and fracturing knuckles (21V-912, 2020 to 2022). NHTSA’s open evaluation PE26006 says the lateral link failures it is looking at exceed the recalls.
  • Steering, 2023 2023 model years222 complaints naming steering, 58 per 100,000 sold. Recall 25V-092 covers a power steering circuit board that can overstress and cut assist on 2023 Model Ys and Model 3s, 23V-385 a loose steering wheel fastener on 2022 and 2023 cars. NHTSA closed analysis EA24001 in June 2026 and evaluation PE23003, on two cars delivered without the wheel’s retaining bolt, in April 2026.
  • Brakes, 2021 to 2023 2021–2023 model years471 complaints naming brakes, 61 per 100,000 sold. Recall 21V-387 covers brake caliper bolts that may be loose on 2020 and 2021 Model Ys, which can let a caliper separate and touch the wheel rim. Tesla tightens or replaces the bolts free; the 2022 and 2023 counts have no recall of their own.
  • Backup camera, 2023 to 2025 2023–2025 model years120 complaints naming backup camera, 13 per 100,000 sold. Recall 25V-002 covers a computer circuit board that can short and lose the rearview image on 2023 to 2025 cars, and 26V-283 a software delay on 2020 to 2023 cars; 24V-035 covers a 2023 software fault. Tesla fixes the software over the air and replaces boards that failed.
  • Door latches, 2021 2021 model years14 complaints naming latches and locks, 8.7 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s evaluation PE25010 covers 2021 Model Ys whose outside door handles stop working when the 12-volt battery runs low. A small count of complaints, but NHTSA notes that Tesla cars have manual releases inside the cabin which a child may not be able to use.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Model Y: 304 mentions in 223 threads, 105 positive and 171 negative, a net of −21 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Model Y is 56 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/TeslaModelY and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Tesla Model Y price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners report drivetrains and batteries holding up past 150,000 miles and battery health staying high after 60,000, and they describe the refreshed Model Y as better built than the early cars. What they report going wrong is battery health tests of 80 to 85 percent after a few years, front control arm bushings that wear and knock, and a grinding steering column on newer refreshed cars, a different group from the 2023’s steering complaints, which recall 25V-092 covers.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Drivetrain and battery hold up past 150,000 miles for many owners
  • Battery health stays high after 60,000 miles for several owners
  • Refreshed cars are described as better built than early ones

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report battery health tests of 80 to 85 percent after a few years
  • Front control arm bushings wear, creak or knock and get replaced
  • Owners report a grinding steering column on newer refreshed cars

Other complaints that come up

  • Price and value. Depreciation is steep, and owners report losing a large share of value
  • Dealers and markups. Service refuses warranty work unless the owner can reproduce the problem

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Front lateral link fasteners 2020–2021Recall 21V-835. The fasteners may loosen and let the lateral link separate from the subframe. Tesla tightens or replaces them free; recall 22V-895 covers the same part on 2023 cars.
  • Suspension knuckles 2020–2022Recall 21V-912. The front and rear knuckles may fracture and let the suspension links separate. Tesla inspects and replaces the knuckles as needed, free.
  • Upper control arm bolts 2020Recall 20V-709. The bolts joining the upper control arm and steering knuckle may be loose and let the arm detach. Tesla tightens them free.
  • Steering wheel fastener 2022–2023Recall 23V-385. The steering wheel fastener may be loose. Tesla repairs it free.
  • Power steering assist 2023Recall 25V-092. A power steering circuit board can overstress and cause a loss of assist. Tesla’s fix is an over-the-air software update.
  • Seat recliner weld 2024Recall 24V-886. The weld holding the front seat recliner may fail. Tesla replaces the seat assembly free.
  • Headlight low beams 2020–2023Recall 26V-507. The low beams may be too bright and exceed the maximum light output. Tesla will replace the headlights free, and our Model Y price guide says the repair was not yet available.
  • Battery pack contactors 2026Recall 25V-690. The contactors may fail and cause a loss of drive power on some 2026 Model Ys and 2025 Model 3s. Tesla replaces them free.
  • Front lateral link fasteners 2026Recall 26V-558. One or more fasteners may not have been tightened properly, which can let the link loosen or separate. Tesla tightens or replaces them and checks the alignment, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Front lower lateral link detachment 2021–2023PE26006, open. NHTSA opened the evaluation on July 29, 2026 after 156 complaints of front lower lateral link failures on 2021 to 2023 Model Ys and 2018 to 2020 Model 3s. In most cases the owner had no warning, and the car may not be drivable afterward. NHTSA says the failures exceed the scope of the earlier lateral link recalls.
  • Electronic door handles inoperative 2021PE25010, open. NHTSA opened the evaluation on September 15, 2025 after nine complaints that 2021 Model Y doors would not open, four of them from owners who broke a window to get in, mostly parents with a child inside. NHTSA’s early reading is that the electronic locks get too little voltage, and the owner’s manual describes a jump-start method to power them from outside.
  • Unexpected brake activation 2021–2022PE22002, closed. NHTSA opened the evaluation on February 16, 2022 and closed it on June 29, 2026, citing a low demonstrated hazard, no identified collisions, Tesla’s software updates and reports that fell to 45 in 2024, 19 in 2025 and 3 so far in 2026, without finding that no defect exists.
  • Loss of steering control 2023EA24001, closed. NHTSA upgraded its evaluation of lost power steering assist on 2023 Model Ys and Model 3s on February 2, 2024, and closed it on June 25, 2026 after Tesla’s recall 25V-092.
  • Steering wheel detachment 2023PE23003, closed. NHTSA opened the evaluation on March 4, 2023 after two 2023 Model Ys left the factory without the steering wheel’s retaining bolt, and closed it on April 24, 2026.

What to check before you buy a used Model Y

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Tesla dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The front suspension on a 2020 to 2023.Confirm 20V-709 (2020), 21V-835, 21V-912 and 22V-895 (2023) are closed, then have an independent shop check the lateral links and control arms. Open evaluation PE26006 covers failures beyond those recalls, usually with no warning.
  • Door handles on a 2021.Ask when the 12-volt battery was last replaced, and learn where the manual door releases are before you drive away.
  • Headlights on a 2020 to 2023.Recall 26V-507 covers low beams that are too bright and, per our price guide, had no repair yet. Ask the seller to say whether the notice has arrived.
  • Power steering on a 2023.Confirm 25V-092 (steering assist) and 23V-385 (steering wheel fastener) are closed.
  • Strut and contactor recalls on a 2026.Ask the dealer to show lateral link recall 26V-558 and, on a car that qualifies, contactor recall 25V-690 are done before you take delivery.
  • Battery health and warranty.Reddit owners report battery tests of 80 to 85 percent after a few years. Our price guide puts the battery and drive unit cover at 8 years or 120,000 miles on a Long Range or Performance, from first delivery, so ask for that date.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look over the suspension, brakes and bushings before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Model Y has 0 complaints on NHTSA’s list and 8 recalls so far, including front lateral link and battery contactor recalls, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024 or 2025, the 2024 has 5 recalls and the 2025 3 to confirm, and neither is covered by evaluation PE26006.

Common questions

What is the worst Tesla Model Y year?

The 2021, at 685 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold on 1,107 complaints, with two open NHTSA evaluations naming it. The 2023 has the most complaints of any year, 1,205.

What is the best used Model Y year?

The 2024 and 2025, at 109 and 47 complaints per 100,000 sold. These rates rest on Good Car Bad Car’s estimates, since Tesla publishes no model-level U.S. sales.

Is there a 2019 Tesla Model Y?

Not for sale. The Model Y reached U.S. buyers in 2020. NHTSA lists three recalls whose model year ranges start in 2019 and no complaints for that year.

What is wrong with the Model Y front suspension?

NHTSA’s open evaluation PE26006 has 156 complaints of front lower lateral links detaching on 2021 to 2023 Model Ys and 2018 to 2020 Model 3s, usually with no warning. It goes beyond recalls 21V-835 and 22V-895, so a clean VIN lookup doesn’t rule it out.

How do I check a used Model Y for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Tesla service center.
