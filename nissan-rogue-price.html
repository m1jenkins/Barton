The 2027 Rogue Hybrid is the Rogue we’d buy. Its e-POWER system uses the 1.5-liter turbo engine only as a generator, and two electric motors drive all four wheels. It starts at $35,490 ($37,065 with destination), and Nissan estimates 38 mpg combined, short of the RAV4 Hybrid’s 41. Edmunds scores it 8.2 out of 10 against 6.4 for the gas Rogue, but only the $41,490 SR Technology is at dealers until January 2027. Reddit is warier than the reviewers, mostly over Nissan’s recent engine and CVT record.

What we like

  • 38 mpg combined (Nissan’s estimate) and standard all-wheel drive on the Hybrid
  • Edmunds timed a Hybrid SR at 6.9 seconds to 60 mph, a second or more quicker than most small SUVs
  • Hybrid drivetrain and battery covered for 8 years/100,000 miles
  • Gas Rogue starts at $29,690 with $3,000 of Nissan cash

What we’d think twice about

  • Only the $41,490 Hybrid SR Technology is at dealers until January 2027
  • Hybrid SV costs $3,400 more than an AWD gas SV
  • No EPA label, NHTSA or IIHS rating for the Hybrid yet
  • Gas Rogue ranks 15th of 20 small SUVs at Edmunds and its engine has had bearing recalls

What people praise

  • Reliability. Owners report high mileage without major trouble when the CVT fluid is changed on schedule

  • Interior and tech. Interior and equipment feel upscale, even on lower trims

What owners and shoppers complain about

  • Reliability. Owners report the 1.5-liter turbo three-cylinder losing coolant or failing outright

  • Dealers and markups. Owners report slow warranty repairs, no loaner cars and dealers denying follow-on claims
  • Fuel economy. Owners report real-world economy in the low to mid 20s, mostly in city driving

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Which Hybrid you’re quoted. Only the SR Technology ($43,065 with destination) is on lots now. Get the trim, price and delivery month in writing before a deposit.
  • The 38 mpg. It’s Nissan’s estimate until the EPA rates the car, and Edmunds lists 37 for the SV and Platinum. Check the window sticker.
  • Cash or the rate. On a gas Rogue, Nissan’s cash can’t be combined with its special rate or lease, so price both.
  • Accessories. Hybrids arrive with port-installed extras such as $470 floor mats and a $795 lighting package. You can refuse or negotiate them.

Common questions

How much is the Nissan Rogue Hybrid?

From $35,490 for the SV to $43,490 for the Platinum, all with AWD, plus $1,575 destination. Edmunds says buyers are getting about $1,200 off.

How many mpg does the Rogue Hybrid get?

Nissan estimates 38 mpg combined (41 city/36 highway) for the SR, pending EPA confirmation. Edmunds lists 37 for the SV and Platinum, and its test SR returned 42.2 mpg. The gas Rogue is rated 30 with front drive and 29 with AWD.

When does the Rogue Hybrid go on sale?

Now, as the $41,490 SR with the Technology Package. The SV, SR and Platinum are due in January 2027.

What is e-POWER, and do I plug it in?

No plug. The 1.5-liter turbo engine only charges the battery, and two electric motors drive the wheels, so there’s no transmission. Nissan covers the hybrid system and battery for 8 years/100,000 miles.

What does Reddit think of the Rogue Hybrid?

In the 41 threads about the new Hybrid and the plug-in, people like the power, the quiet and the electric-car feel, but call a first-year hybrid unproven and say it costs more than a RAV4 Hybrid. Across all Rogues, most complaints are about CVT and 1.5-liter turbo failures on older cars.

Is the Rogue Hybrid worth it over the gas Rogue?

For most buyers, yes. A Hybrid SV costs $3,400 more than an AWD gas SV and brings 225 hp against 201, 37 to 38 mpg against 29, and an 8.2 Edmunds score against 6.4. If payment comes first, the gas Rogue starts at $29,690 with $3,000 of Nissan cash.

How much under MSRP can you get a Nissan Rogue?

On a Hybrid, 2–3% under is a fair deal and 4% or more a strong one. On a gas Rogue, 4–5% is fair and 6% or more strong, before Nissan’s cash. Our estimate from Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book and CarWhere.
