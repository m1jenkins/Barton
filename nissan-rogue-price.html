The bottom line

New, 2027 Hybrid

The 2027 Rogue Hybrid is the Rogue we’d buy. It starts at $35,490, and Nissan estimates 38 mpg combined. Edmunds scores it 8.2 out of 10 against 6.4 for the gas Rogue, but only the $41,490 SR Technology is at dealers until January 2027. New 2027 Hybrid

Best when: You want the quiet, quick one and can take the SR Technology now or wait for the SV, SR and Platinum in January.

Check: The exact trim, price and delivery month in writing, and that Nissan’s 4.9% covers the trim you’re quoted.

New gas Rogue (2026 or 2027)

Best when: Payment comes first. The 2026 has 0% for 60 months and $4,500 on a Platinum; the 2027 has $3,000 cash or 2.9% in some regions.

Check: The model year, whether the cash or the rate applies (Nissan’s cash can’t be combined with its special rate or lease), and engine recalls 25V437 and 26V080.

Nissan Certified

Best when: You want a 2021 to 2026 gas Rogue with a warranty; Nissan offers 4.99% for up to 36 months on a 2021 or newer Certified Rogue.

Check: Certified means up to 6 years old with under 80,000 miles, a 167-point inspection and a 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain limited warranty, for gas-powered models (Nissan doesn’t say whether a hybrid qualifies).

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $20,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, proof that the engine software updates were done, and an independent inspection.

On a used gas Rogue, lean to a 2025 or 2026. The 2023 drew 784 NHTSA complaints, more than 400 about the engine, and 2021 to 2025 Rogues with the 1.5-liter VC-Turbo are on engine-bearing recalls. Nissan says the Hybrid’s 1.5-liter is a different engine.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Which Hybrid you’re quoted. Only the SR Technology ($43,065 with destination) is on lots now. Get the trim, price and delivery month in writing before a deposit.
  • The 38 mpg. It’s Nissan’s estimate until the EPA rates the car, and Edmunds lists 37 for the SV and Platinum. Check the window sticker.
  • Cash or the rate. On a gas Rogue, Nissan’s cash can’t be combined with its special rate or lease, so price both.
  • Accessories. Hybrids arrive with port-installed extras such as $470 floor mats and a $795 lighting package. You can refuse or negotiate them.

How much is the Nissan Rogue Hybrid?

From $35,490 for the SV to $43,490 for the Platinum, all with AWD, plus $1,575 destination. Edmunds says buyers are getting about $1,200 off.

How many mpg does the Rogue Hybrid get?

Nissan estimates 38 mpg combined (41 city/36 highway) for the SR, pending EPA confirmation. Edmunds lists 37 for the SV and Platinum, and its test SR returned 42.2 mpg. The gas Rogue is rated 30 with front drive and 29 with AWD.

When does the Rogue Hybrid go on sale?

Now, as the $41,490 SR with the Technology Package. The SV, SR and Platinum are due in January 2027.

What is e-POWER, and do I plug it in?

No plug. The 1.5-liter turbo engine only charges the battery, and two electric motors drive the wheels, so there’s no transmission. Nissan covers the hybrid system and battery for 8 years/100,000 miles.

What does Reddit think of the Rogue Hybrid?

In the 41 threads about the new Hybrid and the plug-in, people like the power and the quiet, but call a first-year hybrid unproven and pricier than a RAV4 Hybrid. Across all Rogues, most complaints are about CVT and 1.5-liter turbo failures on older cars.

Is the Rogue Hybrid worth it over the gas Rogue?

For most buyers, yes. A Hybrid SV costs $3,400 more than an AWD gas SV and brings 225 hp against 201, 37 to 38 mpg against 29, and an 8.2 Edmunds score against 6.4. The gas Rogue starts at $29,690 with $3,000 of Nissan cash.

How much under MSRP can you get a Nissan Rogue?

On a Hybrid, 2–3% under is a fair deal and 4% or more a strong one. On a gas Rogue, 4–5% is fair and 6% or more strong, before Nissan’s cash. Our estimate from Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book and CarWhere.
