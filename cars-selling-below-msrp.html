Pickups leave the most room this month. A fair deal on the Ram 1500 Laramie is 7–9% under sticker, about $4,300–$5,550 on a $61,480 truck, and Chevrolet Colorado, Ford F-150, Chevrolet Silverado 1500, GMC Sierra 1500, Toyota Tundra, Toyota Tacoma and Subaru Forester all start at 5% under or better. The Toyota RAV4, Honda Civic and Nissan Rogue leave the least: 1–3% under at a fair price, and the RAV4’s dearest trims sell at sticker.

Below, all 22 models we cover sit at the trim we’d buy, each with the dollars off and a line on why the number is what it is.

Common questions

Which new cars have the biggest discounts right now?

Ram 1500, Chevrolet Colorado and Ford F-150 lead the 22 models we cover. A fair deal on each starts at 5% under sticker or better, because the makers carry more trucks than the industry average.

Is a car selling below MSRP the price I will pay?

No. A dealer discount, manufacturer cash, destination, add-ons and fees are separate lines on the quote. Compare them on one written out-the-door price, as in buying a new car below MSRP.

Do manufacturer offers come on top of these discounts?

Mostly, though a maker’s low rate often replaces its cash, so you pick one. The deals page lists each offer with its end date and works out cash against 0%.

Can you negotiate a car for me?

Yes. Steer Me Right charges a flat $395 for one model or $695 for up to three. We find it in dealer stock, get written out-the-door offers and negotiate for a price below MSRP. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing, and you’re the only one we work for.
