A few things
you might be wondering.

Why should I use a car buying service in Houston instead of just visiting local dealerships?

Drive Right can compare available offers, review deal terms, and communicate with sellers on your behalf. Vehicle availability, pricing, delivery, and timing vary by transaction, so we document the options and tradeoffs before you decide.

Do I have to pick up the car at the dealership, or can it be delivered?

Delivery depends on the seller, vehicle, destination, and transaction. We can ask about delivery and advance paperwork, but availability and timing remain seller-dependent.

How do you handle my trade-in, and will I still get the Texas sales tax credit?

We can gather and compare trade-in quotes. Valuation and tax treatment depend on the transaction, so confirm the current treatment with the dealer, county tax office, or a qualified tax professional before deciding.

Can you help me find a specific vehicle, like a heavy-duty truck or a hybrid for long commutes?

We can search available inventory using your stated vehicle, trim, feature, budget, and location criteria. Inventory and seller participation change, so an exact match or delivery date is not guaranteed.

Do you help negotiate financing rates and extended warranties?

We can compare the financing and optional-product offers supplied for review and flag terms that need clarification. Approval, rate, coverage, availability, and lender terms are not guaranteed.
