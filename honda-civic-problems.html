The best used Civics are the 2020, 2021, 2024 and 2025 model years, which draw 94, 62, 60 and 45 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold. Skip the 2016 and the 2022. The 2016 draws 295 per 100,000, with steering named in 446 of its 1,084 complaints, and the 2022 drew 963 complaints, 806 of them about steering. Honda’s free recall 24V-744 covers the steering gearbox behind them, but we rate a year on its complaints whether or not the recall is done. The 2023 is the year to ask about: steering is another 263 of its complaints, and without proof of the recall we’d keep looking.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across every Civic listing (sedan, coupe, hatchback, Si, Type R and Hybrid), by U.S. sales. Honda’s 2022 sales of 133,932 look low next to the 2023’s 200,381, which inflates the 2022 rate; its complaint count is the highest of any year either way. Civic generations: what changed

Tenth generation 2016–2021

The sedan arrived for 2016, the coupe followed in March 2016 and ended after 2020, and the hatchback joined for 2017 with the 1.5-liter turbo only. The base engine is a 2.0-liter (158 hp) and the turbo makes 174 hp in the sedan and coupe; the Si and Type R came on the same body, and the Type R is a hatchback with a 2.0-liter turbo. A 2019 refresh made Honda Sensing standard (Wikipedia).

Eleventh generation 2022–

The redesigned Civic launched as a 2022 with a 2.0-liter engine on LX and Sport trims and a 1.5-liter turbo above them; the Si returned with a 200-hp turbo and a manual only. A facelift for 2025 added a hybrid sedan and hatchback (Wikipedia).

Common Civic problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 4,767 Civic complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Steering2,262
  • Electrical system460
  • Fuel and hybrid system442
  • Engine323
  • Brakes285
  • Automatic emergency braking252
  • Wipers and visibility172
  • Transmission and drivetrain169
  • Steering, older cars 2016–2019 model years961 complaints naming steering, 69 per 100,000 sold. Recall 18V-663 covers a torque sensor magnet that can come loose and apply steering assist the wrong way at full lock on 2017 and 2018 cars; dealers replace the gearbox. NHTSA’s complaint field doesn’t say what the 2016 owners describe.
  • Steering, 2022 on 2022–2025 model years1,190 complaints naming steering, 146 per 100,000 sold. Recall 24V-744 covers a steering gearbox with excess internal friction on 2022 to 2025 Civics, which can make the car hard to steer; dealers replace the worm gear spring and add grease, free. NHTSA’s analysis of “sticky steering,” EA23003, closed in January 2025, and recall 23V-704 covers racks replaced in service on 2022 to 2024 cars.
  • Fuel system 2018–2019 model years306 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 47 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 20V-314 and 21V-215 cover a low-pressure fuel pump inside the tank that can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump assembly, free; the VIN lookup shows whether it is open.
  • Engine 2016–2018 model years221 complaints naming engine, 21 per 100,000 sold. CarComplaints reports Honda extended warranties for fuel mixing into the 1.5-liter turbo’s oil in cold weather, with a software update; it isn’t a recall. Recall 16V-074 covers 2.0-liter piston pins on 2016 cars built from September 2015 to February 2016.
  • Automatic emergency braking 2022–2025 model years127 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 16 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s field names the driver-assist system, not the cause. Its separate investigation of unexpected automatic braking, EA24002, covers 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs and 2018 to 2022 Accords, and doesn’t name the Civic.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Civic: 317 mentions in 304 threads, 211 positive and 69 negative, a net of +45 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Civic is 62 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/civic and r/CivicSi and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Honda Civic price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit’s reliability complaints are the 1.5-liter turbo’s oil dilution in cold weather, head gasket leaks on turbo and hybrid engines, and air conditioning failures on the tenth generation. The 2022 steering spike barely shows up in the threads, so the complaint counts here carry that story, not Reddit.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report Civics running past 150,000 miles with only routine maintenance
  • Older Civic and Si engines are seen as proven and durable
  • Honda CVTs and the hybrid powertrain are seen as dependable with regular care

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report oil dilution on the 1.5-liter turbo, especially in cold weather
  • Owners report head gasket leaks on turbo and hybrid engines
  • Owners report air conditioning failures on the tenth-generation Civic

Other complaints that come up

  • Noise and refinement. Road and wind noise are loud on the highway, especially in the hatchback
  • Dealers and markups. Dealers add large markups and bundled packages on the Si, often called mandatory
  • Price and value. Used Si and Civic asking prices run high for their mileage

Our own reading of public Reddit comments from the Arctic Shift archive, January 2025 onward, collected November 18, 2026; the method is in the price guide. Complaints are what owners report, not confirmed defects.

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Steering gearbox friction 2022–2025Recall 24V-744. A steering gearbox built with excess friction can make the car hard to steer. Dealers replace the worm gear spring and redistribute or add grease, free. It also covers the Type R and the Hybrid.
  • Replaced steering racks 2022–2024Recall 23V-704. A rack installed as a service repair may have been assembled wrong, letting a tire rub the suspension. Dealers inspect and replace the rack as needed, free.
  • Steering sensor magnet 2017–2018Recall 18V-663. A magnet in the torque sensor can come loose and apply assist the wrong way at full lock. Dealers replace the gearbox, free.
  • Piston wrist pin clips 2016 2.0-literRecall 16V-074. Pistons may lack a wrist pin circlip, which can let the pin damage a cylinder and seize the engine. Dealers replace the pistons and damaged parts, free.
  • Halfshaft heat treatment 2017 sedan, coupeRecall 17V-706. A right halfshaft heat-treated improperly can break and leave the car unable to move. Dealers check the serial number and replace it as needed, free.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump 2018–2020Recall 20V-314 and 21V-215. The in-tank pump can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump assembly, free.
  • Brake master cylinder tie rod 2020–2021Recall 23V-458. A fastener between the brake booster and master cylinder may be assembled wrong and let the cylinder separate. Dealers inspect and repair the booster as needed, free.
  • High-pressure fuel pump 2025Recall 24V-763. The pump may crack and leak fuel on 2025 Civics and Civic Hybrids. Dealers inspect and replace it as needed, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Momentary increased steering effort 2022–2023EA23003, closed. NHTSA opened the analysis on November 29, 2023, covering 2022 and 2023 Civics, the 2023 CR-V and the Acura Integra, and closed it in January 2025. Honda’s recall 24V-744 followed in October 2024.

What to check before you buy a used Civic

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Honda dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Steering recall on any 2022 to 2025.Ask the dealer for proof that 24V-744 is done, and if the steering rack was ever replaced, that 23V-704 is too.
  • Fuel pump recalls.On a 2018 to 2020 hatchback or sedan, confirm 20V-314 and 21V-215; on a 2025, confirm 24V-763.
  • Oil on a 2016 to 2018 turbo.Owners and CarComplaints report fuel mixing into the oil in cold weather. Check the dipstick for a high level or a fuel smell, and ask which warranty extension still applies.
  • The 2016 and 2017 recalls.Confirm 16V-074 (2.0-liter pistons), 16V-725 (parking brake) and 17V-706 (halfshaft) are closed, and on a 2017 or 2018 that 18V-663 is too.
  • Air conditioning on a tenth-generation car.Reddit owners report A/C failures on 2016 to 2021 Civics. Run it on the hottest setting during the test drive and make sure the air turns cold.
  • An independent inspection.Owners also report head gasket leaks on turbo and hybrid engines. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check coolant level and the steering before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Civic has 34 NHTSA complaint and 1 recall so far, and the hybrid joined the line for 2025, so it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024 or 2025, steering recall 24V-744 applies to the used ones and not to a new car.

Common questions

What is the worst Honda Civic year?

The 2016 has the highest rate of the years we can fairly compare, 295 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold. The 2022 has the most complaints, 963, and 806 of them name steering, which Honda’s free recall 24V-744 covers.

What is the best used Civic year?

The 2020, 2021, 2024 and 2025 model years, at 94, 62, 60 and 45 complaints per 100,000. Ask for proof of recall 24V-744 on the 2024 and 2025.

Is the 2022 Honda Civic reliable?

It drew 963 NHTSA complaints, more than any other year, and 806 name steering. Honda recalled the steering gearbox (24V-744) and NHTSA closed its analysis, but we rate the year on its complaints, so we’d skip it. If you buy one anyway, get proof the recall is done.

Does the Honda Civic 1.5-liter turbo have oil dilution?

CarComplaints reports fuel mixing into the oil in cold weather on 2016 to 2018 turbo cars and says Honda extended warranties and updated software. It isn’t a recall, so a VIN lookup won’t show it.

How do I check a used Civic for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Honda dealer.
