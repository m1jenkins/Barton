# Steer Me Right

The language of Steer Me Right's paid car-buying service: who pays, what we search for, and how a deal with a dealer moves from first email to close.

## People

**Buyer**:
The person who paid for a full service or concierge plan and is buying the car.
_Avoid_: Client, customer, user

**Dealer**:
A dealership we ask for a written out-the-door price on a car.
_Avoid_: Seller, store

## The deal

**Deal**:
One buyer's search and negotiation, from the paid intake through to close.
_Avoid_: Case, job, order

**Stage**:
Where a deal stands: Confirming search, Shortlisted, Emailing dealers, Quotes in, Price agreed, or Closed. A deal is in Confirming search from the paid intake until its shortlist arrives.
_Avoid_: Status, phase

**Car**:
One specific vehicle on a deal's shortlist, sitting at one dealer.
_Avoid_: Listing (in buyer-facing copy), option

**Out-the-door price**:
A dealer's written total for a car, including fees, tax and registration.
_Avoid_: OTD (in public copy), sticker, price

**Asking price**:
The price a dealer advertises for a car, before tax and fees.
_Avoid_: Sticker, list price

**Estimated total**:
Our own estimate of a car's out-the-door price, shown until a dealer gives a written one.
_Avoid_: Quote, expected price

**Best price so far**:
The lowest written out-the-door price still standing on one car. A price the dealer has raised or withdrawn no longer counts.
_Avoid_: Best offer, top quote

**Buyer Update**:
A short message written for the buyer about their deal, and the only free text a buyer ever sees from us on the Deal Tracker. The buyer sees one only when Mason posts it, tells an agent to post it, or approves an agent's draft. Mason can take one back.
_Avoid_: Note (a note is internal to Mason and the agents), status update

**Buyer Question**:
A question a buyer asks about their deal, either on the Deal Tracker or by replying to one of its emails. A Buyer Update answers it. A reply from anyone but the buyer counts only once Mason releases it.
_Avoid_: Ticket, request, message (unqualified)

**Buyer Pick**:
The offer a buyer chooses on the Deal Tracker: one car at its best price so far. It records the price as picked, so a later rise shows. It's what they want; the price isn't agreed until Mason confirms it with the dealer. Mason can clear a pick made by mistake. Instead of picking, a buyer can ask us to keep looking.
_Avoid_: Selection, order, acceptance

**Buyer Document**:
A file a buyer hands us for their deal, such as a loan pre-approval, trade-in photos, a trade-in title, a payoff letter, proof of insurance or ID. Once handed over, the buyer can't open it on the Deal Tracker; they see only a receipt of its type and time.
_Avoid_: Attachment, upload (as a noun)

## Pages

**Deal Tracker**:
A buyer's private page and their main channel with us for the whole deal: where it stands, Buyer Updates, Buyer Questions, their pick and their documents. Dealers stay unnamed until a price is agreed, and then only the agreed dealer is named.
_Avoid_: Client dashboard, portal, buyer dashboard

**Ops dashboard**:
Mason's private page covering every deal and everything that needs him.
_Avoid_: Dashboard (unqualified), admin
