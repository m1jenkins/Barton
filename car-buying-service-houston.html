The Houston car market right now

The Census Bureau counted 317 new-car dealer locations across the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro area in 2023. S&P Global Mobility ranked the Houston market fourth in the country for new-vehicle registrations in the first half of 2025. Of the ten markets with the most registrations, it had the highest average monthly payment, $840. Its 63 days’ supply was third-lowest of the ten, after Detroit and Dallas–Fort Worth. S&P links the high payments in Houston and Dallas to their large numbers of full-size pickups.

Buying a car around Houston

Look at what the heat did to a used car

Houston summers run hot, and AAA Texas calls heat and vibration a car battery’s two worst enemies. Ask the dealer how old the battery is, and look for a crusty white or green buildup on its terminals. AAA also says to check the hoses and belts for cracking, so look at the rubber under the hood for splits and shiny, hardened spots. On a used search we can ask the dealer for photos of the battery label and the engine bay. Have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.

Test the air conditioning in slow traffic

AAA says a car’s air conditioning cools better while the car is moving, so a run at highway speed can hide a weak system. Ask the dealer for a test drive that includes time at a stoplight or in slow traffic, run the air conditioning on full the whole way, and see whether the cabin keeps cooling. If the air turns warm when you stop, ask the dealer to have the system checked in writing before you talk price.

Plan the drive to the dealer

A dealer on the far side of Houston can mean a long trip to pick up the car, and the route may run on a toll road. TxDOT says it collects tolls on its Houston-area toll roads electronically, including most of the Grand Parkway (SH 99). Tell us where you’d start and the longest drive you’d make, and we ask the dealers inside that window. Before pickup day, check your route for toll roads and how you’d pay.

Measure your garage before you pick a truck

Full-size pickups are a big part of the Houston market, and S&P Global Mobility links the area’s high monthly payments to them. A crew-cab pickup or a large SUV can be too long or too wide for an older garage or a tight apartment space. Tell us the longest and widest vehicle you can park and where it will live, and we check each model against those limits before we ask any dealer for a price.

How it works from Houston

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Houston?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Houston-area dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Can you tell if a used car has heat wear?

Only from photos. We can ask the dealer for pictures of the battery label, the battery terminals and the hoses and belts under the hood, and send them to you. Photos miss things, so have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.

Will you contact dealers across the whole Houston area?

Yes. Tell us where you’d start and the longest drive you’d make, and we ask dealers across that area. They are all Texas dealers, so both plans cover them. Check your route for toll roads before you go to pick up the car.

Can you check whether a big pickup will fit my garage?

Yes. Tell us the longest and widest vehicle you can park and where it will live, and we compare each model’s published size with those limits before we ask any dealer for a price. Measure the space yourself first, and ask the dealer to confirm the exact size of the truck you’re buying.
