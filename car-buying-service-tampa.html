Buying a car around Tampa

Look at what the sun did to a used car

AAA says the sun can fade, split or crack a dashboard and steering wheel, and fade paint, especially darker colors. On a used car, look for a cracked dash top and a faded roof and hood. AAA also says heat and air conditioning use can make a battery drain faster, so ask how old the battery is. On a used search we can ask the dealer for photos of those spots and of the battery label. Have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.

Run the air conditioning for the whole test drive

AAA says that if the inside of a car isn’t cool, the air conditioning’s refrigerant charge may be low or the system may not be working. Run the air conditioning at full blast through the whole drive, and watch how fast the cabin cools. If it stays warm, get a repair estimate before you talk price on the car. Tell us if you want an afternoon drive, and we ask the dealer for one.

Expect tolls on the way to a dealer across the bay

The Selmon Expressway and the Sunshine Skyway Bridge both charge tolls, and the Florida Department of Transportation has made the Skyway cashless. The Tampa Hillsborough Expressway Authority says SunPass users pay lower toll rates than cash or toll-by-plate users. Tell us which side of the bay you’d drive to, and we weigh the dealers by how far you’d go.

How it works from Tampa

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Tampa?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Tampa Bay dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Will you contact dealers on both sides of Tampa Bay?

Yes. Tell us how far you’d go to pick up the car and which side of the bay you’d rather drive to, and we ask dealers across that area, from Tampa and Brandon to St. Petersburg and Bradenton. They are all Florida dealers, so both plans cover them.

Will I pay tolls to pick up the car?

Possibly, if the route crosses the Selmon Expressway or the Sunshine Skyway Bridge. The Skyway takes no cash, and SunPass users pay lower tolls than toll-by-plate users. Tell us where you’d start, and we weigh dealers by how far you’d drive.

Can you tell if a used car has sun damage?

Only from photos. We can ask the dealer for pictures of the dashboard, the paint and the battery label, and send them to you. Photos miss things, so have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.
