The strongest offer this month is Hyundai’s 0% for 60 months on a gas or plug-in Tucson, worth about $6,100 less than the same loan at the average rate on a $36,000 crossover. The one that outlasts November is Kia’s 0% on a leftover 2026 Sportage, good through January 4. Truck buyers should run the arithmetic before taking cash: Chevrolet’s $6,000 beats its 0% only if you can borrow for less than 4.64%.

151 offers on 22 models are in the tables below, and 113 of them end November 30. Offers differ by ZIP, and every figure comes from the price guides, read November 15 to 21, 2026.

Common questions

Can I get 0% APR and cash back together?

Seldom. On the F-150, Silverado, Sierra, CX-5, Ram and Tundra the cash and the low rate are separate offers. A small bonus sometimes rides with the rate, such as Ford’s $500 with its 0%, but the headline cash does not.

Does every buyer get these rates?

No. The rates and leases need approval through the maker’s own lender, and most are for well-qualified buyers. The guides say which offers need which lender.

Is the dealer’s discount on top of the manufacturer’s offer?

The price guides treat them as separate lines, and the list of cars selling below MSRP covers the dealer side. Ask for both on one written quote so you can see the price after each.

Can you negotiate a car for me?

Yes. Steer Me Right charges a flat $395 for one model or $695 for up to three. We find it in dealer stock, get written out-the-door offers and negotiate for a price below MSRP. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do.

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing, and you’re the only one we work for.
