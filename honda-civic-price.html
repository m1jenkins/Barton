New, as a 2026

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Honda dealers put them on 35.1% of quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,159. On a car that sells 1–2% under sticker, that can be more than the whole discount.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 look the same on the lot. The 2027 costs $300 more, and Honda’s 3.99%, which ends November 30, doesn’t cover it.
  • Honda financing. The 3.99% and every $500 offer need Honda Financial Services. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • “No payments for 90 days.” Interest still builds during those 90 days, so it isn’t a discount.
  • Steering on a used one. On a 2022 to 2025 Civic, ask for proof that steering recall 24V744 is done.

Is the Honda Civic a good car?

Most reviewers rank it at or near the top of its class. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.4 out of 10, first of eight compact cars. Edmunds rates the gas Civic 6.9 and the hybrid 7.9, its 2026 Top Rated Car.

How much under MSRP can you get a Honda Civic?

Less than most compact cars. On a 2026 LX or Sport, 2–3% under MSRP is fair and 4–5% strong. On hybrids, 1–2% is fair and 3–4% strong. That’s our read of Edmunds, CarWhere and iSeeCars figures, and a dealer may land a point either side.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Civic?

The 2026, for most buyers. It costs $300 less with the same engines, and Honda’s 3.99% financing covers only the 2026, through November 30. Buy the 2027 if you want the new gas Sport-L or S+ Shift on the Sport Touring Hybrid.

Is the Civic Hybrid worth it?

Yes, for most buyers. A Sport Hybrid sedan costs $2,700 more than a gas Sport and is rated 49 mpg combined against 34, about 110 fewer gallons a year at 12,000 miles. It’s also much quicker and adds heated seats and a moonroof.

What is the destination charge on a Civic?

$1,295 on the 2026 and the 2027, for every Civic including the Si and Type R. Honda’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and the $455 premium paint charge is extra too.

Does Honda offer cash back on the Civic?

No. On November 16, 2026, Honda offered 3.99% financing on the 2026 and leases on both years, but no customer cash. Honda owners get $500 on the sedans, and military buyers and graduates $500 on every Civic, all through Honda Financial Services.

Is Honda still making the Civic Si?

Not for 2027. Honda lists no 2027 Si, so the 2026 ($31,495 plus $1,295 destination, six-speed manual only) is the last one for now. It has no special financing or lease from Honda.

How much is a Civic Type R?

The 2026 is $47,395 and the 2027 $48,695, both before the $1,295 destination charge. Markups have mostly gone: Kelley Blue Book’s fair purchase price for a 2026 is $48,100, a little under the $48,690 sticker with destination.

Is a used Civic a better deal than new?

Rarely. A used 2025 asks a median $25,575 at about 29,500 miles, while Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 LX is $25,508 with destination. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Honda’s 3.99%. A HondaTrue Certified 2023 to 2025 is the used route we’d take.
