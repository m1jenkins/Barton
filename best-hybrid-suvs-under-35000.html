Best hybrid SUVs under $35,000 (2026)

By Mason · Updated November 21, 2026

  • Our pick

  • The five

  • Plans

Five hybrid SUVs start under $35,000 once destination is counted. We’d buy the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE: $33,495 with front drive or $34,895 with AWD, rated 43 mpg combined, with 37.8 cubic feet of cargo room and a J.D. Power owner score of 85 out of 100. The Kia Sportage Hybrid S is the one to price against it, $710 cheaper with heated seats and 41 mpg. The Honda CR-V and Subaru Forester hybrids start higher.

How we ranked them

Every price here is MSRP plus the maker’s destination charge, because a $34,700 sticker is $36,295 with a $1,595 fee, and most “under $35,000” lists leave that out. We ranked on what the cheapest sensible trim gives you: reviews and owner scores, mpg, room, and whether all-wheel drive fits under the line. The order is our judgment.

The five, ranked

  1. 1 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid 2026

    The LE is the cheapest RAV4 and it isn’t stripped: 12.3-inch digital gauges, a 10.5-inch touchscreen with wireless CarPlay and Android Auto, and Toyota Safety Sense 4.0. It’s rated 43 mpg combined with front drive and has 37.8 cubic feet of cargo room, against 22.8 in the Niro and 21.5 in the Corolla Cross. J.D. Power gives the RAV4 an owner score of 85 out of 100, tied for first among compact SUVs.

Common questions

What is the cheapest hybrid SUV?

The Kia Niro, at $31,385 with destination for the 2027 Niro Hybrid. Next come the Sportage Hybrid LX at $32,085 and the Tucson Hybrid SE at $32,950.

Which hybrid SUV under $35,000 has standard all-wheel drive?

The Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid. The RAV4 LE, Sportage Hybrid S and Tucson Hybrid offer it as an option; the Niro doesn’t offer it.

Is the Honda CR-V Hybrid under $35,000?

No. The cheapest CR-V hybrid, the Sport Hybrid, is $35,630 before a $1,450 destination charge, or $37,080. The Subaru Forester Premium Hybrid is $36,180 with destination.

Does destination count toward the $35,000?

Yes, in this ranking. It is on every window sticker and the dealer can’t waive it.
