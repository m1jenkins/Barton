Questions before you choose.

Can you get me a price below MSRP?

Usually, yes. On new cars, getting you a discount off MSRP is the goal of every negotiation. How much depends on the model, local supply and timing. Some high-demand models sell at or above sticker, and we’ll tell you up front if yours is one of them.

Which plan is right for me?

Steer Me Right if dealers within 200 miles of you are likely to have the car: one model, any trim, color or package. Steer Me Right Pro if you want dealers nationwide in the mix. It adds shipping quotes when the best deal is far away, support on your trade-in, and offers on up to three models if you’re torn between a few. Switching to a different model isn’t included on the Steer Me Right plan. Steer Me Right Private Client is for cars with an MSRP of $100,000 or more: it adds allocation and factory-order work, negotiation of market adjustments, and a check of the lease or loan terms and the paperwork before you sign. Not sure which fits? Ask Mason before you pay.

What happens after I pay?

You tell us what you’re looking for right after checkout. We reply to confirm the search and when it starts, then get to work.

How long does it take?

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You’re the only one we work for.

Do I have to buy the car you find?

No. We negotiate; you decide. You review the vehicle, the deal and the final paperwork, and you decide whether to buy. A specific price or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the U.S. We’re based in Austin and work with buyers nationwide, remotely. The Steer Me Right plan covers dealers within 200 miles of you, and Steer Me Right Pro covers dealers nationwide.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. or email hello@steermeright.com, Monday–Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central.
