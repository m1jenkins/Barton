What does Drive Right do?

We help Texas car buyers research vehicles, compare offers, and negotiate purchases. Your plan determines whether you receive AI-assisted research and outreach, an advisor through the buying process, or expanded sourcing and coordination.

What will it cost?

AI Agent Buying Service is $195, Hire Drive Right is $495, and Ultimate Concierge is $895. These are service fees. The vehicle, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, and any delivery costs are separate. Confirm current terms before booking.

Where can you help me buy?

We’re based in Austin and serve buyers across Texas. Search reach, seller participation, and delivery options depend on your plan and vehicle. We confirm those details with you before work begins.

Am I in control of the final decision?

Always. Building a brief doesn’t commit you to a vehicle. You choose your plan and decide whether to buy, after reviewing the vehicle, financing, insurance, and final paperwork. A specific discount or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.
