Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Why should I use Drive Right instead of buying a car myself?

Buying a car through a dealership can be stressful and time-consuming. Most people buy a car every 3 to 5 years, while dealership salespeople sell 3 to 5 cars per day! Your advisor finds the car you want, negotiates on your behalf, and keeps you fully informed. The service can reduce research and seller-communication work while helping you compare the documented price.

How do purchase outcomes vary?

Savings vary by vehicle, inventory, financing, trade-in, and market conditions. We do not promise a specific outcome. Our role is to research the deal, identify avoidable charges, and help you compare the available terms before you decide.

How does the trade-in process work?

We can gather and compare trade-in quotes and explain the stated options alongside your proposed purchase. Valuation and tax treatment depend on the transaction, and the final decision remains yours.

Can you help with financing or leasing?

We can help you compare the financing or leasing offers supplied for review and flag fees, incentives, and terms that need clarification. Approval, rate, availability, and final lender terms are not guaranteed.

Do you serve all of Texas?

Yes. While we are based in Austin, we serve clients across the entire state of Texas. Whether you are in Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, El Paso, or anywhere in between, we can handle the negotiation and logistics for you. We even handle out-of-state deals and coordinate shipping directly to your door.

Can you help me find a used or certified pre-owned car?

We can search new, used, and certified pre-owned inventory, compare available offers, and flag issues in the records supplied for review. Availability, condition, price, and seller terms vary by vehicle.

How do you get paid?

Drive Right currently offers a $295 consultation, a $495 Full Service option, and an $895 Concierge option. The pricing page describes the included work for each tier before checkout.
