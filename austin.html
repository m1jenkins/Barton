Who this service suits

Car-buying help can be useful when you have several vehicles to compare, limited time to contact sellers, or a written offer that needs organizing before you decide. Start with your budget, must-have features, and purchase timing. If you already have a candidate, send its listing and quote so the discussion starts with the actual vehicle.

Drive Right’s listed plans cover different amounts of research, seller communication, and coordination. You retain the purchase decision. A service engagement does not establish that a particular car is available or that a seller will accept your offer.

Austin base, Texas coverage

Drive Right operates from Austin and serves Texas buyers. Its other named service areas are Arlington, Dallas, El Paso, Fort Worth, Houston, New Braunfels, San Antonio, and San Marcos; these are service areas, not branch offices. The Texas resource hub provides statewide context.

For an Austin-area request, provide your ZIP code, the seller’s location, and how far you are willing to travel. Exact coverage boundaries and in-person availability still need owner confirmation. This page does not offer walk-in appointments or list a public storefront.

Remote work and in-person steps

The listed service includes research, written offer comparison, and seller communication; the AI plan specifically includes email outreach where available. Before booking, ask which steps can be completed remotely for your purchase and whether any advisor attendance is available. A test drive, physical inspection, document signing, and vehicle handover each need their own arrangement with the relevant provider or seller.

Pickup, delivery, and the transaction

Delivery coordination is listed under Concierge where available. Confirm who provides transport, its cost, the pickup and destination, expected timing, and how the vehicle’s condition will be documented. Do not plan around a delivery date until the seller or carrier confirms it.

Before any deposit or trip, ask the seller to confirm the VIN, availability, written total, deposit terms, required documents, and the person handling handover. If financing or a trade-in is involved, ask what still needs approval or inspection. These are preparation questions, not a promise that Drive Right performs every transaction step.

Austin-specific purchase planning

Local sources checked September 5, 2026. Verify current requirements with the responsible agency for your vehicle and county.

Use the correct county paperwork route

If your transaction belongs with Travis County, start with the Travis County Tax Office’s buyer instructions. For an individual sale, the office recommends having the seller accompany you so a representative can check the title before the application is submitted. Ask the office which documents your specific transfer requires; a seller’s location alone is not enough to choose your paperwork route.

Travis County offers title-transfer walk-ins at selected locations. Check the current title-services page for hours and locations before setting a handover time. Drive Right is not a county office or title-processing authority.

Account for Travis and Williamson emissions rules

The TCEQ emissions program includes Travis and Williamson counties. Its overview specifies annual testing for gasoline vehicles from model-year ages 2 through 24 and excludes electric and diesel vehicles, motorcycles, and mopeds. Check applicability for the vehicle’s registration county and age before scheduling. An emissions result does not answer the mechanical-condition questions in the used-car inspection draft checklist.

Plan dealer visits around the I-35 corridor

TxDOT’s I-35 Capital Express Central page identifies Austin construction segments and links to the current closure map. As a practical planning step, check that map before a test drive or pickup, reconfirm the seller’s entrance, and avoid making several appointments depend on one tight travel estimate. No fixed travel-time estimate is assumed here.

Questions to settle before booking

Can I use the $195 plan if I already found a car?

Share the listing and what you want help comparing. Ask Drive Right to confirm whether the AI plan’s research and available dealer outreach fit the request or whether advisor support is needed.

Will an advisor meet me at an Austin dealership?

In-person attendance is not established in the current service record. Ask for confirmation of availability, location, scope, and any separate fee before making plans.

Does the service include a mechanic’s inspection?

The listed plans do not establish an included mechanical inspection. Ask who would select and book an independent inspector, what the inspection covers, and who pays. Research and offer comparison do not substitute for a mechanic’s findings.

What should I send with an inquiry?

Send your ZIP code, budget, vehicle requirements, desired timing, links to candidates, and any written quotes. Mention a trade-in, travel limit, or delivery need. Use the form below to ask about scope; do not include payment details or identity documents.
