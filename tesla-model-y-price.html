The bottom line

Used, 2023 or 2024

A used Model Y is one of the easier EVs to buy at a dealer, if you pick the year carefully. A 2023 averaged $33,200 in September listings, against $41,630 for the cheapest new one with fees. They sell in a median 16 days, so dealers rarely cut much. Shop a 2024 Long Range and aim for 1–2% under asking.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Open recalls. Run the VIN at NHTSA. The headlight recall on 2020–2023 cars (26V507) has no fix yet, and the suspension, seat and steering wheel recalls need a service visit.
  • The front suspension. NHTSA opened an investigation in July 2026 into front lateral links failing on 2021–2023 Model Ys. Have an independent shop look at it before you buy.
  • Hardware 3. Most cars built before mid-2023 have Hardware 3, which Tesla says can’t run unsupervised FSD. If a listing charges for FSD, check the car’s screen shows it.
  • The “warranty” on the window. A dealer’s warranty is its own or a third party’s. Tesla’s used warranty covers only cars Tesla sells. Ask for the first delivery date.
  • Door handles on a 2021. NHTSA is investigating 2021 Model Ys whose outside handles stop working when the 12-volt battery runs low. Ask when that battery was last replaced.

How much is a used Tesla Model Y?

Dealers listed them at an average of $26,400 for a 2020 up to $39,700 for a 2025 in September 2026, by Recurrent’s count. Each 10,000 miles takes about $1,000 off. A new Model Y starts at $39,990 before $1,640 in fees.

How much under asking can you get a used Model Y?

On a 2023 to 2025, 1–2% under asking is fair and 3–4% strong. On a 2020 to 2022, about 1% is fair and 2–3% strong. Those ranges are our estimate: used Model Ys sell in about 16 days, so dealers rarely cut much.

What is the best year for a used Model Y?

The 2024, in our view. It was built after the switch to Hardware 4, has five recall campaigns against 17 to 24 for the 2020 to 2023, and battery cover that runs into 2032. A 2023 built after mid-year is next, at about $3,300 less.

Does the Tesla battery warranty transfer to a used buyer?

Yes, at no cost, whoever sells you the car. It runs 8 years or 120,000 miles on a Long Range or Performance and 8 years or 100,000 miles on a Standard or Standard Range, counted from first delivery.

Does a used Model Y from a dealer come with Tesla’s warranty?

Not Tesla’s used warranty. Its 1-year/10,000-mile cover applies only to used cars Tesla sells and delivers itself. The remainder of the original 4-year/50,000-mile basic warranty and the battery warranty do carry over.

Does Full Self-Driving transfer with a used Model Y?

Tesla says FSD bought outright stays with the car. It now sells FSD only as a subscription, which doesn’t carry over. Check that FSD shows on the car’s own screen before you pay extra for it. Tesla says Hardware 3 cars can’t run unsupervised FSD.

Is a used Model Y reliable?

Mixed. U.S. News predicts 78 out of 100. Owners of three-year-old Teslas reported 226 problems per 100 vehicles in J.D. Power’s 2026 dependability study, against an industry average of 204, and Germany’s TÜV failed 17.3% of two- to three-year-old Model Ys at inspection.
