The best used Foresters are the 2022 to 2025 model years, which draw 89, 51, 18 and 59 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a yardstick of 89 for this car. Skip the 2016 to 2020: they draw 233, 336, 291, 463 and 341, and the 2019 and 2020 stand out for one part, with visibility named in 406 of the 2019’s 836 complaints and 301 of the 2020’s 605. In the complaint text we read, nearly all of those are cracked windshields. The 2021 (139) is Check first, and the 2022 sits right at the yardstick.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints by Subaru’s U.S. Forester sales, which count gas and hybrid Foresters together. Sales were low in 2022 (114,096 against 152,566 in 2023), which lifts that year’s rate, and the 2025 is a redesign with 105 complaints and 3 recalls. The 2026 is too new to rate and has a do-not-drive ball joint recall. Our Forester price guide covers what a new or used one costs.

Forester generations: what changed

Fourth generation 2014–2018

The SJ Forester went on sale in March 2013 with a 170-hp 2.5-liter, a 250-hp turbo that rose to 268 hp, a six-speed manual or CVT, and a facelift for 2017 (Wikipedia). The 2016 to 2018 years on this page are its last three.

Fifth generation 2019–2025

The SK Forester had one U.S. engine, a 182-hp 2.5-liter, with a CVT only, and a facelift for 2022 that introduced the Wilderness trim (Wikipedia). The 2025 is a redesign, which our Forester price guide covers.

Common Forester problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 3,522 Forester complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Windshields, 2019 and 2020 2019–2020 model years707 complaints naming wipers and visibility, 198 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s visibility category holds 406 complaints on the 2019 and 301 on the 2020, against 24 on the 2022. In the text we read, almost all describe a windshield cracking, and a few mention EyeSight, Subaru’s camera-based driver assist. No recall covers it, so a VIN lookup shows nothing; ask when the glass was last replaced.
  • Passenger air bag sensor, 2016 to 2018 2016–2018 model years368 complaints naming air bags, 70 per 100,000 sold. Recall 19V-701 covers 2015 to 2018 Foresters with heated seats whose front passenger occupant detection connection can loosen, which can switch the passenger air bag off with someone in the seat. Most of the air bag complaints we read mention the seat’s warning light. Dealers inspect and replace the harness, free.
  • Engine on the 2019 2019 model years113 complaints naming engine, 63 per 100,000 sold. Recall 19V-856 covers a PCV (crankcase ventilation) valve that can separate and let parts enter the engine, which can cost power. Dealers replace the valve, or the short block engine if the parts can’t be found, free. The 2019 has 113 engine complaints.
  • Electrical faults across the years 2016–2020 model years511 complaints naming electrical system, 58 per 100,000 sold. Electrical is among the four leading parts on every year from 2016 to 2020 (93 on the 2016, 135 on the 2019). NHTSA’s field doesn’t say what failed, so the count shows only which part owners pointed to.
  • Automatic emergency braking, recent years 2022–2026 model years56 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 9.1 per 100,000 sold. The system draws a steady trickle on the 2022 to 2026 (15, 13 and 13 complaints on the 2022, 2023 and 2025). No recall or open investigation names the Forester’s system.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Forester: 635 mentions in 475 threads, 247 positive and 320 negative, a net of −15 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Forester is 62 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/subaruforester and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Subaru Forester price guide has the full topic breakdown.

The head gasket and oil worries are about older engines than the table’s 2016 and later years, and the windshield pattern behind the 2019 and 2020 rates barely shows up in the threads, so the complaint counts here carry that part of the record.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report 150k to 250k miles with only routine maintenance on well-kept Foresters
  • Models after about 2011 are seen as far less prone to head gasket failure
  • Hybrid uses a planetary eCVT seen as sturdier than the older belt CVT

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report head gasket leaks and oil consumption on older Forester engines
  • Owners report CVT valve body and solenoid failures on 2014 to 2016 models
  • Owners report rusted rear strut towers and worn control arm bushings and wheel bearings

Other complaints that come up

  • Interior and tech. Owners report the large touchscreen lagging, slow to start and dropping CarPlay
  • Fuel economy. Owners report city and short-trip economy in the low to mid 20s

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Left-front ball joint 2026Recall 26V-685. A castle nut on the lower control arm ball joint may be loose, which can let the joint separate and cost control of the car. Subaru tells owners not to drive an affected car until the dealer has replaced the joint, free.
  • Passenger air bag sensor harness 2015–2018 with heated seatsRecall 19V-701. A loosening connection in the passenger seat’s occupant detection system can switch off the passenger air bag when someone is in the seat. Dealers inspect and replace the harness, free.
  • PCV valve 2019Recall 19V-856. An aluminum crankcase ventilation valve can separate and send parts into the engine, which can cost power while driving. Dealers replace the valve, or the engine block assembly if needed, free.
  • Front driveshaft 2023–2024Recall 23V-754. The outer race of the front driveshafts can crack and break, which can cut drive power or let the car roll away in Park. Dealers inspect and replace the shafts, free.
  • Engine water pipe bolts 2024Recall 23V-723. Loose bolts can let a coolant pipe leak onto hot exhaust. The cars covered had not been delivered when it was filed, so owners got no letter; the VIN lookup says if yours was affected.
  • Transmission inhibitor switch 2022Recall 23V-755. Water can enter a poorly welded switch and cause it to fail, which can leave the reverse lights and rearview camera off. Dealers replace the switch, free.
  • 18-inch wheels 2025Recall 25V-033. Alloy wheels with an incorrect lug seat can attach badly and cost stability. Dealers inspect and replace the wheels, free.
  • Moonroof glass and hatch bracket 2026Recall 26V-346 and 25V-889. The moonroof’s glass panel may not be bonded properly and can detach; the rear hatch support bracket may be wrong or backwards and let the hatch fall. Dealers inspect and replace the parts, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Occupant detection system failure 2016–2018PE19009, closed. NHTSA opened the evaluation on August 6, 2019 after 51 complaints that the passenger air bag sensor failed or switched the air bag off with an adult in the seat on 2016 to 2018 Foresters. Subaru filed a defect report on October 3, 2019 covering 366,282 2015 to 2018 Foresters with seat heaters, and recall 19V-701 followed. NHTSA closed the evaluation on January 22, 2020.

What to check before you buy a used Forester

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Subaru dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Passenger air bag recall on a 2016 to 2018.Confirm 19V-701 is done, and on a car with heated seats check that the passenger air bag warning light is off with someone in the seat.
  • The windshield on a 2017 to 2021.Look for chips and cracks in the glass, ask when it was last replaced, and ask whether the replacement kept the camera system calibrated. No recall covers it.
  • The 2019 recalls.Confirm 19V-856 (PCV valve), 21V-263 (rear stabilizer bracket bolts) and 19V-065 (steering) are closed on a 2019.
  • Driveshaft and water pipe on a 2023 or 2024.Confirm 23V-754 (driveshaft) and, on a 2024, 23V-723 (engine water pipe).
  • The ball joint on a 2026.Ask whether 26V-685 is done and get the repair in writing. Subaru tells owners not to drive an affected car until it is fixed.
  • An independent inspection.Reddit owners report head gasket leaks and oil consumption on older engines, CVT faults on 2014 to 2016 cars and rusted rear strut towers. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check coolant and oil level, the rear suspension and the CVT before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Forester has 37 NHTSA complaints and 4 recalls so far, including ball joint recall 26V-685, so it is too new to rate. The 2022 to 2024 record describes the previous design, so it says little about the redesign, and a used 2022 to 2024 has fewer open questions than a new one.

Common questions

What is the worst Subaru Forester year?

The 2019, at 463 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold, then the 2020 at 341. Visibility, which in the complaint text we read means cracked windshields, accounts for 406 of the 2019’s 836 complaints.

What is the best used Forester year?

The 2022 to 2025 model years, at 89, 51, 18 and 59 complaints per 100,000 sold. The 2024 is lowest at 18, though it has two recalls.

Do Subaru Foresters have a windshield problem?

NHTSA complaints on the 2019 and 2020 Forester name visibility 406 and 301 times, and nearly all of those we read describe a cracked windshield. No recall covers it, so a VIN lookup won’t show it.

Is the 2025 Subaru Forester reliable?

It is a redesign and the early record is mixed: a rate of 59, 105 complaints and three recalls (25V-033, 25V-419 and 26V-436). We’d buy one with the recalls confirmed.

How do I check a used Forester for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Subaru dealer.
