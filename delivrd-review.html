Delivrd review: what its $1,000 car negotiation service includes, from its own pages

By Mason · Updated November 10, 2026

Short answer: Delivrd (often searched as “Delivered”) negotiates new-car deals for a flat $1,000, paid up front, and brings you out-the-door prices from several dealers with trade-in and financing review. Read the rules before you pay: new cars only, refunds only before work starts, $250 to change trim and $500 to change cars. Steer Me Right does the same finding and negotiating for new, used and CPO cars at $395 (one model) or $695 (up to three). Facts checked November 10, 2026.

We run Steer Me Right, a competitor, so this is not a neutral review. We don’t rate Delivrd or repeat customer reviews; every statement below comes from Delivrd’s own pages, linked beside it, so you can check them yourself.

Who Delivrd is

Delivrd is a car negotiation service at delivrdto.me. Its team page lists founder Tomi Mikula with deal coordinators and deal negotiators, and its press kit says it negotiates with dealerships for buyers across all 50 states.

What Delivrd costs

Its Insta-Pay Deal Negotiation is $1,000 per deal, paid up front, per its Insta-Pay page on November 10, 2026. The service agreement describes it as “Flat fee. One deal. No hidden costs, no percentage of the sale, no upsells,” and says “We put in the hours whether you buy the car or not.”

What you get

By its Insta-Pay page, Delivrd gets out-the-door pricing from several dealers, gives you at least two options to choose from, reviews your trade-in and financing, and helps you close. On November 10, 2026 the page said work begins within 5 to 8 business days of the form; other Delivrd pages have said 10 to 15 business days. It also warns that some models carry long waits, such as 6 to 12 months for a Lexus GX550 allocation.

Rules to know before you pay

  • New cars only. On November 10, 2026 the Insta-Pay page was titled “Insta-Pay Deal Negotiation (New Cars)” with the heading “New Cars Only.” In October 2026 the same page called this a temporary pause on used cars; that wording is gone.
  • Refunds. The service agreement says you can get a refund if Delivrd hasn’t started working your deal. Once work begins, “There are no refunds after work has started.”
  • Change fees. $250 if you change the features or trim to a configuration that is harder to find, and $500 if you switch to a different car entirely.
  • Paid up front. The $1,000 is due before work starts.

How Delivrd is paid

Delivrd’s About page says it never accepts kickbacks or bonuses from dealerships or manufacturers. The buyer pays the flat fee. That is the same model as Steer Me Right: dealers and lenders pay us nothing, and you’re the only one we work for.

Who Delivrd suits

A buyer of a new car who knows the exact configuration, wants trade-in and financing review bundled in, and can wait for the quoted start date. If your car is used or CPO, or you are still choosing between models, Delivrd’s own rules point you elsewhere; see Delivrd alternatives and our used car buying service.
