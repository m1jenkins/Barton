What “near me” means for a car buying service

You don’t visit us, and we don’t visit the dealer. A negotiation happens by phone and email whether the advisor is across town or across the country, and the dealer’s written offer reads the same either way. The local part is the inventory: which dealers within driving distance of you have the car, and what each will put in writing.

So the useful question is not “is there a car buying service in my city?” It is “will this one contact the dealers around me, and who pays it?” We contact dealers that have your car in stock wherever you live. You pay us a flat fee, and nobody else pays us anything.

Common questions

Is there a car buying service near me?

Steer Me Right works with dealers in every state from one office in Austin, Texas. The work is done by phone and email, so you don’t need a local office. You pick up the car from the dealer.

Do you charge more in bigger cities?

No. The fee is $395 for one model or $695 for up to three, wherever you live.

Can you use dealers in a neighboring state?

Yes, on Steer Me Right Pro, which includes out-of-state dealers and help coordinating shipping when the dealer is open to it. The Steer Me Right plan covers dealers in your own state.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel before negotiations begin and you get a full refund. During negotiations, refund eligibility depends on the work already done. Read the refund policy.
