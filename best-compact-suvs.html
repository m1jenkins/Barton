Best compact SUVs for 2026, ranked

By Mason · Updated November 21, 2026

  • Our pick
  • At a glance
  • The ranking
  • Plans
  • Questions

The Toyota RAV4 is our pick for most buyers: every one is a hybrid, rated 43 mpg combined with front drive, and J.D. Power’s owner score of 85 out of 100 is tied for the best in the class. The catch is price. Our estimates put a fair deal on a Tucson Hybrid or Sportage Hybrid at 3–4% under sticker against 1–2% for the RAV4, so they cost less to buy and give up a few mpg. The Chevrolet Equinox ranks last: it’s rated 27 mpg combined, 16 behind the RAV4, and U.S. News puts it 10th of 15 compact SUVs.

How we ranked them

The order is our judgment. We weighed each publisher’s score (Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book, U.S. News and J.D. Power, each quoted separately and never averaged), what owners report on Reddit and in J.D. Power’s owner ratings, fuel economy, and what a car costs after a typical dealer discount. A hybrid priced like a gas car beat a better-reviewed gas car, which is why the CX-5 and Forester sit below the three hybrids.

  1. 1 Toyota RAV4 2026

    Toyota dropped the gas engine, so every RAV4 is a hybrid: 43 mpg combined with front-wheel drive against 40 for a CR-V Hybrid and 38 for a Tucson Hybrid. J.D. Power’s owner score of 85 out of 100 is tied for the best in the class, and IIHS rates it Good in seven of its eight tests.

    Who it suits. Buy it if you keep cars for a decade and want the lowest fuel bill in the class. It loses value about half as fast as the average vehicle.

    The catch. Dealers had 24 days of RAV4s in stock at the end of October against 87 for the industry, Toyota offers no cash back and most trims sell within 2% of sticker. Reviewers also fault its ride and seat comfort.

    The XLE Premium adds a power liftgate, heated SofTex (synthetic leather) seats and parking sensors that brake the car. AWD adds $1,400.

Common questions

What is the best compact SUV for 2026?

The Toyota RAV4, in our ranking. It’s hybrid-only, rated 43 mpg combined with front drive, and J.D. Power’s owner score of 85 out of 100 ties it for first among compact SUVs. The Hyundai Tucson Hybrid is second for its cargo room and the Kia Sportage Hybrid third for its price.

Which compact SUVs come as hybrids?

The RAV4 (every one), CR-V, Tucson, Sportage and Forester. Nissan’s Rogue Hybrid starts at $37,065 with destination, and the CX-5 and Equinox have no hybrid.

What is the cheapest compact SUV?

On starting price with destination, the Sportage LX at $30,485, then the Rogue S at $31,265 and the Tucson SE at $31,350. The RAV4 LE is the dearest starting point at $33,495, but it’s a hybrid.

Does destination count in these prices?

Yes. Each car’s price is its MSRP plus the maker’s destination charge, which runs from $1,450 on the CR-V and Forester to $1,995 on the Equinox. Dealers add it to every window sticker.
