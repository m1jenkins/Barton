What a car-buying service does

A car-buying service does the part of car shopping you’d otherwise do across listing sites, dealer calls and written quotes. We start from your search details, find cars that fit, and send you written recommendations. Your advisor also compares the offers, negotiates price and fees with the dealers and lines up the next steps. People search for this work under different names: car buying agent, car buyer’s advocate, car negotiation service or car buying consultant. The job is the same, and the difference that matters is who pays the person doing it. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing.

The fee doesn’t include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance or delivery costs. What’s in stock, which dealers respond and how long it takes all vary. We can take the legwork off your hands, but you still need to check the car and the final paperwork yourself.

What a car negotiator can review

A car negotiator works from the information available for a particular vehicle and seller. That may include the listed price, a written quote, and seller-provided fee details. We compare those terms, flag anything that needs explaining, and negotiate price and fees with the dealer. The goal is a short list of written offers you can compare. We can’t promise a specific discount.

Negotiation is one part of the purchase. A mechanic, lender or official source may need to check the car’s condition, the seller’s claims, the financing, taxes, registration, warranty, insurance or contract. Arranging an inspection and confirming the terms before you sign is up to you.

What stays with the buyer

You decide which car, if any, to buy. You also arrange the inspection, pick financing and insurance, review the contract and final paperwork, and confirm pickup or delivery. On Steer Me Right Pro, we get shipping quotes when the best deal is far away. You pay the carrier; shipping is not part of our fee.

Questions about
the service.

What will the car buying service cost?

The Steer Me Right plan is a $395 USD one-time service fee and covers one model. Steer Me Right Pro is a $695 USD one-time service fee and covers up to three models. The fee doesn’t include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance or delivery costs.

Can Steer Me Right help if I already know the exact car?

Yes. Your search details can include a specific year, make, model, trim, mileage range, color, transmission, and search radius. What we can find still depends on what’s in stock, which dealers respond and your plan.

Can Steer Me Right choose the car for me?

No. Steer Me Right can research and document options, but you choose the vehicle and decide whether to proceed after reviewing its condition and the final terms.

Is the service only for buyers in Texas?

No. Steer Me Right is based in Austin and supports buyers nationwide through remote service. Our city pages are service areas, not storefronts.
