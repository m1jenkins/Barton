What a car-buying service does

A car-buying service helps organize work that buyers would otherwise manage across listing sites, seller conversations, and offer documents. Drive Right starts with your search details, researches vehicles that fit it, and documents recommendations for your review. Depending on the plan, an advisor can also compare available offers, discuss price and fees with participating sellers, and coordinate the next steps that are included in the engagement.

The service fee pays for that support. It does not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs. Inventory, seller participation, timing, financing, and purchase outcomes vary. A car-buying service can make the process more organized, but it cannot replace your review of the vehicle and final transaction documents.

What a car negotiator can review

A car negotiator works from the information available for a particular vehicle and seller. That may include the listed price, a written quote, and seller-provided fee details. Drive Right can compare those terms, identify questions that need clarification, and communicate about price and fees with participating sellers. The goal is to give you a clearer set of documented options to review—not to promise a particular discount or outcome.

Negotiation is one part of the purchase. Vehicle condition, seller representations, financing, taxes, registration, warranties, insurance, and contracts may require review by the relevant qualified provider or official source. You remain responsible for arranging any independent inspection and for confirming every material term before signing.

What stays with the buyer

  • You decide which vehicle, if any, to buy.
  • You review the vehicle condition and arrange an independent inspection when appropriate.
  • You select and confirm financing and insurance.
  • You review the purchase contract, registration, taxes, warranties, optional products, and final paperwork.
  • You confirm pickup or delivery arrangements for the specific purchase. Ultimate Concierge includes delivery coordination where available.

What will the car buying service cost?

Full Service is a $395 USD one-time service fee. Ultimate Concierge is a $695 USD one-time service fee. The service fee pays for that support. It does not include the vehicle price, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance, or delivery costs.

Can Drive Right help if I already know the exact car?

Yes. Your search details can include a specific year, make, model, trim, mileage range, color, transmission, and search radius. The available research and seller communication still depend on inventory, seller participation, and the plan you choose.

Can Drive Right choose the car for me?

No. Drive Right can research and document options, but you choose the vehicle and decide whether to proceed after reviewing its condition and the final terms.

Is the service only for buyers in Texas?

No. Drive Right is based in Austin and supports buyers nationwide through remote service. A listed city is a service area, not a claim of a storefront or local office.
