What to pay: how far under MSRP

A leftover 2026 Sportage sells further under sticker than a 2027, and Kia sells further under than Toyota does on the RAV4. Edmunds’ Texas suggested prices (sales through November 2) run 4.7–6.6% under sticker with destination on the 2026 gas Sportage and 5.0–6.2% under on the hybrid, but only 3.2–3.9% on the plug-in, while TrueCar’s starting market averages are lower, 0.8–4.5% under. CarWhere’s 13 verified 2026 buyers paid a median 4.8% under MSRP (the middle half 4.3–8.7%). Edmunds counts 113 days to sell a new 2026 gas Sportage and 175 for the hybrid, against 34 for the average new vehicle, and Kia adds $750 customer cash. The 2027 is tighter: Edmunds’ prices on the 2027 hybrid run 3.4–3.7% under, buyers are paying about $700 (gas) to $800 (hybrid) under, and new 2027s turn in 27 and 21 days.

The trim we’d buy

A Sportage Hybrid S. A 2026 S costs $32,790 with AWD standard, and a 2027 starts at $31,290 with front drive (AWD $32,890). That is $700 more than a 2027 Hybrid LX and adds heated front seats, a power driver’s seat, blind-spot detection and 18-inch wheels. It was Edmunds’ pick of the 2026 hybrids. The hybrid costs $1,600 more than a gas LX ($30,590 against $28,990 on a 2027), makes 232 hp against 187, and the EPA puts its fuel cost at $1,450 a year against $2,150. AWD drops a hybrid to 35 mpg combined from 41 or 42, so take front drive on a 2027 S unless you see snow.

Pick the gas EX ($30,590 as a 2026) if sticker price matters most. It’s Edmunds’ pick of the gas trims, but its 2.5-liter four took more than 9.5 seconds to reach 60 mph in Edmunds’ test and is rated 28 mpg combined. Skip the X-Pro Prestige: it’s rated 24 mpg combined, and Edmunds found its all-terrain tires soften on-road handling. The plug-in (X-Line from $40,490, 33 miles of EPA electric range) costs $7,700 more than a 2026 Hybrid S; consider it only if you can charge at home.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Kia dealers put add-ons on 43.3% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,477. On a $30,590 EX that is about a fair discount’s worth. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • The model year and the rate. The 0% is for a 2026. A 2027 gas Sportage gets 1.99% and a 2027 hybrid 0.90%. Ask which model year the dealer’s quote assumes.
  • AWD-only hybrids on the 2026. A 2026 hybrid S, EX, X-Line or SX Prestige is all-wheel drive, rated 35 mpg combined against 42 for the front-drive LX. A 2027 S or EX comes in front drive at 41.
  • Specially tagged cars. Kia’s $750 Retail Bonus Cash on a gas 2026 Sportage is for specially tagged vehicles only, and it doesn’t work with Kia’s leases. Ask the dealer to show the tag.
  • The instrument panel recall. Recall 26V046 covers 2026 Sportages, Hybrids and Plug-ins: a software error can make the instrument panel display fail. Kia fixes it over the air or at a dealer, free. Ask the dealer whether the car you want has had it.
  • The Kia Finance requirement. The 0%, 1.99% and 0.90% rates need Kia Finance America approval for well-qualified buyers, and Kia says only a limited number of customers qualify. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • The slow gas engine. Edmunds’ gas test car took more than 9.5 seconds to reach 60 mph, which it calls seconds behind class leaders. Drive a gas Sportage with passengers aboard, and the hybrid too, before you choose.

Common questions

Is the Kia Sportage a good car?

Reviewers rank it near the top of its class, with a caveat about the gas engine. U.S. News scores the 2026 gas Sportage 8.9 out of 10, third of 14 compact SUVs, and the Sportage Hybrid 9.3, second of 19 hybrid SUVs. Edmunds rates the gas car 7.8 and the hybrid 8.6, Kelley Blue Book gives the gas car 4.6 out of 5, and J.D. Power owners score it 81 out of 100. Consumer Reports expects the 2026 to be more reliable than the average new car, and iSeeCars counts 21.4% of its value lost in three years against 24.8% for the average vehicle.

How much under MSRP can you get a Kia Sportage?

On a 2026, 4–5% under MSRP is a fair deal on a gas Sportage and 6% or more a strong one. On a hybrid LX, S, EX or X-Line, 3–4% is fair and 5–6% strong; on a plug-in, 2–3% is fair. Those ranges are our read of Edmunds’ Texas prices (4.7–6.6% under on gas, 5.0–6.2% on hybrids), TrueCar’s lower averages and CarWhere’s 13 verified buyers (a median 4.8% under). A 2027 may sell a couple of points closer to sticker, and Kia’s cash is separate.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Sportage?

Price both. A 2026 costs $100 to $200 less on most trims, carries 0% for 48 months and $750 customer cash, and sells further under sticker. A 2027 hybrid S or EX comes in front drive, which the 2026 never offered, and its rate is 0.90% for the hybrid and 1.99% for gas.

Is the Sportage Hybrid worth it over the gas Sportage?

For most buyers, yes. A 2027 hybrid LX costs $1,600 more than a gas LX ($30,590 against $28,990) but is rated 41 mpg combined against 28 and makes 232 hp against 187. The EPA puts its fuel cost at $1,450 a year against $2,150. Edmunds measured 8.0 seconds to 60 mph in the hybrid and more than 9.5 in the gas car, and its cargo area is the same size (39.5 cubic feet behind the rear seats against 39.6).

Is AWD worth it on a Sportage?

If you see snow, yes. On the gas Sportage it adds $1,800 and costs 2 mpg (26 combined against 28). On a hybrid it costs more fuel: 35 mpg combined against 41 or 42 for front drive. A 2026 hybrid S, EX, X-Line or SX Prestige is AWD only; a 2027 S or EX can be front drive.

What is the destination charge on a Sportage?

$1,495 on both the 2026 and the 2027, up from $1,395 at the 2026 launch. Kia’s advertised starting prices leave it out.

Does Kia offer cash back on the Sportage?

Yes. In Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York on November 18, 2026, Kia listed $750 customer cash on a 2026 Sportage and Sportage Hybrid and $500 on a 2027 Sportage Hybrid, plus $750 Retail Bonus Cash on a 2026 hybrid or plug-in (and on specially tagged gas cars). All end January 4, 2027.
