How we set these discount targets

A leftover 2026 Sportage sells further under sticker than a 2027, and Kia sells further under than Toyota does on the RAV4. Edmunds’ Texas suggested prices (sales through November 2) run 4.7–6.6% under sticker with destination on the 2026 gas Sportage and 5.0–6.2% under on the hybrid, but only 3.2–3.9% on the plug-in, while TrueCar’s starting market averages are lower, 0.8–4.5% under. CarWhere’s 13 verified 2026 buyers paid a median 4.8% under MSRP (the middle half 4.3–8.7%). Edmunds counts 113 days to sell a new 2026 gas Sportage and 175 for the hybrid, against 34 for the average new vehicle, and Kia adds $750 customer cash. The 2027 is tighter: Edmunds’ prices on the 2027 hybrid run 3.4–3.7% under, buyers are paying about $700 (gas) to $800 (hybrid) under, and new 2027s turn in 27 and 21 days.

Check: That Kia’s rate covers the car you’re buying, that a hybrid S or EX is the AWD you want (the 2026 had no front drive), and that recall 26V046 is done.

Best when: You want a front-drive hybrid S or EX (new for 2027), or a gas X-Line with the sunroof and liftgate standard.

Check: The $100 to $200 price rise, and that the 0.90% (hybrid) or 1.99% rate is the one in your quote.

CPO (Kia Certified Pre-Owned)

Best when: You want a 2023 or newer Sportage with a warranty.

Check: That it’s certified, not just used. Kia’s terms are a 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty and 1 year or 12,000 miles of Platinum coverage after a 165-point inspection. Ask for the age and mileage limits in writing.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $24,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2023 that its seven recalls are done.

If you buy used, look at a 2024 or 2025 and be wary of a 2020 or 2023. The 2020 drew 172 NHTSA owner complaints, 81 about the engine. The 2023 drew 212 and seven recalls; the 2024 drew 77 and the 2025 56.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Kia dealers put add-ons on 43.3% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,477. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products: refuse or negotiate them.
  • The model year and the rate. The 0% is for a 2026. A 2027 gas Sportage gets 1.99% and a 2027 hybrid 0.90%. Ask which year the quote assumes.
  • AWD-only 2026 hybrids. A 2026 hybrid S or EX is AWD only, at 35 mpg combined against 42 for the front-drive LX. A 2027 comes in front drive at 41.
  • Specially tagged cars. Kia’s $750 Retail Bonus Cash on a gas 2026 Sportage is for specially tagged vehicles only. Ask the dealer to show the tag.
  • The instrument panel recall. Recall 26V046 covers 2026 Sportages, Hybrids and Plug-ins: a software error can make the instrument panel display fail. Ask if the free fix is done.

Is the Kia Sportage a good car?

Reviewers rank it near the top of its class, with a caveat about the gas engine. U.S. News scores the 2026 gas Sportage 8.9 out of 10, third of 14 compact SUVs. Edmunds rates the gas car 7.8 and the hybrid 8.6.

How much under MSRP can you get a Kia Sportage?

On a 2026 gas Sportage, 4–5% under MSRP is fair and 6% or more strong. On a hybrid LX, S, EX or X-Line, 3–4% is fair and 5–6% strong. That’s our read of Edmunds, TrueCar and CarWhere figures. Kia’s cash is separate.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Sportage?

Price both. A 2026 costs $100 to $200 less on most trims, carries 0% for 48 months and $750 customer cash, and sells further under sticker. A 2027 hybrid S or EX comes in front drive, at 0.90%.

Is the Sportage Hybrid worth it over the gas Sportage?

For most buyers, yes. A 2027 hybrid LX costs $1,600 more than a gas LX and is rated 41 mpg combined against 28, with 232 hp against 187. The EPA puts its fuel cost at $1,450 a year against $2,150.

Is AWD worth it on a Sportage?

If you see snow, yes. On the gas Sportage it adds $1,800 and costs 2 mpg (26 combined against 28). On a hybrid it costs more fuel: 35 mpg combined against 41 or 42 for front drive.

What is the destination charge on a Sportage?

$1,495 on both the 2026 and the 2027, up from $1,395 at the 2026 launch. Kia’s advertised starting prices leave it out.

Does Kia offer cash back on the Sportage?

Yes. On November 18, 2026, Kia listed $750 customer cash on a 2026 Sportage or Sportage Hybrid and $500 on a 2027 Sportage Hybrid, plus $750 Retail Bonus Cash on a 2026 hybrid or plug-in. All end January 4, 2027.

Is a used Sportage a better deal than new?

Rarely. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2025 is $28,319, and Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 LX is $28,870 with destination. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Kia’s 0% on a new 2026. Kia Certified Pre-Owned is the used route we’d take.
