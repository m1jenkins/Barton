The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The Accord is the midsize sedan most reviewers would buy, and the hybrid is the version to get: an EX-L Hybrid is rated 48 mpg combined against 31 for a gas SE. There’s no all-wheel drive. TrueCar’s November averages run 1.2% to 4.9% under sticker by trim. Price a 2026 EX-L Hybrid and aim for 3–4% under MSRP.

  • No all-wheel drive, unlike the Camry

How we set these discount targets

Accords sell under sticker, and the dearer the trim, the further under. Edmunds says they sell in about 25 days against a 34-day market average, with about 31,400 in stock nationwide, and Cox Automotive counted 57 days of supply for Honda at the end of October (it doesn’t list the Accord on its own). TrueCar’s November averages, from 513 sales, put the 2026 at 1.2% to 4.9% under sticker by trim; Edmunds’ Texas prices run 2.3% to 5.7% under, and CarWhere’s 105 verified buyers of 2025 and 2026 Accords paid a median 6.3% under MSRP.

Best when: Most buyers. Honda’s 3.49–3.99% and its leases end January 4.

Check: That the EX-L you want is in stock nearby, since Edmunds says stock is tight, and that Honda’s rate beats your bank’s in total.

Wait for the next Accord

Best when: You can hold off and want whatever Honda changes next.

Check: Honda hasn’t announced a U.S. 2027 Accord, its changes or its price.

CPO (HondaTrue Certified)

Best when: You want a 2023 to 2025 with a warranty, and you’re paying cash.

Check: HondaTrue Certified covers 2021 and newer cars under 80,000 miles: the powertrain to 7 years/100,000 miles from first sale, everything else 2 years past the new-car warranty. The hybrid battery is covered 8 years/100,000 miles from first sale.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $25,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, fuel pump recall 24V763 on a 2023 or 2024, and hybrid software recall 25V785 on a 2023 to 2025 Hybrid.

Buying used? Make 2023 the floor. NHTSA has an open investigation into 2018 to 2022 Accords that brake hard on their own. On a 2024 or 2025, ask about the steering: owners have filed 128 NHTSA complaints about it, and there’s no recall.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Honda dealers put add-ons on 35.1% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,159. On a $35,000 Accord that’s most of a 3% discount. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • The January 4 deadline. Honda’s 3.49–3.99% financing, the leases and the $750 owner offer all end January 4, 2027.
  • A scarce EX-L. Edmunds says EX-L Hybrid stock is tight. The Sport-L Hybrid has the same leather and power seats for $400 more, so don’t pay over sticker.
  • Honda financing. The low rates and the $500 offers need Honda Financial Services. Interest still builds during “No payments for 90 days.” Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • Recalls on a used one. Ask for proof that fuel pump recall 24V763 is done on a 2023 or 2024, and software recall 25V785 on a 2023 to 2025 Hybrid.

Is the Honda Accord a good car?

Most reviewers rank it at or near the top of its class. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.5, first of four midsize cars. Edmunds rates it 8.3, second of six midsize sedans behind the Toyota Camry. NHTSA gives it five stars in every test.

How much under MSRP can you get a Honda Accord?

On an LX, SE or Sport Hybrid, 2–3% under MSRP is fair and 4–5% strong. On an EX-L or Sport-L Hybrid, 3–4% is fair, and on a Touring Hybrid, 4–5%. That’s our read of TrueCar, Edmunds and CarWhere figures. Honda adds no cash back.

Is the Accord Hybrid worth it?

Yes, for most buyers. An EX-L Hybrid costs $4,400 more than a gas SE and is rated 48 mpg combined against 31, about 135 fewer gallons a year at 12,000 miles. It loses value a little faster: 22.1% in three years by iSeeCars’ count.

Is there a 2027 Honda Accord?

Not in the U.S. yet. On November 17, 2026, Honda listed only the 2026 and hadn’t announced a 2027 price. Edmunds lists a 2027 as upcoming. The 2026 is the one to price now.

What is the destination charge on an Accord?

$1,295 on every 2026 Accord. It was $1,195 when the 2026 launched, which is why some sites still show a $29,590 starting price. With today’s charge an LX is $29,690.

Does Honda offer cash back on the Accord?

No. On November 17, 2026, Honda offered 3.49–3.99% financing and leases on the 2026 but no customer cash. Honda owners get $750. Military buyers and recent graduates get $500 through Honda Financial Services.

Should I buy an Accord or a Toyota Camry?

Price both. The Camry is hybrid-only, starts at $29,600, offers all-wheel drive and is an IIHS Top Safety Pick+, and Edmunds rates it the better car. The Accord has the bigger discounts and a gas version from $28,395. See our Camry price guide.

Is a used Accord a better deal than new?

Rarely. A used 2025 asks a median $28,124 at about 17,700 miles, while Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 LX is $29,018 with destination. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Honda’s 3.99%. A HondaTrue Certified 2023 to 2025 is the used route we’d take.
