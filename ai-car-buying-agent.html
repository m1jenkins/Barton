Getting started

What you bring to the brief.

A clear brief helps returned options stay focused on the things that will actually affect your decision.

  • ✓Vehicle type, make or model preferences, and must-have features.
  • ✓Budget, target location, timing, and tolerance for new, used, or certified inventory.
  • ✓Relevant financing, trade-in, delivery, or dealer constraints.
  • ✓Deal-breakers that should shape the returned options.

You stay in the driver’s seat.

You decide whether to buy, finance, trade, insure, or sign. You also remain responsible for verifying vehicle condition and history, financing and insurance terms, taxes, registration, warranties, seller representations, and final contract language.

Drive Right does not promise a particular vehicle, discount, loan approval, interest rate, delivery date, or purchase outcome. Inventory, pricing, incentives, and dealer responses can change before you act.

Questions, answered.

Does the AI agent purchase the car for me?

No. The service can help research, communicate, and organize terms, but you decide whether to proceed and remain responsible for the purchase and final paperwork.

Can it contact every dealer?

No outcome is guaranteed. Outreach depends on the participating dealer, available contact channels, inventory, timing, and the approved service scope.

What should I do before paying?

Read the current pricing card, confirm the scope and availability, and make sure the service matches the decisions you want help organizing.
