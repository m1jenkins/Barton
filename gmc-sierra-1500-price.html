The trim we’d buy

A 2026 SLT crew cab with the 5.3L V8, with 4WD if you need it. At $55,400 it starts $4,600 above an Elevation, but the Elevation starts as a double cab with the 2.7-liter turbo four. The SLT comes as a crew cab with the V8, a 10-speed automatic, leather and 10-way power front seats. It’s also gone for 2027, along with the SLE, and TrueCar’s averages show SLTs selling about 7.5% under MSRP, with the Denali the deepest discount of any trim.

If you tow often or drive long highway miles, get the 3.0-liter Duramax diesel. It’s rated 24 mpg combined with 4WD against 16 for the 5.3L, and it’s the only engine GMC puts money on this month: $8,250 cash, or 0% for 60 months plus $2,000. Skip the 6.2L V8 for now. NHTSA has an open investigation into failures of GM’s 6.2-liter that covers 2021 to 2026 trucks, including ones already fixed under a 2025 recall. The Denali is the step up for a nicer cabin; the AT4X is for serious off-roading and costs $24,500 more than an SLT.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Which engine. GMC’s cash and 0% apply only to the 3.0L Duramax. Make sure a quote names the engine before you compare it with an ad.
  • Cash or 0%. On the diesel you get one or the other. Work out the total cost of each with your bank’s rate before you choose.
  • Dealer add-ons. GMC dealers put add-ons on 36.4% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,441. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • Fees. Documentation fees run from about $85 in California to $999 in Florida, by CarEdge’s count. Compare out-the-door prices.
  • The 6.2L V8. On a 2021 to 2024 truck, ask for proof that recall 25V274’s inspection was done. NHTSA’s open investigation also covers 2025 and 2026 engines.
  • The transfer case recall. A few 2026 4WD trucks are under recall 26V289 and shouldn’t be driven until fixed. Have the dealer check the VIN before delivery.

Common questions

Is the GMC Sierra 1500 a good truck?

Reviewers split on it. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.0 out of 10, tied for third of six full-size pickups, and Kelley Blue Book gives it 4.3 out of 5. Edmunds is cooler, at 6.7 and fifth of large trucks, faulting the ride and fuel economy. Consumer Reports expects it to be less reliable than the average new car, and iSeeCars finds it loses 27.8% of its value in three years, against 23.7% for full-size pickups.

How much under MSRP can you get a GMC Sierra 1500?

On a 2026 SLE, Elevation or SLT, 5–7% under MSRP is a fair deal and 8–10% a strong one. Denali, AT4 and AT4X trucks sit at 5–7%, and the Pro has less room, about 2–3%. Those ranges are our read of TrueCar’s market averages and iSeeCars’ invoice figures, and GMC’s diesel cash comes on top.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Sierra 1500?

The 2026, unless you want the new truck. It costs less (the Denali starts $4,800 lower), the SLE and SLT exist only as 2026s, and GMC’s cash and 0% offers are on the 2026 diesel. The 2027 brings 5.7L and 6.6L V8s, a 10-speed automatic on every engine and a redesigned cabin, but GMC lists just one lease offer on it so far.

What is the destination charge on a Sierra 1500?

$2,795 on the 2026 and the 2027. GMC’s advertised starting prices leave it out.

Does GMC offer cash back on the Sierra 1500?

Only on the diesel. On November 16, 2026, GMC offered $8,250 ($6,250 plus $2,000 bonus cash) on a 2026 Sierra 1500 with the 3.0L Duramax, or 0% for 60 months plus $2,000, in Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York. Gas 2026 trucks got lease deals only. The offers end November 30.

Is the Sierra’s 6.2L V8 reliable?

It has a record. GM recalled nearly 600,000 2021 to 2024 trucks and SUVs with the 6.2L in 2025 for crankshaft and connecting-rod defects that can cause engine failure, and NHTSA has since opened a wider investigation covering 2021 to 2026 engines, including ones already fixed. Until that closes, we’d take the 5.3L V8 or the diesel.

Is the Sierra 1500 the same truck as the Silverado?

They’re closely related and offer the same four engines. The Sierra costs more (the 2026 Pro starts at $38,600, against $37,200 for a Silverado WT) and is finished a step higher inside. With the two this close, price both and let the better deal decide.
