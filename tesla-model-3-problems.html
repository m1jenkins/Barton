The best used Model 3s are the 2023, 2024 and 2025 model years, which draw 187, 158 and 75 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a Model 3 yardstick of 221. Skip the 2018 and the 2021. The 2018 draws 741 per 100,000 on 1,040 complaints, the most of any year, with the front suspension named in 194. The 2021 draws 559, with 411 of its 680 complaints naming automatic emergency braking. The 2019, 2020 and 2022 are the years to ask about, because NHTSA’s open evaluation PE26006 covers 2018 to 2020 Model 3s whose front lateral links can detach with no warning. The 2017 is too small to rate: Tesla began deliveries in July 2017, and no 2016 Model 3 was sold.

Tesla publishes no model-level U.S. sales, so the rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across both Model 3 listings, by Good Car Bad Car’s estimates, the method our Tesla Model Y vs Toyota RAV4 page uses. The 2021 estimate of 121,610 looks low next to the 2020’s 206,500, which inflates that year’s rate; its 680 complaints are third highest either way. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

Model 3 generations: what changed

Original Model 3 2017–2023

Tesla held its first delivery event on July 28, 2017. Trims came and went: a Mid Range from October 2018, Standard Range and Standard Range Plus from March 2019, and a rear-wheel-drive car from November 2021 to January 2024 beside the Long Range and Performance (Wikipedia).

Refreshed Model 3 2024–

Tesla announced the redesign on September 1, 2023, and U.S. deliveries began in January 2024 from Fremont. A cheaper Model 3 Standard arrived in October 2025, and turn signal stalks returned for the 2026 model year (Wikipedia).

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Model 3: 158 mentions in 135 threads, 75 positive and 67 negative, a net of +4 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Model 3 is 52 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/TeslaModel3 and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Tesla Model 3 price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners report high-mileage cars that run trouble-free, with slow battery loss, and describe the refreshed Model 3 as better built than earlier ones. What they report going wrong is worn control arms and suspension bushings, 12-volt battery faults, paint peeling on the rocker panels, and rattles, rust and build quality problems on early 2018 and 2019 cars. The 2018’s suspension complaints sit beside those reports.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Many high-mileage owners report trouble-free driving and slow battery loss
  • Drivetrain and battery hold up past 100,000 miles with little routine maintenance
  • Refreshed cars show better build quality than earlier ones

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report paint peeling on the rocker panels across several model years
  • Owners report worn control arms, suspension bushings and 12-volt battery faults
  • Owners report rattles, rust and build quality problems on early 2018 and 2019 cars

Other complaints that come up

  • Price and value. Owners report steep depreciation on both used and new cars
  • Noise and refinement. Owners report road noise, wind noise and rattles on pre-refresh cars
  • Dealers and markups. Owners report inconsistent service quality and slow repair appointments

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Front lateral link fasteners 2018–2021Recall 21V-835 and 23V-235. The fasteners may loosen and let the front lateral link separate from the subframe. The repair is a service visit and free.
  • Brake caliper bolts 2019–2021Recall 21V-387. Loose caliper bolts can let a caliper separate and contact the wheel rim. Tesla tightens or replaces them free.
  • False collision warning software 2017–2021Recall 21V-846. A software communication error in release 2021.36.5.2 may cause a false forward collision warning or unexpected automatic braking. Tesla’s fix was an over-the-air software update.
  • Rearview camera cable 2017–2020Recall 21V00D000. The camera cable harness can be damaged as the trunk lid opens and closes, which keeps the image from displaying. Tesla repairs it free.
  • Pyrotechnic battery disconnect 2023Recall 23V-434. The pyrotechnic battery disconnect may be defective on some 2023 Model 3s and Model Ys. Tesla replaces it free.
  • Power steering assist 2023Recall 25V-092. The power steering circuit board can overstress and cause a loss of assist; Tesla began issuing a software update in October 2023, and the recall followed in February 2025.
  • Battery pack contactors 2025Recall 25V-690. The contactors may fail and cause a loss of drive power on some 2025 Model 3s and 2026 Model Ys. The repair is a service visit and free.
  • Headlight low beams 2017–2023Recall 26V-507. The low beams may be too bright and exceed the maximum light output. Tesla filed the recall on August 5, 2026, and our Model 3 price guide says the repair was not yet available.
  • Rearview camera delay 2021–2023Recall 26V-283. Software release 2026.8.6 may delay the rearview image when the car is put in reverse. A software update fixes it.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Front lower lateral link detachment 2018–2020PE26006, open. NHTSA opened the evaluation on July 29, 2026 after 156 complaints of front lower lateral link failures on 2018 to 2020 Model 3s and 2021 to 2023 Model Ys. In most cases the owner had no warning, and the car may not be drivable afterward. NHTSA says the failures exceed the scope of recalls 21V-835 and 23V-235 and don’t seem tied to the production issue behind them.
  • Unexpected brake activation 2021–2022PE22002, closed. NHTSA opened the evaluation on February 16, 2022 on sudden slowing with adaptive cruise control, Autopilot or Full Self-Driving engaged, and closed it on June 29, 2026. It cited a low demonstrated hazard, no identified collisions, Tesla’s software updates and reports that fell to 45 in 2024, 19 in 2025 and 3 so far in 2026, without finding that no defect exists.
  • Loss of steering control 2023EA24001, closed. NHTSA upgraded its evaluation of lost power steering assist on 2023 Model 3s and Model Ys on February 2, 2024, and closed it on June 25, 2026 after Tesla’s recall 25V-092.
  • Sudden unintended acceleration 2013–2020DP20001, closed. NHTSA denied a petition on Model S, Model X and Model 3 acceleration in January 2021, saying that in every case with event data the crash came from pedal misapplication.

What to check before you buy a used Model 3

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Tesla dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The front suspension on a 2018 to 2021.Confirm 21V-835 and 23V-235 are closed, then have an independent shop check the lateral links and control arms. NHTSA’s open evaluation PE26006 covers failures beyond those recalls, and owners often get no warning.
  • Headlight and camera recalls on a 2017 to 2023.Recall 26V-507 covers low beams that are too bright and, per our price guide, had no repair yet; 21V00D000 (2017 to 2020) and 26V-283 (2021 to 2023) cover the rearview camera.
  • Power steering on a 2023.Confirm 25V-092 (steering assist) and 23V-434 (battery disconnect) are closed.
  • Battery contactors on a 2025.Ask for proof that 25V-690 was done; our price guide says it needs a service visit.
  • Battery and drive unit warranty.Our price guide puts the cover at 8 years or 120,000 miles on a Long Range or Performance and 8 years or 100,000 on a Standard or Standard Range Plus, from first delivery. Ask for the delivery date.
  • Wear parts on any used car.Reddit owners report worn control arms and bushings and 12-volt battery faults. Ask when the 12-volt battery was last replaced.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look over the suspension, brakes and body before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Model 3 has 19 NHTSA complaints and 1 recall so far, for seat back fasteners, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024 or 2025, the 2024 has 5 recalls and the 2025 3 to confirm, including battery contactor recall 25V-690 on the 2025.

Common questions

What is the worst Tesla Model 3 year?

The 2018, at 741 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold on 1,040 complaints, the most of any year. The 2021 is next at 559, on a sales estimate that looks low.

What is the best used Model 3 year?

The 2023, 2024 and 2025, at 187, 158 and 75 complaints per 100,000 sold. These rates rest on Good Car Bad Car’s estimates, since Tesla publishes no model-level U.S. sales.

Is there a 2016 Tesla Model 3?

No. Tesla held its first Model 3 delivery event on July 28, 2017, so the 2017 is the first model year, and it is too small to rate.

What is wrong with the Model 3 front suspension?

NHTSA’s open evaluation PE26006 has 156 complaints of front lower lateral links detaching on 2018 to 2020 Model 3s and 2021 to 2023 Model Ys, usually with no warning. It is separate from recalls 21V-835 and 23V-235, so a clean VIN lookup doesn’t rule it out.

How do I check a used Model 3 for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Tesla service center.
