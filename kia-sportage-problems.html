Sportage generations: what changed

Fourth generation 2017–2022

The QL Sportage launched as a 2017 in North America with a 181-hp 2.4-liter or a 240-hp 2.0-liter turbo, both with a six-speed automatic, and was facelifted for 2020 (Wikipedia). The 2016 on this page is the last year of the generation before it.

Fifth generation 2023–

The redesigned Sportage was revealed in October 2021 as a 2023, is built in West Point, Georgia, and added a hybrid at launch; it was facelifted in May 2025 (Wikipedia). Our Sportage price guide covers the current lineup.

Common Sportage problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 1,502 Sportage complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Engine, 2016 to 2020 2016–2020 model years523 complaints naming engine, 127 per 100,000 sold. The engine is the leading part on the 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020 (64, 230, 97, 41 and 91). In the text we read, oil is the word that comes up most, with knocking, stalling and fires named less often. Recall 18V-907 covers a fuel pipe on cars that already got a replacement engine under an earlier recall, but no recall covers the oil complaints, and NHTSA’s field doesn’t say what failed.
  • Brake control unit fire risk, 2014 to 2021 2016–2021 model years94 complaints naming brakes, 19 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 22V-051 (2014 to 2016) and 21V-137 (2017 to 2021) cover a hydraulic brake control unit that can short and start an engine compartment fire. Dealers install a fuse, free, and Kia advises owners to park outside until it’s done.
  • The 2023 redesign year 2023 model years49 complaints naming electrical system, 35 per 100,000 sold. The 2023 has 49 electrical complaints, and in the text we read many name the screen, a warning light or the instrument cluster. Recall 23V-298 covers instrument cluster software that can leave the display blank, and 22V-651 an alternator terminal nut that can loosen and stall the engine.
  • Brakes on the 2023 2023 model years35 complaints naming brakes, 25 per 100,000 sold. Recall 23V-410 covers a brake booster diaphragm that can misalign and cost power brake assist; dealers replace the booster as needed, free. The 2023 has 35 brake complaints.
  • Power steering, 2024 2024 model years5 complaints naming steering, 3.1 per 100,000 sold. Recall 24V-422 covers a power steering circuit board that can short and leave the car without assist. Dealers replace the electric power pack, free.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Sportage: 390 mentions in 389 threads, 147 positive and 210 negative, a net of −19 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Sportage is 58 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/KiaSportage and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Kia Sportage price guide has the full topic breakdown.

The Theta engine worries match the 2016 to 2020 engine counts in the table, and the owners who call recent years free of them agree with the lower 2021 and 2022 rates. The 2023 recall list rarely comes up in the threads.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report no problems past 50,000 miles, and the long warranty reassures them
  • Recent years are seen as free of the old Theta engine failures
  • Hybrid owners report trouble-free driving at moderate mileage

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report older Theta II and GDI engines failing or burning oil
  • Owners report the hybrid 12-volt battery draining after the car sits a few days
  • Owners report paint and piano-black trim discoloring or peeling on several model years

Other complaints that come up

  • Dealers and markups. Owners report dealers pushing add-ons like ceramic coating and etching at inflated prices

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Brake control unit short 2014–2021Recall 21V-137 and 22V-051. The hydraulic brake control unit can short and cause an engine compartment fire. Dealers install a fuse, free, and Kia tells owners to park outside until it is done.
  • Fuel pipe after an engine replacement 2016–2018Recall 18V-907. On cars that already had a replacement engine, the high-pressure fuel pipe may have been damaged or badly torqued, which can leak fuel. Dealers inspect and replace the pipe, free.
  • Power steering circuit board 2024Recall 24V-422. The circuit board can short and leave the car without power steering assist. Dealers replace the electric power pack, free.
  • Brake booster diaphragm 2023Recall 23V-410. The diaphragm can misalign and cause a vacuum leak that cuts power brake assist. Dealers replace the booster as needed, free.
  • Alternator terminal and curtain air bags 2023Recall 22V-651 and 22V-878. A loose alternator nut can stall the engine or cause a surge, and curtain air bags may have been twisted during installation. Dealers tighten the nut and reinstall the air bags, free.
  • Roof molding 2023–2024Recall 24V-025. The molding can loosen and detach and become a road hazard. Dealers replace or secure it, free.
  • Instrument cluster software 2023 and 2026Recall 23V-298 and 26V-046. A software error can leave the instrument panel blank. Dealers update it, and on the 2026 the fix is also delivered over the air, free.
  • Tow hitch harness module 2017–2022Recall 22V-703. Moisture and debris can short the module on cars fitted with the Kia harness and start a fire. Dealers replace a fuse or the module, free.

What to check before you buy a used Sportage

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Kia dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Brake control unit recall on a 2016 to 2021.Confirm 22V-051 or 21V-137 is closed. It is a fire recall, and the fix is a fuse.
  • The engine on a 2016 to 2020.Ask for service records showing regular oil changes, check the dipstick, and listen for knocking on a cold start. If the car got a replacement engine, confirm 18V-907 on a 2016 to 2018.
  • The 2023 recalls.Confirm 22V-651, 22V-878, 23V-298, 23V-410, 23V-531 and 24V-025 are closed; there are six on a 2023.
  • Steering on a 2024.Confirm 24V-422 (power steering circuit board) is done.
  • Hybrid battery on a Hybrid.Reddit owners report the 12-volt battery draining after a few days parked. Ask the seller whether it has been replaced and have a shop test it.
  • An independent inspection.Owners also report peeling paint and trim on several model years. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine, the battery and the paint before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Sportage has 48 NHTSA complaints and 1 recall so far, an instrument panel software fix, so it is too new to rate. The facelift that brought the 2026 is the same generation as the 2023 to 2025, whose record is mixed: a rough first year and two clean ones.

Common questions

What is the worst Kia Sportage year?

The 2017, at 555 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold, with 230 of its 404 complaints about the engine. The 2020 (206) and 2018 (200) follow.

What is the best used Sportage year?

The 2022 has the lowest rate, 38. The 2021, 2024 and 2025 are also Buy, at 61, 69 and 42. Confirm recall 21V-137 on the 2021 and 24V-422 on the 2024.

Is the 2023 Kia Sportage reliable?

It is the weakest year of the current generation: 151 complaints per 100,000 sold, 212 complaints and 7 recalls, among them the brake booster (23V-410) and curtain air bags (22V-878). Dealers fix them free, so we’d buy one only with proof they are done.

Does the Kia Sportage have engine problems?

On the 2016 to 2020 the engine is the most-named part, from 64 complaints on the 2016 to 230 on the 2017. Reddit owners report older Theta II and GDI engines failing or burning oil, and recent years are seen as free of that.

How do I check a used Sportage for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Kia dealer.
