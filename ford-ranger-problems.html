The best used Rangers are the 2020 to 2023 model years, at 130, 127, 68 and 59 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold, and the 2025 at 65. Skip the 2019, the first year back: it draws 304, led by transmission and drivetrain complaints (92 of 272), and its 11 recalls include shifter, blower motor and rear light faults. The redesigned 2024 is Check first at 214 and 13 recalls, and the 2025 and 2026 trucks carry engine recalls (26V-343, 26V-548) that can cut drive power.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints by U.S. Ranger sales. They compare years with each other; they don’t predict one truck. The Ranger returned to the U.S. for 2019, so the table starts there. The 2026 is too new to rate; our Ranger price guide covers what it costs new.

Ranger generations: what changed

The T6 Ranger returns 2019–2023

The T6 truck returned to the U.S. for 2019 with a 2.3-liter EcoBoost four-cylinder and a 10-speed automatic, built at Michigan Assembly, and the Tremor off-road trim arrived for 2021 (Wikipedia).

Second generation (P703) 2024–

Unveiled May 10, 2023 and engineered for the U.S. market. It has the 2.3-liter EcoBoost as the base engine, an optional 2.7-liter twin-turbo V6 on XLT and Lariat, a 10-speed automatic, and a Raptor with a 3.0-liter EcoBoost V6 (Wikipedia).

Power train is the top complaint part on every Ranger year from 2019 to 2025 (92 on the 2019, 60 of 120 on the 2021). NHTSA’s label spans the transmission, axles and driveline, so it can’t isolate the 10-speed. Recalls 19V-071, 19V-366 and 20V-197 cover shifter and shift cable faults on 2019–2020 trucks, and owners on Reddit say newer Rangers sorted out early 10-speed problems.
  • Electrical system 2019–2024 model years105 complaints naming electrical system, 25 per 100,000 sold. Electrical is second on the 2019 (41) and the 2024 (32). NHTSA’s label names a part, not a failure, and no recall on its list obviously matches the 2024 count. The 2025 instrument cluster recall (25V-399) and the 2026 camera screen recall (26V-605) are electrical.
  • Engine 2024–2026 model years23 complaints naming engine, 20 per 100,000 sold. Engine counts are small (10 on the 2024, 12 on the 2025), but two recalls cover engines that can fail and cut drive power: 26V-343 (2025–2026, new engine long block, owner letters mailed October 23, 2026) and 26V-548 (2026, piston domes, remedy expected December 2026). Owners on Reddit report timing chain trouble on the 2.7 EcoBoost and oil use on the 2.3, which NHTSA’s counts can’t confirm.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Ranger: 292 mentions in 166 threads, 158 positive and 95 negative, a net of +21 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Ranger is 54 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/FordRanger and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Ford Ranger price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit’s Ranger threads mix this page’s trucks with the older Ranger Ford stopped selling in the U.S. before 2019, which we don’t rate. For the trucks that are rated, owners say newer Rangers have sorted out the early 10-speed problems, which fits the 2019’s 272 complaints falling to 39 on the 2022 and 19 on the 2023. The newer-truck gripes are timing chain trouble on the 2.7 and oil use on the 2.3. Frame and leaf spring hanger rust and the old 4.0 V6’s timing chain guides are older-Ranger complaints that don’t apply to a 2019 or later.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Newer Rangers are said to have sorted out early 10-speed transmission problems
  • Older Ranger four-cylinder and 3.0 engines often reach 200,000 miles with basic care
  • Older Rangers are simple to work on, with cheap, plentiful parts

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report timing chain guide failures on the older 4.0 V6
  • Owners report frame and leaf spring hanger rust on older trucks in salt states
  • Owners report timing chain trouble on the 2.7 EcoBoost and oil use on the 2.3

Other complaints that come up

  • Space and practicality. Owners report the short five-foot bed limits hauling on the newer Ranger
  • Price and value. Owners report higher Ranger trims feel expensive for a midsize truck
  • Fuel economy. Owners report the older 4.0 V6 getting mid-teens mpg or worse

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Shifter leaves Park 2019Recall 19V-071. On some 10-speed trucks the shift lever can move out of Park without the key in the ignition and without the brake pedal pressed. Dealers inspect and repair the shifter interlock, free.
  • Shift cable clip 2020Recall 20V-197. A shift cable lock clip that isn’t fully seated can leave the transmission in a different gear than the shifter shows. Dealers correct the clip, free.
  • Rearview camera 2020Recall 20V-575. A camera circuit board fault can make the rearview camera work intermittently on 2020 Rangers and other Ford models. NHTSA’s recall query RQ21002 is still checking the recall’s timing and scope.
  • Front upper ball joint 2024–2025Recall 24V-770 and 25V-310. The front upper control arm ball joint fastener may be missing or loose, which can let the arm detach from the knuckle. Dealers inspect and tighten or replace the parts, free.
  • Side curtain air bags 2024–2026Recall 25V-541. The side curtain air bags may contact the B-pillar reinforcements and tear when they deploy. Dealers install protective shields, free.
  • Fuel pump bolts 2024Recall 25V-597. The bolts that secure the high-pressure fuel pump may be loose, which can leak fuel. Dealers replace the mounting bolts, free.
  • Engine failure 2025–2026Recall 26V-343. The engine may fail and cut drive power. Dealers replace the engine long block, free; owner letters were mailed October 23, 2026.
  • Piston domes 2026Recall 26V-548. Piston domes made incorrectly can fail and cut drive power. Dealers inspect and replace the long block if needed, free, once the remedy arrives, expected in December 2026.
  • Rearview camera screen 2026Recall 26V-605. On trucks with the 10.1-inch screen, the display can lose power and drop the rearview camera image. The remedy is still under development.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Rearview camera recall timing 2020RQ21002, open. NHTSA opened a recall query in August 2021 to check the timing and scope of Ford’s rearview camera recall 20V-575 and Ford’s reporting. It covers 2020 Rangers and other Ford models and is still open.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump failures 2024–2025PE24019, closed. NHTSA closed it on September 19, 2025 after Ford’s recall 25V-455 for 2021–2023 F-150s, Broncos and other models. It names 2024–2025 Rangers among vehicles with similar pumps that the recall leaves out, and says it will keep watching them.

What to check before you buy a used Ranger

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Ford dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The engine recalls on a 2025 or 2026.Ask for proof that 26V-343 was done (a new engine long block) and, on a 2026, that 26V-548’s remedy is scheduled; it isn’t expected until December 2026.
  • The shifter recalls on a 2019 or 2020.Confirm 19V-071, 19V-366 and 20V-197 are closed; they cover shifter and shift cable faults.
  • The rearview camera on a 2020 or 2026.A 2020 needs 20V-575 done, and a 2026 with the 10.1-inch screen has 26V-605 with no remedy yet. The VIN lookup shows both.
  • The front ball joint on a 2024 or 2025.Ask for proof of 24V-770 or 25V-310, and of 25V-597 on a 2024.
  • The passenger seat belt on a Super Cab.Recalls 19V-518 and 21V-811 cover a front passenger belt that may be assembled wrong on 2019–2021 Super Cabs.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s scan for transmission codes and look over the front suspension before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Ranger has 9 complaints so far and 8 recalls, including two engine recalls and a rearview camera recall whose remedy is still under development. We’d wait for those remedies before calling it better than a 2020 to 2023.

Common questions

What is the worst Ford Ranger year?

The 2019, the first year after Ford’s U.S. gap. It draws 304 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold against 130 for the 2020 and 59 for the 2023, and 11 recalls.

What is the best used Ranger year?

The 2020 to 2023 model years have the lowest rates, from 59 for the 2023 to 130 for the 2020, and the 2025 draws 65. The 2025 carries engine recall 26V-343, so confirm it was done.

Why does the table start at 2019?

Ford returned the Ranger to the U.S. for 2019, so earlier model years have no sales to divide by and no truck to rate.

Is the 2024 Ranger reliable?

It draws 214 complaints per 100,000 and 13 recalls, more than three times the 2023’s rate, and we rate it Check first. The recalls cover front ball joints, windows and fuel pump bolts, and each is free at a Ford dealer.

How do I check a used Ranger for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that truck; recall repairs are free at a Ford dealer.
