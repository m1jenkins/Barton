The best used Tacomas are the 2020 to 2023 model years, which draw 24 to 38 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold, and the 2025 at 44. Skip the 2016 to 2019 trucks (170, 124, 85 and 88), where transmission and drivetrain, fuel-system and brake complaints stack up beside recalls for the rear differential, crank position sensor, fuel pump and brake master cylinder, and skip the 2024 (82), the first year of the redesign, which has recalls for rear brake hoses and shock absorbers.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across the Tacoma and Tacoma Hybrid listings, by U.S. sales. They compare years with each other; they don’t predict one truck. The Tacoma’s yardstick is low, 38, because its 2020 to 2023 trucks draw so few complaints, so Avoid here means a bad year for a Tacoma. Tacoma generations: what changed

Third generation (N300) 2016–2023

Sold in the U.S. from September 2015 and built in Mexico and San Antonio. It came with a 2.7-liter four-cylinder (159 hp) or a 3.5-liter V6 (278 hp), a six-speed automatic or manual, and a 2020 facelift added new styling and wired Apple CarPlay and Android Auto (Wikipedia).

Fourth generation (N400) 2024–

Revealed May 19, 2023, with production starting in December 2023. It uses a 2.4-liter turbo four-cylinder (228 to 278 hp) with a six-speed manual or eight-speed automatic, plus an i-FORCE MAX hybrid rated at 326 hp (Wikipedia).

Common Tacoma problems by component

  • Transmission and drivetrain 2016–2024 model years374 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 18 per 100,000 sold. Power train leads the 2016 (136) and the 2024 (55). Recall 17V-285 (2016–2017) covers a rear differential oil leak, 24V-152 (2022–2023) rear axle shafts that can separate, and 25V-656 (2025) a front driveshaft joint that can break. No recall explains the 2024 count, and owners on Reddit worry the newer turbo four and its transmission are unproven.
  • Fuel and hybrid system 2018–2020 model years164 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 22 per 100,000 sold. Toyota recalled the fuel pump on 2018–2019 trucks (20V-012) and 2017–2020 trucks (20V-682); dealers replace the pump assembly, free. NHTSA’s label also catches the hybrid system on newer trucks.
  • Brakes 2018–2019 model years103 complaints naming brakes, 21 per 100,000 sold. Recall 18V-888 (2018–2019) covers improper machining of the brake master cylinder, which can damage a seal and affect braking; dealers replace it, free. The 2024 and 2025 4x4s have their own recall for rear brake hoses (25V-058).
  • Structure 2025 model years47 complaints naming body and structure, 17 per 100,000 sold. Structure is the top part on the 2025 with 47 of 122 complaints. NHTSA’s label doesn’t say which structural part, and no recall on its list explains the count.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Tacoma: 454 mentions in 311 threads, 245 positive and 164 negative, a net of +17 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Tacoma is 43 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/ToyotaTacoma and r/tacomaworld and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Toyota Tacoma price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Owners on Reddit report Tacomas passing 200,000 miles on routine maintenance and say the four-cylinder is simple and durable. Their worries are frame rust on older trucks in salt-belt states, the unproven turbo four and its transmission on the 2024 and 2025, and costly seals, bushings and suspension parts on high-mileage trucks. Rust is a long-term condition NHTSA’s complaint counts barely register, so the table can’t judge it; a frame inspection can.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report Tacomas passing 200,000 miles with only routine maintenance
  • Owners report 2024 and 2025 trucks trouble-free over their first miles
  • Four-cylinder is simple, durable and easy to maintain

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report frame rust on older trucks, especially in salt-belt states
  • Owners worry the newer turbo four and its transmission are unproven long term
  • Older high-mileage trucks need costly repairs for worn seals, bushings and suspension parts

  • Dealers and markups. Dealer service quotes for fluids and brakes run high against independent shops
  • Space and practicality. Rear seat is tight for adults and car seats
  • Fuel economy. Owners report mediocre real-world fuel economy, often around 19 mpg

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Rear differential oil leak 2016–2017Recall 17V-285. Oil can leak where the rear differential carrier meets the axle housing. Dealers inspect it, tighten or replace the fastening nuts and gasket, and replace the carrier if parts are damaged, free.
  • Crank position sensor 2016–2017 V6Recall 17V-356. A crank position sensor may malfunction and stall the six-cylinder engine. Dealers replace it with an improved design, free.
  • Brake master cylinder 2018–2019Recall 18V-888. Improper machining can damage a seal and affect brake performance. Dealers replace the master cylinder, free.
  • Fuel pump 2017–2020Recall 20V-012 and 20V-682. Two campaigns cover the fuel pump on certain Tacomas. Dealers replace the fuel pump assembly with an improved one, free.
  • Rear axle shafts 2022–2023Recall 24V-152. Debris can leave axle fasteners improperly secured, so a shaft may separate from the axle housing. Dealers inspect, tighten the retaining nuts and replace damaged parts, free.
  • Steering column spiral cable 2023Recall 23V-480. A weak weld in the spiral cable assembly can disconnect and deactivate the driver air bag. Dealers inspect and replace the assembly if necessary, free.
  • Rear brake hoses 2024–2025 4x4Recall 25V-058. Mud and dirt packed inside the rear wheels can damage the brake hoses and leak brake fluid. Dealers replace both rear hoses, free.
  • Shock absorber flanges 2024–2025Recall 26V-514. The metal flange on the front and rear shock absorbers may corrode and let the oil reservoir detach. Dealers inspect and replace the shocks as necessary, free; Toyota expects the final remedy in March 2027.

What to check before you buy a used Tacoma

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Toyota dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The fuel pump on a 2017 to 2020.Confirm 20V-012 or 20V-682 was done. The VIN lookup lists what is open; the dealer’s service record shows what was replaced.
  • The differential and crank sensor on a 2016 or 2017.Ask for proof of 17V-285 (rear differential oil leak) and, on a V6, 17V-356 (crank position sensor).
  • The master cylinder on a 2018 or 2019.Recall 18V-888 replaces a master cylinder with a seal that can fail; confirm it is closed.
  • The rear axle on a 2022 or 2023.Confirm 24V-152 (axle shafts) and 22V-520 (child seat anchor welds, 2022–2023) are done.
  • Hoses, shocks and driveshaft on a 2024 or 2025.Ask which of 25V-058, 26V-514 and 25V-656 apply and are closed; the shock recall’s final remedy isn’t expected until March 2027.
  • The frame on an older truck.Owners on Reddit report frame rust on older Tacomas, especially in salt-belt states. Have a shop look at the frame and underbody before you sign.

Common questions

What are the worst Toyota Tacoma years?

The 2016 to 2019 model years and the 2024. They draw 170, 124, 85, 88 and 82 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold against 24 for the 2023, with recalls for the rear differential, crank sensor, fuel pump, master cylinder and, on the 2024, brake hoses and shocks.

What is the best used Tacoma year?

The 2020 to 2023 model years have the lowest rates, from 24 for the 2023 to 38 for the 2020, and the 2025 draws 44. The 2025 shares its recalls with the 2024.

Is the 2024 Tacoma reliable?

It draws 82 complaints per 100,000, nearly double the 2025’s 44, and 6 recalls. We rate it Avoid on the numbers, and we’d want 25V-058 and 26V-514 shown as handled on any 2024 we considered.

Do Tacoma hybrids have more problems?

NHTSA counts the Tacoma and Tacoma Hybrid listings together here, so these figures can’t split them. The 2024 and 2025 shock recall and the 2025 driveshaft recall cover hybrid and gas trucks.

How do I check a used Tacoma for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that truck; recall repairs are free at a Toyota dealer.
