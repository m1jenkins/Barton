About Drive Right

Car-buying support.
Focused on your decision.

Drive Right was built to help buyers organize vehicle research, compare written terms, and communicate with sellers.

M

Hi, I'm Mason.

Founder & Lead Advisor

As a lifelong car enthusiast, I've spent years researching, purchasing, owning, and selling vehicles across new, used, certified pre-owned, and enthusiast markets.

That experience led me to help friends, family members, and coworkers compare vehicles and deal terms, and eventually to start Drive Right Car Buying.

Buying a car is often the second-largest purchase most people will ever make, yet it can also be one of the most stressful. What car is right for you? Should you lease, finance, or pay cash? What's a fair price? Are those dealer add-ons and packages actually worth it?

There's a reason so many people dread the car-buying process. Dealers do this every day, and most buyers walk in unprepared. That can lead to wasted time, unnecessary stress, and paying more than you should.

That's where Drive Right comes in.

I help organize the car-buying process. Whether you need help choosing a vehicle, comparing options, reviewing pricing, or communicating with sellers, I'm here to guide you through the scope you select.

Maybe you're shopping for a new minivan and can't decide between a Toyota Sienna and a Honda Odyssey. Maybe you're looking for a midsize crossover and feel overwhelmed by the dozens of options available. Or maybe you already know exactly what you want and just need someone to help make sure you get the right deal.

Whatever stage you're in, Drive Right can help you compare the available options and understand the tradeoffs before you decide.

A vehicle purchase can combine product research, seller communication, pricing, financing, trade-in, optional products, and contracts. Drive Right helps organize those inputs so buyers can compare the documented options before deciding.
