Hi, I'm Mason.

As a lifelong car enthusiast, I’ve owned more than my fair share of vehicles — over 60 cars and counting. From new and used cars to certified pre-owned vehicles and even classics, I’ve bought, owned, negotiated, and sold just about every type of car.

Through that experience, I’ve become an expert in car buying and negotiation. Over the years, I’ve helped friends, family members, and coworkers find the right vehicles, avoid bad deals, and confidently purchase cars they love. That passion eventually led me to start Drive Right Car Buying.

Buying a car is often the second-largest purchase most people will ever make, yet it can also be one of the most stressful. What car is right for you? Should you lease, finance, or pay cash? What’s a fair price? Are those dealer add-ons and packages actually worth it?

There’s a reason so many people dread the car-buying process. Dealers do this every day, and most buyers walk in unprepared. That can lead to wasted time, unnecessary stress, and paying more than you should.

That’s where Drive Right comes in.

I help take the pressure and confusion out of buying a car. Whether you need help choosing the right vehicle, comparing options, reviewing pricing, or negotiating the best possible deal, I’m here to guide you through the process from start to finish.

Maybe you’re shopping for a new minivan and can’t decide between a Toyota Sienna and a Honda Odyssey. Maybe you’re looking for a midsize crossover and feel overwhelmed by the dozens of options available. Or maybe you already know exactly what you want and just need someone to help make sure you get the right deal.

Whatever stage you’re in, I can help you Drive Right — saving you time, stress, and potentially thousands of dollars on your next car.

Dealerships employ trained professionals whose entire job is maximizing what you pay. Most buyers walk in once every few years, unprepared and unfamiliar with the process. We're changing that equation, bringing expertise, data, and negotiating discipline to your side of the table.
