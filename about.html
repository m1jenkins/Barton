Hi, I'm Mason.

I’m a car enthusiast. I’ve researched, bought, owned and sold new, used, certified pre-owned and enthusiast cars.

Friends, family and coworkers started asking me to handle their deals. Eventually that became Steer Me Right.

Buying a car means picking the model, chasing dealers, comparing fees, sorting out financing and a trade-in, and reading the paperwork. Most people do it every few years. The salesperson does it every day.

I take that work off your plate. Your plan sets how much of it I handle, and every decision about the purchase stays with you.

Maybe you’re stuck between a Toyota Sienna and a Honda Odyssey. Or you already know the car and just want someone to get the price down.

How we work.

  • Prices and fees in writing

    We get every quote in writing and show you our fee before you pay. Dealers pay us nothing.

  • Offers compared

    We line up the listings and offers against the market and point out anything that doesn’t add up.

  • Dealer calls handled

    We do the research and the dealer calls your plan covers. You decide whether to buy.
