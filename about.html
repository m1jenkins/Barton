Based in Austin, Drive Right is a car buying and negotiation service for buyers nationwide. We organize vehicle research, compare written terms, and communicate with participating sellers remotely. See what the service covers or compare $295 Full Service and $895 Ultimate Concierge.

Hi, I'm Mason.

Founder & Lead Advisor

As a car enthusiast, I research, purchase, own, and sell vehicles across new, used, certified pre-owned, and enthusiast markets.

That experience led me to help friends, family members, and coworkers compare vehicles and deal terms, and eventually to start Drive Right Car Buying.

A vehicle purchase can bring together model research, seller communication, price and fee comparison, financing, trade-in questions, optional products, and final paperwork. Buyers may need to decide what car fits, which written terms are comparable, and which questions still need answers.

Drive Right gives that work a structure. The selected plan defines which research, negotiation, fee review, and coordination tasks an advisor can handle while the buyer keeps every purchase decision.

I can help with choosing a vehicle, comparing options, reviewing pricing, or communicating with sellers within the plan you select.

Maybe you're shopping for a new minivan and can't decide between a Toyota Sienna and a Honda Odyssey. Maybe you're looking for a midsize crossover and feel overwhelmed by the dozens of options available. Or maybe you already know the car and want help comparing the written terms.

How we work.

  • Quoted prices and fees

    We compare available listings, quoted terms, and relevant market information, then flag assumptions and items that need clarification.

    We can take on the research and seller-communication work included in your selected plan while you retain every purchase decision.
