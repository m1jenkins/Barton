The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The Tucson gives you the most equipment and the longest warranty for the money, and the hybrid is the one to buy. U.S. News ranks it first among compact SUVs, tied with the Mazda CX-5. The gas engine is slow, and an all-new Tucson arrives in early 2027. Price a 2026 Hybrid SEL Convenience and aim for 3–4% under MSRP.

  • Interior and tech. Large screens, head-up display and 360 camera feel modern and easy to use

How we set these discount targets

Tucsons sell under sticker, and there’s plenty to choose from. Edmunds counted about 40,800 new Tucsons nationwide and says they sell in about 25 days, against 34 for the market. Its Texas suggested prices run 5.7–7.5% under sticker with destination (a $29,201 SE against $31,350; a $32,047 Hybrid SEL against $34,400), though its November tips say about $1,200 off MSRP on average, with no general customer rebates. TrueCar’s page shows buyers paying 1.9% less than MSRP across the Tucson line (our read of TrueCar was blocked, so that figure is from a search-result summary), and iSeeCars puts dealer invoice 4.0% under MSRP on the gas SE through SEL Premium, 5.0% on the Limited and 3.4% on every hybrid. CarWhere has too few verified 2026 Tucson purchases to publish a typical discount.

Best when: You want the redesign and can wait until early 2027. Hyundai calls it all-new, with a next-generation hybrid system.

Check: That price and mpg are published before you commit; Hyundai had posted neither on November 18, 2026.

CPO (Hyundai Certified Used Vehicles)

Best when: You want a 2021 to 2025 with a warranty. Hyundai’s 5.99% for up to 60 months applies to select cars.

Check: That it’s Certified (a 173-point inspection, a 5-year/60,000-mile comprehensive warranty, a 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain and hybrid battery warranty), not the cheaper Select Used program.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $22,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2019 to 2021 that recall 20V543 was done.

Buying used? Look at a 2023 or 2024. Skip the 2019 to 2021 unless recall 20V543 was done: an ABS control unit can short and start an engine-compartment fire. The 2025 has seven recalls. Check any used Tucson’s open recalls by VIN.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Open recalls. NHTSA lists six on the 2026, including 25V549 (connecting rod bolts; Hyundai replaces the engine) and 26V316 (sudden braking from camera software). Ask for the VIN’s recall status.
  • The bonus cash needs Hyundai financing. On the gas SEL Premium and Limited, it requires Hyundai Motor Finance and goes toward the down payment. Get your bank’s rate first.
  • All-wheel drive on the hybrid. The SEL Convenience, Limited and Night come with it and are rated 36 mpg combined. A front-drive Hybrid SE or SEL is rated 38.
  • A slow gas engine. Edmunds timed an AWD gas Limited at 10.2 seconds to 60 mph. The hybrid’s extra power is the main reason to pay for it.
  • Dealer add-ons and fees. They come on top of the advertised price. Ask for an out-the-door quote and refuse or negotiate any add-on you didn’t pick.

Is the Hyundai Tucson a good car?

Yes. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.3 out of 10, first in compact SUVs and tied with the Mazda CX-5. Edmunds scores it 7.9 and faults a slow gas engine and a screen-only control layout. NHTSA gives it five stars overall.

How much under MSRP can you get a Hyundai Tucson?

On a gas SE through SEL Premium, 3–4% under MSRP is a fair deal and 5–6% a strong one, in our estimate. A Limited has more room (4–5% fair). A Hybrid Limited or Night has less (2–3%). Hyundai’s bonus cash and financing come on top.

Should I buy the gas Tucson or the Hybrid?

The Hybrid, for most buyers. Edmunds says it’s quicker and gets about 10 more mpg. A Hybrid SEL Convenience is rated 36 mpg combined against 26 for an AWD gas SEL Premium and costs $300 less ($35,250 against $35,550).

Does Hyundai offer cash back on the Tucson?

Yes, as bonus cash through November 30, 2026: up to $3,000 on the gas Tucson, $1,750 on the Hybrid and $4,000 on the Plug-in. On the gas SEL Premium and Limited it requires Hyundai Motor Finance and goes toward the down payment.

Is a new Hyundai Tucson coming?

Yes. Hyundai’s site lists an all-new Tucson and Tucson Hybrid for early 2027, with no U.S. price or mpg yet. The 2026 is the last of the current generation, introduced for 2022 and refreshed for 2025.

What is the destination charge on a Tucson?

$1,650, per iSeeCars. That matches Hyundai’s own fine print on a Hybrid Night ($44,575 including destination against a $42,925 sticker). Hyundai’s MSRPs leave it out.

Is AWD worth it on a Tucson?

If you see snow. It adds $1,500 on the gas Tucson and the Hybrid SE and SEL, and costs about 2 mpg combined. The Hybrid SEL Convenience, Limited, Night and both plug-ins come with it.

Is a used Tucson a better deal than new?

Rarely. A used 2025 asks a median $24,493, while Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 SE is $29,201. Hyundai finances the new one at 0% or 2.9% against 11.19% on average for used loans. A used 2023 or 2024 is the value play.
