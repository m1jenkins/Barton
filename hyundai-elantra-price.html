The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The Elantra is the value play against the Civic and Corolla: it starts $1,870 below a Civic LX, the Hybrid Blue is rated 54 mpg combined, and it sells about 6% under sticker. The base engine is slow and the cabin is loud at speed. A redesign is due in 2027, so the 2026 is the last of this design.

  • 54 mpg combined as a Hybrid Blue; 35 for the gas SE
  • 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty
  • IIHS Top Safety Pick and five stars from NHTSA
  • About 6% under sticker, plus $2,000 Hyundai cash on gas trims

  • Base engine takes 9.4 seconds to 60 mph, Edmunds found
  • Wind noise on the highway

New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. It gets the $2,000 bonus cash, 3.49% financing and the 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty.

Check: That Hyundai’s cash and rate cover the trim you want (the Hybrid and N get less), and that recalls 26V692000 (front struts) and 26V693000 (wheel bearings) are done.

Wait for the next-generation Elantra

Best when: You don’t need a car before 2027 and want the new design, a larger screen and the redesigned hybrid.

Check: That Hyundai hasn’t announced U.S. prices or an on-sale date, and that a new design usually costs more than a closeout.

CPO (Hyundai Certified Used)

Best when: You want a 2021 to 2026 with a warranty. Hyundai’s 5.99% helps.

Check: That it’s Certified Used, not Select Used. Certified adds a 173-point inspection, 5 years/60,000 miles of comprehensive cover and a transferable 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty from first sale; Select Used gives only 90 days or 5,000 miles of powertrain cover.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $17,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and the engine on a 2019 or 2020 (the top complaint category on both).

Buying used? Look at a 2023 or newer. The 2019 and 2020 drew 253 and 255 NHTSA owner complaints, with engine problems leading the list, against 118 for the 2023. Check any used Elantra’s open recalls by VIN.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. CarWhere found them on 34.1% of the quotes it studied, averaging $1,454. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products: refuse or negotiate them.
  • The wheel and strut recalls. NHTSA recalls 26V693000 (lug nuts that may loosen) and 26V692000 (front strut fasteners) cover some 2026 Elantras. Ask the dealer to show both are done.
  • Which trims the cash covers. The $2,000 is for the gas Elantra. The Hybrid gets $500 and the N none.
  • Hyundai financing. The college rebate needs Hyundai Motor Finance, and in Dallas so does the $2,000. Compare the total with your bank’s rate.
  • Wireless CarPlay. It comes only with the 8-inch screen. The 10.25-inch screen needs a cable, Edmunds found.

Is the Hyundai Elantra a good car?

For value, yes. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.0 out of 10 and Kelley Blue Book 4.6 out of 5. Edmunds gives the gas car 6.8, finding the base engine slow and the cabin loud, and the Hybrid 7.6.

How much under MSRP can you get a Hyundai Elantra?

On a gas SE through Limited, 3–4% under MSRP is a fair deal and 5–6% a strong one. That’s our read of Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book and iSeeCars figures, so a dealer may land a point either side. Hyundai’s $2,000 cash comes on top.

Is the Elantra Hybrid worth the extra money?

If you drive a lot. A Hybrid Blue costs $1,700 more than a gas SEL Sport and is rated 54 mpg combined against 34, about 130 fewer gallons a year at 12,000 miles. This month the gas car gets the bigger offer.

Does the Hyundai Elantra have a redesign coming?

Yes. Hyundai showed the next Elantra in South Korea in June 2026, and it’s expected in U.S. showrooms in 2027. There are no U.S. prices yet, so the 2026 is the current car.

What is the destination charge on an Elantra?

$1,295 on the 2026. Hyundai’s advertised starting prices leave it out: the SE’s $22,825 is $24,120 with destination.

Does Hyundai offer cash back on the Elantra?

On the gas car, yes: $2,000 bonus cash through November 30, checked November 18, 2026. The Hybrid gets $500 and the N none. Military buyers and recent graduates get $500.

Is the Elantra N worth buying?

Edmunds ranks it first of four sport compacts, and it starts at $35,300, less than a GR Corolla ($40,520) or Civic Type R ($47,395). See the Elantra N section above.

Is a used Elantra a better deal than new?

Rarely this month. A used 2025 asks a median $21,235, and Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 SE is $22,700 with destination, before $2,000 of Hyundai cash. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Hyundai’s 3.49%.
