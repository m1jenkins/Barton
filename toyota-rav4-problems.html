The best used RAV4s are the 2022 to 2025 model years, which draw 45, 55, 44 and 21 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold. Skip the 2019, 2020 and 2021: they draw 203, 152 and 125, led by fuel-system, engine, steering and air bag complaints, and Toyota recalled them for engine casting cracks, lost steering assist, cracked front control arms and air bag faults. A finished recall doesn’t change our rating of a year. The 2016 to 2018 RAV4s sit in the middle.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across every RAV4 listing (gas, Hybrid and Prime or Plug-in Hybrid), by U.S. sales. They compare years with each other; they don’t predict one car. The 2026 redesign is too new to rate; our RAV4 price guide covers what it costs new.

Complaints are owner reports to NHTSA, counted once each across every Toyota RAV4 listing (all the trim and body-style listings) and read on November 21, 2026. Each rate divides a model year’s complaints by the U.S. sales of the same calendar year. Model years and calendar years don’t line up, since a model year’s cars sell across two calendar years, so the rate compares years with each other. Sales come from Toyota’s year-end releases for 2019 to 2025 and Good Car Bad Car for 2016 to 2018. Our verdicts use this car’s own yardstick, the median of 55 complaints per 100,000 across its 2019 to 2025 model years: Buy up to 1.25 times it, Check first up to twice it, Avoid above that. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

RAV4 generations: what changed

Fourth generation 2013–2018

The 2016 refresh added LED lamps and a color display, and the RAV4 Hybrid debuted with it (Wikipedia). The 2016 to 2018 years on this page are the last of that design, with the 2.5-liter four-cylinder and six-speed automatic as the core powertrain.

Fifth generation 2019–2025

It went on sale in December 2018 with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder and an eight-speed automatic; the Hybrid, with standard all-wheel drive, followed in March 2019 (Wikipedia). The RAV4 Prime plug-in arrived for 2021 and was renamed Plug-in Hybrid for 2025. A refresh for 2022 brought new headlights and USB-C ports, the Woodland trim arrived for 2023, and the Adventure and TRD Off-Road trims were dropped for 2025.

Sixth generation 2026–

Toyota sells the redesigned 2026 only as a hybrid or plug-in hybrid, so the gas engine that carried the 2019 to 2025 cars is gone (Wikipedia; Toyota’s 2026 RAV4 page). Its record is too short to compare with the figures above.

Common RAV4 problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 3,808 RAV4 complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Electrical system833
  • Engine671
  • Fuel and hybrid system492
  • Air bags353
  • Body and structure299
  • Brakes291
  • Transmission and drivetrain259
  • Steering204
  • Fuel and hybrid system 2019–2021 model years418 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 32 per 100,000 sold. CarComplaints reports that some hybrids’ fuel tanks won’t fill to the rated 14.5 gallons, and that a settlement extended that coverage to 15 years or 150,000 miles. NHTSA’s fuel label also catches complaints about gas models, so this count is wider than that one issue.
  • Engine 2019–2020 model years413 complaints naming engine, 47 per 100,000 sold. Recall 20V-064 covers porosity in 2.5-liter engine castings that can crack and leak coolant. The fix is free and can mean a replacement engine. Ask for proof it was done on any 2019 or 2020.
  • Air bags 2020–2021 model years207 complaints naming air bags, 25 per 100,000 sold. Two recalls: 20V-734 (steering column breakaway pins that can affect the driver air bag) and 23V-865 (a short circuit in the passenger occupant sensor that can stop the passenger air bag firing). Both are free; a VIN lookup shows whether they are open.
  • Electrical system 2016–2018 model years301 complaints naming electrical system, 25 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s complaint field names the part, not the failure. For 2016 to 2018, one recall (23V-734) covers replacement 12-volt batteries that don’t fit the tray, and NHTSA’s closed investigation PE21005 traced battery-area fires on 2013 to 2018 RAV4s to that fit.
  • Steering 2022–2025 model years82 complaints naming steering, 4.6 per 100,000 sold. The counts are small beside 2019 and 2020. Recall 20V-373 (water in the steering gearbox, lost assist) covers only 2019 and 2020, so nothing in NHTSA’s recall list explains these; check the steering on any 2022 to 2025 you drive.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the RAV4: 774 mentions in 806 threads, 596 positive and 141 negative, a net of +56 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the RAV4 is 54 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/rav4 and r/RAV4Club and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Toyota RAV4 price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit’s reliability gripes are water leaking in from roof rails or clogged drains, a coolant valve message, and worry over salt on the hybrid’s exposed high-voltage cable. The threads rarely give a model year, so Reddit neither confirms nor contradicts the year-by-year table.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report 200,000 miles or more with only routine maintenance
  • Hybrid has fewer wear parts, with no starter or alternator to replace
  • Toyota durability is trusted enough that extended warranties seem unnecessary

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report water leaking into the cabin from roof rails or clogged drains
  • Owners report a coolant valve fault that triggers an engine maintenance message
  • Owners worry road salt corrodes the exposed hybrid high-voltage cable

Other complaints that come up

  • Dealers and markups. Dealer service quotes pad repairs with unrelated maintenance and upsells
  • Noise and refinement. Owners report loud road and cabin noise with rough refinement
  • Interior and tech. Interior feels plain and plasticky, cheap below the top trims

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Engine casting cracks 2019–2020Recall 20V-064. Porosity in 2.5-liter engine castings can crack and leak coolant, which can overheat the engine. Dealers inspect and replace the engine as needed, free.
  • Steering assist loss 2019–2020Recall 20V-373. Water can enter the electric steering gearbox cover and cut power assist. Dealers replace the gearbox, free.
  • Front control arm cracks 2019–2020Recall 20V-286. The front lower suspension arms may crack and separate from the wheel assembly. Dealers replace both arms, free.
  • Air bag recalls 2020–2021Recall 20V-734 and 23V-865. 20V-734 covers steering column breakaway pins that can affect the driver air bag, and 23V-865 a short in the passenger occupant sensor. Both fixes are free.
  • Plug-in charger fire risk 2021–2022 PrimeRecall 23V-478. A damaged DC-DC converter module can short and overheat. Until the dealer replaces it, Toyota tells owners not to charge below about 41 degrees Fahrenheit.
  • Brake caliper bolts 2024Recall 24V-911. Front caliper and wheel-bearing bolts may be loose, which can damage the brake hose or detach a wheel. Dealers inspect and tighten them, free.
  • Front seat brackets 2025Recall 26V-256. Seat rail brackets may be welded improperly. Dealers inspect and repair the welds, free.
  • Power loss at higher speeds 2026 Plug-in HybridRecall 26V-659. A software error can cut power or shut the plug-in system down at higher speeds. Dealers update the software, free; owner letters begin November 22, 2026.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

NHTSA’s public investigation list showed no open investigation naming the RAV4 on November 21, 2026. The last, PE21005 into battery-area fires on 2013 to 2018 RAV4s, closed in August 2024 after Toyota’s battery-tray recall 23V-734.

What to check before you buy a used RAV4

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Toyota dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Hybrid cables on 2019 to 2022 AWD hybrids.Toyota’s support program 22TE09 covers corroded high-voltage cables for 8 years or 100,000 miles. It isn’t a recall, so a VIN lookup won’t show it; ask for the service record.
  • The 2019 and 2020 recalls.Confirm 20V-064 (engine), 20V-373 (steering) and 20V-286 (control arms) are closed on the VIN. A dealer can print the history.
  • The fuel tank on a 2019 to 2021 hybrid.Owners report hybrids that stop filling well short of the rated 14.5 gallons. Ask what the seller’s last fill-up took.
  • A plug-in’s charger and software.On a 2021 or 2022 Prime, confirm the DC-DC converter recall (23V-478); on a 2026, the software recall 26V-659.
  • Damp carpet and headliner.Owners on Reddit report water leaking in from roof rails or clogged drains. Press the carpet under the seats and look for stains at the roof edges.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look over the brakes, steering and underbody before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

Toyota sells the 2026 only as a hybrid or plug-in hybrid, so the 2019 to 2025 gas engine’s record doesn’t carry over. With 56 complaints, 2 recalls and no full year on the road, it is too early to say it is more reliable; the plug-in needs its recall 26V-659 software update.

Common questions

What are the worst Toyota RAV4 years?

The 2019 and 2020 model years. They draw 203 and 152 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold against 45 to 21 for 2022 to 2025, and carry recalls for engine casting cracks, steering assist loss and front control arm cracks.

What is the best used RAV4 year?

The 2022 to 2025 model years have the lowest complaint rates, from 21 for the 2025 to 55 for the 2023. The 2025 is still collecting complaints, so its rate will rise a little.

Is the 2021 RAV4 reliable?

It draws 125 complaints per 100,000, and 147 of its 513 complaints name air bags. Two recalls cover those, but we rate the year on its complaints, so we’d skip it. If you buy one anyway, get both recalls shown as done.

Do RAV4 hybrids have more problems than the gas model?

NHTSA counts them together here, so these figures can’t split them. The hybrid-specific items are the 2019 to 2022 AWD cable program 22TE09 and the fuel tank fill complaints on 2019 to 2021 hybrids.

How do I check a used RAV4 for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car; recall repairs are free at a Toyota dealer.
