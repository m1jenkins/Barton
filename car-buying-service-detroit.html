The Detroit car market right now

The Census Bureau counted 263 new-car dealer locations across the Detroit metro area in 2023, an area of six Michigan counties. S&P Global Mobility ranked the Detroit market ninth in the country for new-vehicle registrations in the first half of 2025, although Detroit is only 14th in population. Its average monthly payment was $586, the only one under $600 among the ten markets with the most registrations, and its days’ supply was 61, the lowest of the ten.

Buying a car around Detroit

Ask where a used car spent its winters

Michigan’s environment agency says road salt is the most commonly used deicer and that it corrodes vehicles. A car that lived through Michigan winters takes that salt from below. Rust shows first on the brake lines, the exhaust and the rocker panels under the doors. On a used search we can ask the dealer for photos of the underside. Before you sign, have a mechanic you choose put the car on a lift.

Check what tires the car wears

Michigan State Police say all-season tires have limited ice and snow capability, and that winter tires grip better. A certified winter tire carries a mountain-and-snowflake symbol on the sidewall. Tell us if you want winter tires, and we ask each dealer what tires are on the car and for a photo of the sidewall, so you can compare cars on their tires as well as their price.

How it works from Detroit

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Detroit?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Detroit-area dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Can you tell if a used car has road-salt rust?

Only from photos. We can ask the dealer for pictures of the underside and send them to you. Photos miss things, so have a mechanic you choose put the car on a lift before you sign, especially on a car that spent its winters outside.

Can you find out what tires a car has?

Yes. We can ask the dealer what tires are on the car and for a photo of the sidewall, and a certified winter tire shows a mountain-and-snowflake symbol. Tell us whether you want winter tires, and we put the answer next to each dealer’s price.

Can you include dealers in Ohio?

Yes. For a Michigan buyer they are out-of-state dealers, which Steer Me Right Pro includes. If you live in Ohio, they are covered on either plan.
