How to Buy High-Demand Vehicles at MSRP

By Mason • Market Strategy

Some vehicles, including Toyota Sienna hybrids and certain SUV or performance trims, have limited supply. Buyers may face long waitlists and market adjustments of $5,000 or more. When supply is tight, compare dealers outside your immediate area and include the full written price in the comparison.

Paying above MSRP is not the only option. Compare dealer networks, shipping costs, and the written out-the-door price before deciding whether a distant vehicle is worth pursuing. That is the process we use at Drive Right for high-demand searches.

Compare dealers outside your area

Dealership markups are localized symptoms. If you live in a booming metropolitan area like Austin or Dallas, the local demand outstrips supply, enabling aggressive regional pricing. However, a dealership 500 miles away in a different zip code might have the exact same vehicle sitting quietly on their allocation sheet without a single markup installed.

We source from out-of-state dealers when the written offer still makes sense after travel or shipping. Flying out to sign paperwork and drive the car home, or paying about $800 for transport, can cost less than a local $5,000 markup. Compare both totals before choosing.

Compare similar vehicles

A specific model may not be the only vehicle that meets your needs. The Toyota Sienna is popular, but heavy markups can make a comparable minivan worth considering.

A Honda Odyssey offers similar utility, a standard V6 engine, and often broader availability. Comparing its written price with the Sienna's can avoid paying a premium for the more constrained model.

If you must wait for a specific vehicle: Contact several dealers, ask about allocation timing and deposits, and get the price and refund terms in writing before placing an order.

What the search requires

Hunting down phantom inventory, coordinating interstate transport logistics, and deciphering which dealerships actually sell at MSRP requires hundreds of hours of cold-calling and spreadsheet mapping. Most consumers simply do not have the time to execute this properly.
