The "Anti-Broker" Transparency Series

Why We Accept $0 in Kickbacks

By Mason • Trust & Transparency

A car broker's compensation can affect whose interests they serve. Before hiring one, ask whether the broker receives money from the dealer as well as from you.

Some brokers receive a finder's fee or other payment from the dealership after a transaction. If the dealer pays the broker, ask how that payment affects the broker's recommendations and negotiation.

When the dealer also pays the broker

In real estate, "dual agency", representing both the buyer and the seller in a single transaction, is heavily regulated, frowned upon, and strictly illegal in multiple states. That is because serving two opposing financial masters is inherently impossible.

In car sales, a $500 or $1,000 dealer payment can create a conflict. The broker may have an incentive to steer you toward a dealer that pays more instead of the dealer with the best written terms.

Ask how "100% free" services are paid: If an auto broker charges you nothing, ask whether dealers pay a referral fee and how that fee affects the service.

How Drive Right is paid

Our approach is simple. A consultant cannot be objective if their compensation is tied to the seller. Before taking our first client, we set the core rule for our car buying service: We accept exactly zero dollars from dealerships. No kickbacks, no referral fees, and no hidden payouts.

We keep our revenue separate from dealership profit. Clients pay us a flat fee, and we act on their behalf.

  • Dealer comparison: We can compare written offers from multiple dealers without a dealer commission determining the shortlist.
  • Negotiation: We can question price, add-ons, and financing without preserving a referral payment.
  • Disclosure: Clients pay us a flat fee, and we do not accept kickbacks or referral fees from dealerships.
"Trust is earned in drops and lost in buckets. If there is a hidden commission, there is no trust."

When you evaluate a broker, ask for the compensation structure in writing. At Drive Right, clients pay a flat fee and we do not accept dealership kickbacks or referral fees.
