# Drive Right editorial policy

Current website content is owner-approved as of October 1, 2026. See [the approval record](content-approval-2026-10-01.md). This filename is retained for existing references.

## Writing and page consistency

Use clear, useful language and distinguish general educational material from individual transaction advice. Keep prices, plan names, service scope, contact details, metadata, and structured data consistent with the public pages and the shared service/entity records. Public copy calls the intake a “car search” or “search details.”

Preserve source links, honest dates, author attribution, and any explanation of a hypothetical example. Do not invent credentials, customers, quotations, business locations, or results. Owner approval of existing website content does not supply new facts for later additions.

## Existing website content

Do not add pre-publication warnings, pending-review labels, or further content-verification requirements to existing website copy. Older approval fields and editorial task records do not override the October 1 owner instruction. Record owner approval as owner approval without attributing a third-party review.

## Implementation and corrections

Use the checks in `.github/workflows/checks.yml` for technical validation. Maintain visible-copy/schema parity, functional links, accessibility, content-hashed homepage assets, and generated-preview consistency. An edit to a pinned Texas page requires updating its recorded hash.

Record a concrete reported issue and its correction when one occurs. Existing robots directives, sitemap membership, redirects, and private-preview exclusions are separate implementation decisions; removing draft warnings does not change them.
