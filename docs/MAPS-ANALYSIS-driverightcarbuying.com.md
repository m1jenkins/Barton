## Observed website and page signals

The homepage and entity records identify Drive Right as based in Austin and use `(512) 910-4938` and `hello@driverightcarbuying.com`. The city collection reuses that identity and contact information. The September 18 revision uses the homepage template with one local sentence per city.

Each city has a `Service` area, not a separate `LocalBusiness`. There are no invented offices, street addresses, map pins, opening hours or local ratings. The coastal photograph is reused brand imagery, not purported photography of any city or completed customer transaction.

Google's profile guidance requires an accurate real-world business representation and distinguishes businesses customers can visit from those that travel to customers. A remote service-area page is not evidence of a new eligible physical branch. [Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).
