### Evidence needed to reach a decision

1. The owner confirms whether Drive Right staff actually meet customers in person as part of this service, where that occurs, how customers arrange it, and the real staffed customer-facing hours. Remote calls, email, or a listed city alone do not establish this.
2. If there is an existing Drive Right profile, provide its actual Maps/profile URL and access through the account that manages it. Inspect that profile before proposing field changes or considering a duplicate.
3. If customers visit a real location, establish that it genuinely receives customers and meets the applicable address/signage requirements. If staff travel to customers, establish the real base and in-person service area; protect a non-customer-facing residential address. Keep address evidence private.

If the service is exclusively online/remote, do not create a GBP based on its nationwide SEO positioning. If eligible in-person activity is substantiated, prepare one accurate business representation using the actual model; do not create profiles for remote target cities. These are conditional decision steps, not assertions about Drive Right's operations. Google's [business representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) govern the resulting address, service-area, category, hours, and identity decisions.
