## Prepared example

The private worksheet's first example compares these invented figures for otherwise comparable vehicles and terms:

| Entered item | Quote A | Quote B |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Vehicle selling price | $33,000 | $33,800 |
| Dealer fees | $500 | $500 |
| Selected products / add-ons | $1,500 | $0 |
| Quoted taxes — invented, not calculated | $2,000 | $2,000 |
| Title / registration | $250 | $250 |
| Delivery | $0 | $0 |
| Other quoted purchase costs | $0 | $0 |
| Incentives | None | None |
| Entered purchase total | **$37,250** | **$36,550** |

Formula: each column is the sum of its listed charges. `$37,250 − $36,550 = $700`; Quote B has the lower entered total despite its higher vehicle selling price. No financing, trade-in, down payment, service fee, or real customer's transaction is represented. Equal taxes are invented inputs, not a calculation rule. A public version must retain the hypothetical label beside the result.

The page/CSV implementation is bound in [SEO-06-review-packet.md](SEO-06-review-packet.md), and the first example is directly asserted by the automated tests. The intended lesson is to compare actual itemized offers before attributing value to a headline discount. Primary-source support is FTC written-price/itemization guidance linked in the review packet; the invented numbers have no external evidence claim.

## Evidence required to replace it with a real story

1. Two original written offers or a dated before/after offer for the same vehicle/specification, with taxes, fees, products, eligibility conditions, expiry, and seller identity documented privately.
2. Evidence of what the buyer actually accepted, paid, or declined; distinguish a proposed quote change from a completed purchase. Record cancellations or changed equipment that affect comparability.
3. A reproducible difference calculation with inclusions/exclusions, timeframe, any Drive Right fee, and limitations. A quote difference is not automatically a service-caused saving or a typical result.
4. The customer's original wording and explicit written permission for the proposed quotation, transaction details, name or anonymity choice, photos, channels, and duration; record any material connection. Do not expand a historical quote's permission by inference.
5. Owner factual attestation plus named claims and qualified financial/legal review of the exact proposed story, with approval/expiry dates and file/hash scope.

Keep originals in access-controlled storage and commit only redacted references. No customer has been contacted. Until evidence and exact-copy approval exist, use only the labeled worked example in the private worksheet; no customer name, invented attribution, new savings claim, or public case-study placement is authorized.
