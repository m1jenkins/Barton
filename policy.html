Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Drive Right ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address provided when you book a service, submit an inquiry, or hire our services.
  • Vehicle preferences: Details about the type of vehicle you're looking for, your budget, trade-in information, and related preferences you share with us.
  • Payment information: Billing details processed securely through our third-party payment processor. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, browser type, and referring URLs, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, personalize, and improve our car buying services.
  • Communicate with you about your vehicle search, agent activity, and service updates.
  • Process payments and send transaction confirmations.
  • Send occasional service-related emails (you can opt out at any time).
  • Analyze website usage to improve our site experience.

Information Sharing

We share information in the circumstances below, including optional advertising measurement when you allow it:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party tools we use to operate our business (e.g., payment processors, scheduling software, email services). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • OpenAI ad measurement: If you allow it through our Ad privacy controls, we share website activity and measurement signals with OpenAI as described below.
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (including Google Analytics and Google Ads) to understand how visitors use our website and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Optional OpenAI Ad Measurement

OpenAI ad measurement is off until you choose “Allow ad measurement.” When allowed, the OpenAI Pixel records page visits, successfully saved inquiries and checkout starts. It sends OpenAI measurement signals such as page URLs, browser and device information, event identifiers and advertising click references. It can store a click reference in a first-party cookie named __oppref to connect later activity with an ad.

The Pixel can also recognize supported contact information entered on the site and use automatic advanced matching. It normalizes and hashes that information in your browser before sending it; automatic advanced matching does not send raw contact details. Hashing does not make this data anonymous. We use measurement to understand advertising performance, and send our measured events with the option to exclude them from future user-level personalization. See OpenAI’s explanation of conversion measurement and its Privacy Policy.

You can decline and still use the site, submit an inquiry or purchase a service. Use “Ad privacy” in the footer to change your choice. We remember your preference in this browser for up to 180 days; if storage is unavailable, it applies only to the current page. Turning measurement off stops future measurement events from this integration; it does not retract events already sent. A Global Privacy Control signal keeps this integration off. These controls apply to OpenAI measurement; other tools may have separate settings.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
