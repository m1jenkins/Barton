What a car negotiation service does

You tell the negotiator the car. They find dealers that have it, contact them by phone and email, and ask each one for a written out-the-door price. Then they play the offers against each other, push back on the add-ons and fees that appear in the paperwork, and hand you the best written offer so you can decide. You never sit in the dealer’s office waiting for “the manager.”

At Steer Me Right, one advisor handles your search from the first call to the signed deal. We contact dealers that have your car in stock, negotiate for a price below MSRP on a new car or against comparable sales on a used one, check the fees line by line and send written recommendations: what we found and what we’d do.

Car buying agent, car buyer’s advocate, car buying consultant: the same job

People search for this help under different names. A car buying agent or car buyer’s agent usually means someone who represents the buyer the way a real estate agent represents a home buyer. A car buyer’s advocate stresses the same point: the person is on your side, not the dealer’s. A car buying consultant may only advise, or may also negotiate. A personal car shopper leans toward finding the car.

Whatever the label, ask two questions before you pay anyone. Who pays them? And do they actually negotiate, or only hand you a quote? Steer Me Right does both the finding and the negotiating, and the only money we take is your flat fee. Dealers pay us nothing. You’re the only one we work for.

How a negotiation runs at Steer Me Right

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims you’d accept, colors, budget, trade-in or not, and how far you’d travel. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model. Steer Me Right Pro covers up to three models and adds out-of-state dealers.
  3. We get written offers. We ask each dealer for an out-the-door price in writing, so there is nothing to “re-figure” when you arrive.
  4. We negotiate the price and check the fees. Offers go against each other. Dealer add-ons, protection packages and padded fees get questioned before you ever see the contract.
  5. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call and sign the paperwork.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount, and we say so: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do.
