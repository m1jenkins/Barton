The best used F-150s are the 2023 to 2025 model years, which draw 117, 53 and 27 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold. Skip the 2016, 2018 and 2021: they draw 375, 363 and 311, and the 2021 carries 29 recalls, more than any other year. NHTSA also has an open investigation, EA26001, into 2015 to 2017 trucks with the six-speed transmission that can downshift without warning. The 2017, 2019, 2020 and 2022 trucks sit in between, and in each of them the power train is the part owners name most often.

Ford publishes no F-150-only sales, so the rates use our estimate of 59% of F-Series sales, the same share our F-150 comparison pages use. They compare years with each other rather than predict one truck, and the hybrid, gas and body-style listings are counted together. Our F-150 price guide covers what a new or used one costs.

Complaints are owner reports to NHTSA, counted once each across every Ford F-150 listing (all the trim and body-style listings) and read on November 21, 2026. Each rate divides a model year’s complaints by the U.S. sales of the same calendar year. Model years and calendar years don’t line up, since a model year’s cars sell across two calendar years, so the rate compares years with each other. Ford reports only F-Series sales (which include Super Duty and Lightning), so each year’s F-150 sales are our estimate at 59% of that total. Our verdicts use this car’s own yardstick, the median of 122 complaints per 100,000 across its 2019 to 2025 model years: Buy up to 1.25 times it, Check first up to twice it, Avoid above that. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

F-150 generations: what changed

Thirteenth generation 2015–2020

The F-150 launched for 2015 with a 6-speed (6R80) automatic behind all four engines: a 3.5-liter V6, a 2.7-liter EcoBoost, a 3.5-liter EcoBoost and a 5.0-liter V8. The 10-speed (10R80) arrived for 2017 with the high-output 3.5-liter, and for 2018 it spread to the 2.7- and 3.5-liter EcoBoosts, the 5.0 V8 and the new 3.0-liter diesel, while a 3.3-liter V6 took the base slot with the 6-speed (Wikipedia).

Fourteenth generation 2021–

The redesigned truck carries the 10-speed on every engine and added the PowerBoost hybrid for 2021. The diesel left after 2021 and the 3.3-liter V6 after 2023, and a 2024 refresh trimmed the engine list further. For 2026 the 3.5-liter V6 became standard on most trims that used the 5.0, except the Lobo (Wikipedia). The Lightning is a separate EV and is not counted here.

Common F-150 problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 9,610 F-150 complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Transmission and drivetrain3,343
  • Engine1,875
  • Electrical system1,147
  • Brakes758
  • Body and structure636
  • Wipers and visibility596
  • Fuel and hybrid system456
  • Exterior lighting348
  • Power train, six-speed and early 10-speed trucks 2016–2018 model years1,549 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 100 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s open analysis EA26001 covers 2015 to 2017 trucks with the 6R80 that downshift without warning, often to first or second gear, with rear wheel lockup. Ford’s recall 26V-237 updates software for 2015 to 2017 trucks. Recall 18V-213 covers a missing roll pin that can defeat Park on 2017 and 2018 trucks with the 10-speed.
  • Power train, 10-speed trucks 2019–2022 model years1,487 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 82 per 100,000 sold. Pickup Truck Talk reports owner complaints of harsh 10-speed shifts on 2021 and later trucks, and we found no recall for the shift quality itself. Recall 22V-188 (2021) covers a false low-fluid-pressure code that can drop the transmission into neutral.
  • Engine, 3.5-liter and 5.0-liter years 2016–2018 model years1,178 complaints naming engine, 76 per 100,000 sold. Recalls cover a 3.5-liter block heater cable that can corrode (18V-894, 19V-278) and a 3.5-liter high-pressure fuel pump (18V-392). Reddit owners report cam phaser and timing chain trouble on 5.0- and 3.5-liter trucks, which no recall covers.
  • Engine, 2.7-liter years 2021–2022 model years192 complaints naming engine, 24 per 100,000 sold. Recall 24V-635 covers 2.7-liter intake valves that can break and cut drive power on 2021 and 2022 trucks. Dealers run a cycle test and replace the engine as needed, free.
  • Brakes 2016–2018 model years488 complaints naming brakes, 31 per 100,000 sold. Three recalls cover the brake master cylinder on 3.5-liter EcoBoost trucks (20V-332, 22V-150, 25V-236), which can leak fluid into the booster. Another covers the 2021 electronic brake booster (21V-533).
  • Visibility 2021 model years147 complaints naming wipers and visibility, 34 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s component field names the part, not the cause, and no single recall explains this spike. Ask the seller what was repaired.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the F-150: 700 mentions in 397 threads, 317 positive and 250 negative, a net of +10 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the F-150 is 67 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/f150 and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Ford F-150 price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit’s reliability complaints line up with the table: the threads single out 2018 to 2022 trucks for recalls and costly warranty repairs, and the 10-speed and older 6R80 for hard shifts and failures. The praise goes to the 5.0 and 2.7-liter engines, which owners report running past 200,000 miles with routine maintenance.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report 5.0 V8 and 2.7 EcoBoost trucks passing 200,000 miles with routine maintenance
  • 3.5 EcoBoost trucks often pass 250,000 miles with regular maintenance
  • Older 2010 to 2014 trucks are described as simple, durable and easy to repair

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report cam phaser and timing chain problems on 5.0 and 3.5 engines
  • Owners report 10-speed and 6R80 transmission failures, hard shifts and CDF drum repairs
  • Owners report recalls and costly warranty repairs on 2018 to 2022 trucks

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Unexpected downshift to second 2015–2017Recall 26V-237. A lost signal between the transmission range sensor and the powertrain computer can drop the transmission into second gear. Dealers update the software, free; owner letters went out July 24, 2026.
  • Missing roll pin 2017–2018 10-speedRecall 18V-213. A roll pin may be missing in the 10-speed, which can defeat the Park function. Dealers inspect and fit one, free.
  • Broken intake valves 2021–2022 2.7-literRecall 24V-635. Intake valves can break while driving and cut drive power. Dealers cycle-test the engine and replace it as needed, free.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump 2021–2023Recall 25V-455. The low-pressure fuel pump can fail and stall the engine. On the F-150, dealers update the powertrain software, free.
  • Rear axle hub bolts 2021–2025 Max Trailer TowRecall 23V-896 and 25V-512. Trucks with the Trailer Tow Max Duty package and the 9.75-inch axle can have an axle hub bolt problem. Check the build sheet before you buy a tow-package truck.
  • Oil leak, engine stall 2024–2025 3.5-literRecall 24V-851 and 25V-198. A misaligned engine cup plug can cause a rapid oil leak and a stall. Dealers inspect the plug and replace it as needed, free.
  • Connecting rods 2025Recall 25V-344. Connecting rod and bearing components may have manufacturing defects on certain trucks. The VIN lookup shows whether yours is included.
  • Fuel tank straps 2023–2027Recall 26V-578. The fuel tank may be secured improperly and leak or detach. Dealers inspect and replace the straps, free; owner letters went out September 21, 2026.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Unintended downshift and rear wheel lockup 2015–2017EA26001, open. NHTSA moved from a preliminary evaluation (PE25002, March 2025) to an engineering analysis on January 31, 2026. The preliminary evaluation cited 329 owner questionnaires from trucks with the 6R80 transmission, and Ford’s recall 26V-237, filed in April 2026, updates the software on the same trucks.
  • Rearview camera recall timing 2020RQ21002, open. NHTSA is checking the timing and scope of Ford’s rearview camera recall 20V-575, which covers 2020 F-150s and other Ford models.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump failures 2018–2023PE24019, closed. NHTSA closed it in 2025 after Ford’s recall 25V-455 and says it will keep watching vehicles the recall leaves out.

What to check before you buy a used F-150

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Ford dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Which transmission it has.A 2015 to 2017 with the 6R80 falls under EA26001; confirm recall 26V-237 is done. On any 2018 and later truck, drive it cold and again warm and watch for harsh or late shifts, which owners report on the 10-speed.
  • The engine recalls for its year.A 2021 or 2022 2.7-liter needs 24V-635 closed, a 2024 or 2025 3.5-liter needs 24V-851 and 25V-198, and a 2021 to 2023 needs the fuel pump update 25V-455.
  • Brake master cylinder on 3.5-liter EcoBoosts.Trucks of 2014 to 2018 with the 3.5-liter have three master cylinder recalls. Check the fluid level and ask for the repair record.
  • A Max Trailer Tow package.Look for the Trailer Tow Max Duty package on 2021 to 2025 trucks and confirm the hub bolt recalls (23V-896, 25V-512).
  • Fuel tank straps on a 2023 or newer.Recall 26V-578 is new, with letters sent in September 2026. Confirm the dealer has done it.
  • An independent inspection and a cold start.Owners on Reddit report a cold-start tick on the 5.0 and cam phaser trouble on 5.0 and 3.5 engines. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine and the transmission before you sign.

Common questions

What are the worst Ford F-150 years?

The 2016, 2018 and 2021. They draw 375, 363 and 311 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold on our estimate of F-150 sales, against 53 for the 2024. The 2021 also has 29 recalls.

What is the best used F-150 year?

The 2023 to 2025 model years have the lowest rates, from 27 for the 2025 to 117 for the 2023. Our price guide also says to make 2023 the floor for a used truck.

Is the F-150 10-speed transmission reliable?

Owners report harsh shifts on 2021 and later trucks (Pickup Truck Talk), and the power train is the most-named part in nearly every year here. Drive it cold and warm, and confirm the recalls for its year are done.

Are F-150 EcoBoost engines reliable?

Reddit owners report 2.7-liter trucks passing 200,000 miles and cam phaser trouble on 3.5- and 5.0-liter engines. Recalls cover 2.7-liter intake valves on 2021 and 2022 trucks and oil leaks on 2024 and 2025 3.5-liter trucks.

How do I check a used F-150 for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that truck, and recall repairs are free at a Ford dealer.
