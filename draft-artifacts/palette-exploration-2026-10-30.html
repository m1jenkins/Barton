Road palettes for Drive Right

Four directions drawn from road signs instead of fintech. Same homepage slice in each, so only colour and type change. Headline type is Barlow, which the site already self-hosts and which was drawn from California highway lettering; option 2 tries Overpass, a free cut of the FHWA "Highway Gothic" sign face.

1 · Interstate

Shield blue does the work; red is rationed to the promo bar and small labels, like the cap on an interstate shield. Warm off-white keeps it from feeling like a bank.
