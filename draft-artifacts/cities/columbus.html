Questions
about the service.

How long does it take?

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel before negotiations begin and you get a full refund. During negotiations, refund eligibility depends on the work already done. Read the refund policy.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.

Can you get me a price below MSRP?

Usually, yes. On new cars, getting you a discount off MSRP is the goal of every negotiation. How much depends on the model, local supply and timing. Some high-demand models sell at or above sticker, and we’ll tell you up front if yours is one of them.

Where can you help me buy?

We’re based in Austin and help buyers in Columbus remotely. Search reach and shipping options depend on your plan and the car, and we confirm them with you before work begins.

Am I in control of the final decision?

Always. We negotiate; you decide. Starting a car search doesn’t commit you to a vehicle. You choose your plan and decide whether to buy, after reviewing the vehicle, financing, insurance, and final paperwork. A specific discount or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.

What does Drive Right do?

We help Columbus car buyers get the car they want without the hours of searching, dealer calls and haggling. We research the cars, compare offers, work the dealers and negotiate the price and fees. You make the final call. Read what the car buying service covers.
