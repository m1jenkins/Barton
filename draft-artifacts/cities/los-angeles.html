about the service.

What does Drive Right do?

We help Los Angeles car buyers research vehicles, compare offers, communicate with participating sellers, and negotiate purchases. Full Service includes a dedicated advisor, price negotiation, fee review, and documented recommendations; Ultimate Concierge adds expanded sourcing and coordination. Read what the car buying service covers.

What will it cost?

Full Service is $295 USD one time, and Ultimate Concierge is $895 USD one time. These are service fees. The vehicle, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, and any delivery costs are separate. Confirm current terms before booking.

Where can you help me buy?

We’re based in Austin and help buyers in Los Angeles remotely. Search reach, seller participation, and delivery options depend on your plan and vehicle. We confirm those details with you before work begins.

Am I in control of the final decision?

Always. Building a brief doesn’t commit you to a vehicle. You choose your plan and decide whether to buy, after reviewing the vehicle, financing, insurance, and final paperwork. A specific discount or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.
