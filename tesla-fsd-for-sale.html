Find a Used Tesla With Full Self-Driving Already Included

Updated: August 22, 2026

Direct answer: Tesla now sells Full Self-Driving as a subscription only — roughly $99/month, forever. But every Tesla originally ordered with the FSD package keeps it for life, attached to the car, unless the owner transferred it to a newer Tesla. Those non-transferred FSD cars are rare, badly labeled in listings, and extremely valuable. Drive Right finds them for you: we email and call every relevant dealer, pull the exact build sheet for each candidate, and document that FSD stays with the car — saving you thousands in subscription fees and weeks of dead-end searching.

Why a non-transferred FSD Tesla is suddenly worth thousands more

For years, Full Self-Driving was a one-time upgrade — buyers paid up front and the package stayed with the vehicle through every subsequent owner. That changed when Tesla moved FSD to a subscription-only model for new activations. Today, the only ways to get FSD are paying month after month, or buying a car that already has it.

Here's the part most shoppers miss: FSD follows the VIN, not the owner. When an FSD-equipped Tesla is traded in or resold, the package rides along with the car — unless the original owner used Tesla's limited transfer option to move it to their next Tesla. If they didn't transfer it, whoever buys that used car owns FSD outright. No monthly fee. Ever.

The problem: dealers rarely know what they're sitting on. Listing pages blur the line between Full Self-Driving, Enhanced Autopilot, and basic Autopilot. Salespeople guess. Buyers drive hours to inspect cars, only to discover the FSD was already transferred off. Finding one of these cars on your own means calling dealership after dealership and hoping someone will actually walk to the car and check.

The math: included FSD vs. subscription

The prize

FSD included with the car

$0/month. The factory FSD package stays with the VIN for life, transfers to you at purchase, and typically boosts resale value when you sell.

The treadmill

FSD subscription

Roughly $99/month — about $1,188 per year and nearly $6,000 over a five-year ownership. Stop paying and the feature disappears.

The trap

Buying blind

Overpaying for a car because a listing claimed FSD — then learning at delivery that it was Enhanced Autopilot, or that FSD was transferred off. Confirm before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

What does "non-transferred" FSD mean?

Factory-purchased FSD normally stays with the car when it's sold. Tesla has also let owners make a limited one-time transfer of FSD to a newer Tesla. A "non-transferred" car still carries its FSD package on the original VIN — which is exactly the car you want to buy.

Can you still buy FSD outright from Tesla?

Tesla currently offers FSD (Supervised) to new buyers as a subscription rather than a one-time purchase. Programs change, so confirm terms on Tesla's site — but today, a used car with FSD already attached is the primary way to own the capability outright.

How much can I actually save?

At the current $99/month rate, FSD costs about $1,188 a year — roughly $6,000 over five years. A car with FSD included skips that bill entirely. Your real-world savings depend on how long you keep the car, whether you'd have subscribed, and what premium the seller asks. We show you both sides of that math on every shortlist.

Doesn't the listing say if the car has FSD?

Rarely — and inaccurately when it does. Many listings confuse Full Self-Driving with Enhanced Autopilot or basic Autopilot. The only reliable evidence is the original build sheet plus confirmation the package wasn't transferred. That's the documentation we collect for you.

Which models can have FSD included?

Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, and Cybertruck can all carry the factory FSD package, subject to hardware compatibility. We confirm the exact option codes on each candidate's build sheet.

Do you guarantee you'll find one?

No honest service can — inventory turns daily and these cars are scarce. What we commit to is the process: exhaustive dealer outreach, build-sheet documentation, and transfer-status verification before you invest time in any specific car.

Availability, FSD program terms, and subscription pricing are set by Tesla and can change without notice. Verify final FSD status for the exact VIN with Tesla before purchase.
