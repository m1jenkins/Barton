The Seattle car market right now

The Census Bureau counted 254 new-car dealer locations across the Seattle metro area in 2023. That area is King, Pierce and Snohomish counties. We found no public price, inventory or registration figure for metro Seattle that we could cite, so the Census count is the only market figure on this page.

Buying a car around Seattle

Count the tolls on the way to the dealer

The SR 520 bridge, the SR 99 tunnel and the Tacoma Narrows Bridge all charge tolls. Without a Good To Go! pass, WSDOT photographs your plate and mails a bill at a higher rate than the pass rate. At the Tacoma Narrows Bridge you can also stop at a toll booth. A dealer across one of those crossings can cost more to reach than its price suggests. Tell us which crossings you’d use, and we weigh dealers by how far you would drive.

Ask about the ferry if you live across the water

If you live on Bainbridge Island, on Vashon or on the Kitsap side, a dealer in Seattle means a ferry ride home in the car you just bought. Washington State Ferries runs routes from Seattle to Bainbridge Island and to Bremerton, and from Fauntleroy to Vashon and Southworth. WSDOT’s Best Times to Travel tool shows the busiest and least busy times to take a vehicle on a ferry. Tell us where you live, and we ask the dealers you could reach on your own schedule.

Check where you will charge at home

Find out where an electric car will plug in before you shop for one. Seattle City Light says a faster home charger can use the same type of outlet that powers a laundry dryer or an electric oven. The US Department of Energy says an electrician can tell you whether your home has enough electrical capacity. Tell us if you want an electric car or a plug-in hybrid and whether you can charge at home, and we search the powertrain that fits.

How it works from Seattle

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

What stays with you

You decide which car, if any, to buy. You arrange the inspection, pick the financing and insurance, read the contract and sign it. Our fee does not include the car, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance or delivery. We don’t give tax or registration advice for your state; the dealer’s paperwork and your state’s motor vehicle agency are the sources for that.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Seattle?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Seattle-area dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Will you contact dealers on both sides of Puget Sound?

Yes. Tell us where you live and which crossings you’d drive, and we ask dealers on both shores, from Everett to Tacoma and across the water on the Kitsap side. They are all Washington dealers, so both plans cover them.

Do I need a Good To Go! pass to drive to a dealer?

No. Without a pass, WSDOT photographs your plate and mails a bill at a higher rate than the pass rate, and the Tacoma Narrows Bridge also has toll booths. Tell us which crossings are on your route, and we weigh dealers by how far you’d drive.

Can I take a ferry to pick up a car?

Yes. WSDOT’s Best Times to Travel tool shows the busiest and least busy times to take a vehicle on a ferry. Tell us where you’d start, and we ask the dealers on the shore you can reach.
