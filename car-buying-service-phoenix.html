Buying a car around Phoenix

Look at what the sun did to a used car

Summer heat wears a car from above. Look for a dashboard top that has cracked or gone chalky, headlight lenses turned yellow or cloudy, faded paint on the roof and hood, and tires with cracked sidewalls. A car parked outside for years shows more of it than one kept in a garage. Heat also shortens a battery’s life, so ask how old the battery is. On a used search we can ask the dealer for photos of those spots and of the battery label. Have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.

Test the air conditioning at its worst

A weak air conditioner is the repair you’ll notice first here, and plenty of cars feel fine on a cool morning. Ask the dealer for an afternoon test drive, run the air conditioning at full blast the whole way, and watch how fast the cabin cools. Keep an eye on the temperature gauge in traffic. If the air blows warm, price the repair before you talk price on the car.

Tell us how far across the valley you’d drive

Metro Phoenix spreads over a wide, flat valley. A lower written offer from the far side of it can cost you a long drive to pick up the car. Tell us where you’d start and the longest drive you’d make, and we ask the dealers inside that window.

How it works from Phoenix

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Phoenix?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Phoenix-area dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Will you contact dealers across the whole valley?

Yes. Tell us how far you’d go to pick up the car and we ask dealers across that area, from Surprise to Gilbert and beyond. They are all Arizona dealers, so both plans cover them.

Can you tell if a used car has sun damage?

Only from photos. We can ask the dealer for pictures of the dashboard, the headlights, the tires and the battery label, and send them to you. Photos miss things, so have a mechanic you choose look the car over before you sign.

What should I check on a test drive in the heat?

Run the air conditioning at full blast for the whole drive and see how fast the cabin cools. Watch the temperature gauge in stop-and-go traffic. We can ask a dealer to schedule an afternoon drive, and a mechanic you choose can look the car over before you sign.
