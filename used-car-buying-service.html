Some car buying services only negotiate new cars

A lot of car buying services are built around new cars: one model, one window sticker, the same car at every dealer, so the job is getting the lowest number. Used cars don’t fit that pattern, and some services step back from them.

Delivrd is the clearest example right now. On October 23, 2026, a notice on its Insta-Pay page read “PAUSE on USED: We’re only negotiating new cars,” and the same page lists its fee at $1,000 per deal. Delivrd calls the pause temporary, so check its page before you rule it out.

Drive Right has no pause. Used and CPO cars are part of both plans, at the same flat fee as a new car. Other services that list used cars, with their fees and refund rules, are in car buying services compared, Delivrd alternatives and CarEdge alternatives.

Why a used car is where you want help

  • No sticker to anchor the price. Every used car has its own miles, history and condition, so the asking price is a starting point, not a fact. Knowing what comparable cars sell for is most of the negotiation.
  • The search is the hard part. The right car might sit on a lot two states away. Finding it takes hours of listings, calls and follow-ups.
  • Fees and add-ons hide in the paperwork. Reconditioning fees, dealer add-ons and extended warranties show up late. We check the fees before you sign.
  • CPO isn’t one thing. A manufacturer CPO program and a dealer’s own “certified” label can mean very different coverage. Ask which one you are getting.

What Drive Right does on a used car

  1. You pay the flat fee through Stripe, then tell us the car: models you’d consider, year and mileage range, budget, must-haves and how far you’d travel. Mason replies within one business day of payment to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. Your advisor searches used, CPO and new dealer inventory against your search details. Drive Right Pro covers up to three models and adds out-of-state dealers.
  3. We work the dealers by phone and email, negotiate the price and check the fees, then send written recommendations: what we found and what we’d do.
  4. You make the final call. If the car is at an out-of-state dealer and you’re on Drive Right Pro, we help coordinate shipping when the dealer is open to it.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs take longer, and we tell you that up front. Before you sign for any used car, our used-car inspection checklist covers the title, history and recall checks and the questions to ask an independent mechanic.
