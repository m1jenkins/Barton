Yes, for most buyers. A CR-V Sport Hybrid costs $2,480 more than a gas EX, the trim it matches on equipment, and Honda rates it 40 mpg combined against 30. At the national average of $3.805 a gallon that is about $476 less gas a year at 15,000 miles, so the premium is back in 5.2 years (6.5 years at 12,000 miles).

The case for the hybrid is power as much as fuel. It makes 204 hp against the turbo’s 190, and its electric motor alone is rated 247 lb-ft against 179 for the gas engine. What you give up is practical: 3 cubic feet less cargo room behind the rear seat, 500 pounds less towing, and, on iSeeCars’ figures, a little faster depreciation. Honda also says the next CR-V is hybrid-only, so the gas engine has a short future.

Buy the hybrid if…

  • You keep a car five or six years, or drive about 15,000 miles a year. The Sport Hybrid’s $2,480 premium is back in 5.2 years at that pace.
  • You want the quicker CR-V: 204 hp against 190, and Edmunds faults the gas engine as slow.
  • Your miles are mostly around town. The hybrid is rated 43 mpg in the city against 28.
  • You want the EX’s equipment (heated front seats, moonroof, blind-spot monitoring) with a hybrid, which is what the Sport Hybrid is.

Buy the gas version if…

  • You tow. The gas CR-V is rated 1,500 pounds and the hybrid 1,000.
  • You drive fewer than about 10,000 miles a year. At that pace the premium takes nearly 8 years to earn back.
  • You want the cheapest CR-V: an LX starts at $30,920, $4,710 under the cheapest hybrid.
  • You need every cubic foot: the gas CR-V has 39.3 cubic feet behind the rear seat and the hybrid 36.3.

What the hybrid costs on like-for-like trims

Honda doesn’t sell a CR-V both ways on the same trim, so we paired each hybrid with the gas trim that matches its equipment. The Sport Hybrid carries the EX’s list (heated front seats, power moonroof, 18-inch wheels, remote start), and the Sport-L Hybrid carries the EX-L’s leather, power tailgate and parking sensors.

EPA fuel economy, gas against hybrid

EPA rates the front-drive hybrid at 40 mpg combined and the front-drive gas CR-V at 30. Most of the gain is in town, 43 mpg against 28; on the highway it is 36 against 33, so a commute that is mostly interstate narrows the gap. All-wheel drive costs the hybrid 3 mpg and the gas car 1, which still leaves the hybrid 8 mpg ahead.

How many years until the hybrid pays for itself?

Divide the premium by the yearly gas savings. For the Sport Hybrid over the EX that takes 5.0 to 6.5 years in front-drive form and 5.5 to 7.3 years with AWD, depending on mileage and gas price. The Sport-L Hybrid’s bigger premium over the EX-L stretches it to 6.7 to 8.7 years.

Do this month’s incentives favor one version?

Honda lists no cash back on either version. Its finance and lease offers sit on the 2027 CR-V and CR-V Hybrid alike: 3.49% APR for 36 months in Chicago and Dallas and 3.99% in Los Angeles and New York, ending January 4, 2027, and none on a leftover 2026. The leases are the only place the versions differ: $299 a month for a 2027 LX and $319 for a Sport Hybrid, both for 39 months with $3,999 due, though they aren’t the same trim. Nothing this month offsets the hybrid’s premium or adds to it. Offers are from the CR-V price guide, read at Honda’s offers pages on November 16, 2026.

Resale: does the hybrid hold its value?

iSeeCars puts three-year depreciation at 13.7% for the gas CR-V and 15.9% for the hybrid, and five-year depreciation at 34.8% against 38.8%. The hybrid does lose value a little faster, but it also resells higher: iSeeCars’ comparison expects a five-year-old hybrid to bring $21,819 and a gas CR-V $20,161, so about $1,658 of the premium comes back at resale on its sample prices. Edmunds scores the hybrid 7.7 and the gas CR-V 7.4, in part because it finds the gas engine slow.

What owners on Reddit report about real-world mpg

Hybrid owners in these threads mostly say real mileage lands near the rating until winter or a long highway run. We counted 169 opinions about the CR-V’s fuel economy in 542 Reddit threads about the car, posted from January 2025 to November 2026: 106 positive, 34 negative and 29 mixed. That is a net score of +43 on the same −100 to +100 scale as the CR-V price guide, and it covers every version of the car, not only the hybrid.

Common questions

How much more does the CR-V Hybrid cost than the gas CR-V?

$2,480 for a Sport Hybrid over an EX, which have the same equipment, and $3,325 for a Sport-L Hybrid over an EX-L. The cheapest hybrid is $4,710 above the cheapest gas CR-V, but that compares it with the LX, which has less equipment. Prices are 2026 starting MSRPs without destination, from the CR-V price guide.

How much gas does the CR-V Hybrid save?

About 125 gallons a year at 15,000 miles (375 against 500), which is $476 at the November 16 national average of $3.805 and $500 at EPA’s $4.00. At 12,000 miles it is about 100 gallons. Owners on Reddit report 35 to 40 mpg in city and mixed driving.

Does the CR-V Hybrid come with all-wheel drive?

On the Sport Hybrid and Sport-L Hybrid it is a $1,500 option. The TrailSport and Sport Touring Hybrids come only with AWD. AWD cuts the hybrid to 37 mpg combined, still 8 mpg better than an AWD gas CR-V.

Should I wait for the next CR-V?

Only if you want the new one. Honda says an all-new, hybrid-only CR-V arrives in 2027 as a 2028 model but hasn’t given a price or a date. The current hybrid is on dealer lots now.
