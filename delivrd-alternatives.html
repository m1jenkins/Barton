Delivrd alternatives: car buying services for new and used cars

By Mason · Updated October 18, 2026

Short answer: Delivrd charges a flat $1,000 per deal, paid up front, and on October 18, 2026 it was only negotiating new cars. For a used or CPO car, or a lower fee, Drive Right charges $395 (Full Service) or $695 (Ultimate Concierge) to search new, used and CPO inventory and negotiate for you. CarEdge, Car Pal and Automatch Consulting also publish their prices.

Delivrd, spelled without an “e” and often searched as “Delivered,” is a car negotiation service at delivrdto.me. What Delivrd offers

Delivrd’s service is Insta-Pay Deal Negotiation. Its service agreement describes it as a “Flat fee. One deal. No hidden costs, no percentage of the sale, no upsells.”

  • $1,000 per deal, paid up front, according to its Insta-Pay page. The fee doesn’t depend on whether you buy. In Delivrd’s words: “We put in the hours whether you buy the car or not.”
  • After a consult, Delivrd requests window stickers, pulls out-the-door pricing, compares finance terms and negotiates with several dealers at once. It gives you at least two options and helps you close. Trade-in help and a financing review are part of the service.
  • Founder Tomi Mikula works with a team that includes deal coordinators and negotiators, according to its team page, and Delivrd’s press page says it works with buyers in all 50 states.
  • On October 18, 2026, a notice before Delivrd’s checkout read: “PAUSE on USED: We’re only negotiating new cars.” The Insta-Pay page also warns of long waits for some models, such as 6 to 12 months for a Lexus GX550 allocation.
  • The Insta-Pay page says your deal starts within 7 to 10 business days of your consult. Other Delivrd pages say 10 to 15 business days.

Refunds and changes at Delivrd

Delivrd’s service agreement says you can get a refund “If we haven’t started working your deal yet,” and that “There are no refunds after work has started.” Work starts when its team begins contacting dealerships and pulling numbers. Changing the car costs extra: $250 for a different trim or features on the same car, and $500 for a different car entirely.

Drive Right’s terms work differently. Cancel before negotiations begin and you get a full refund. During negotiations, refund eligibility depends on the work already done. The full terms are in our refund policy.

Why people look for a Delivrd alternative

A used or CPO car

While Delivrd’s used-car pause lasts, a used-car buyer needs someone else. Drive Right searches new, used and CPO inventory on both plans, and Ultimate Concierge also searches auctions, forums, private sellers and out-of-state dealers.

The fee

Delivrd’s $1,000 is more than twice Drive Right’s $395 Full Service. Ultimate Concierge, with the wider search and help arranging shipping for out-of-state deals, is $695.

Timing

Delivrd’s pages say your deal starts 7 to 15 business days after your consult. At Drive Right, Mason replies within one business day of payment to confirm the search and when it starts, and most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front.

Room to change your mind

If you are still deciding between trims or models, Delivrd’s $250 and $500 change fees add up. Settle the car first, or ask any service how it handles a change before you pay.

Other Delivrd alternatives

CarEdge

Its pricing page lists a Concierge at $999 per car search, for new or used cars, and an AI Buying Agent at $49.99 during its beta. Our CarEdge alternatives page has the details, including its paid program for dealerships.

Car Pal

Its pricing page lists full service at $1,195 for cars and SUVs and $1,695 for trucks. It covers new and used cars and arranges inspections for used cars and home or office delivery.

Automatch Consulting

Its payments page lists $499 if you already know the car and $599 if you want help choosing, for new, used or leased cars.

Authority Auto

Handles new-car purchases and leases, with full-service pricing by phone. Its free deal review charges nothing unless it beats a quote you already have; then you split the savings.

Costco Auto Program

For Costco members, the program offers new and used cars at a price prearranged with a participating dealer. You go to the dealer and finish the purchase yourself.

When Delivrd is the better pick

  • You are buying a new car and already know the exact configuration.
  • You want trade-in help and a financing review bundled into the same fee.
  • You prefer a larger team: its team page lists deal coordinators and negotiators alongside the founder.

Common questions

How much does Delivrd cost?

On October 18, 2026, Delivrd listed Insta-Pay Deal Negotiation at $1,000 per deal, paid up front. Its “Hire Delivrd” buttons lead to that service.

Does Delivrd help with used cars?

Not right now. On October 18, 2026, a notice before Delivrd’s checkout read “PAUSE on USED: We’re only negotiating new cars.” Drive Right, CarEdge, Car Pal and Automatch all list used cars.

Can I get a refund from Delivrd?

Only before work starts. Its service agreement says there are no refunds once its team starts contacting dealerships, and it charges $250 or $500 if you change the car.

How long does Delivrd take to start?

Its Insta-Pay page says your deal starts within 7 to 10 business days of your consult. Other Delivrd pages say 10 to 15 business days. We didn’t find how long a whole deal takes.

Does Delivrd take money from dealers?

Its About page says it never accepts kickbacks or bonuses from dealerships or manufacturers. Drive Right is the same: dealers and lenders pay us nothing.
