The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The Ranger is the midsize pickup to price first if ride quality and tech matter to you. Edmunds ranks it first of seven midsize trucks. U.S. News ranks it seventh, citing low-speed transmission jitters and cheap cabin materials. Price a 2026 XLT, aim for 3–4% under MSRP before Ford’s cash, and get your bank’s rate before you accept Ford’s 4.9%.

New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. Ford’s cash, financing and leases apply to the 2026, and it is the truck dealers are stocked with.

Check: That the offer runs in your region, that the truck is not a Raptor if you expect the bonus cash, and that its recalls are done.

New 2027

Best when: You can wait, or you want the 2027’s changes.

Check: Ford has not posted 2027 prices or offers on ford.com, and the 2027 lineup may swap the 2.7L V6 for a 3.0L V6, per a report on Ford’s order guide.

CPO (Ford Blue Advantage Gold)

Best when: You want a 2024 or 2025 with a warranty and you’re paying cash.

Check: That it is Gold Certified, not Blue. Gold covers trucks up to 6 years old with under 80,000 miles, adds a 172-point inspection, a 12-month/12,000-mile comprehensive warranty and 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage. Blue Certified gives 90 days or 4,000 miles.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $31,000, which buys a 2023 or older.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and that you are comparing like with like: the Ranger was redesigned for 2024, so a 2023 is the previous generation.

Buying used? A Gold Certified 2024 or 2025 is the route we’d take. Ask for the recall paperwork in writing: NHTSA lists 13 recall campaigns on the 2024 and 13 on the 2025. A 2023 is the previous generation.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Engine recalls. Two NHTSA recalls cover loss of drive power: 26V343 (2025–2026, a new engine long block) and 26V548 (2026, an inspection). Check any truck’s VIN before you sign.
  • Rearview camera recall. 26V605 covers 2026 Rangers with the 10.1-inch screen, which can lose power and drop the camera image. NHTSA lists the remedy as under development.
  • Dealer-installed extras. Ford’s own price list shows a hard roll-up tonneau cover at $1,470 and cargo management rails at $600. Ask for extras you didn’t request to come off.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 can sit on the same lot. Ford’s current offers are for the 2026.
  • Cash or rate. The 4.9% needs Ford Credit approval, and Ford’s pages do not say whether it combines with the cash. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.

Is the Ford Ranger a good truck?

Most reviewers say yes, with one outlier. Edmunds scores the 2026 a 7.1 out of 10, first of seven midsize trucks, and Kelley Blue Book 4.7 out of 5. U.S. News ranks it seventh of seven, faulting low-speed transmission jitters and cheap cabin materials.

How much under MSRP can you get a Ford Ranger?

On an XL, XLT or Lariat, 3–4% under MSRP is a fair deal and 6–7% a strong one, before Ford’s $2,500 cash. The Raptor has less room, around 1–2%. Those ranges are our read, so a dealer may land a point either side.

What is the destination charge on a Ranger?

$1,895 on the 2026. Ford’s advertised prices leave it out, so a $33,550 XL is $35,445 on the window sticker.

Does Ford offer cash back on the Ranger?

Yes, until November 30, 2026: $2,000 customer cash plus $500 Black Friday bonus cash on a 2026 Ranger. The bonus cash is not available on the Raptor. Ford’s pages do not say whether the cash combines with its 4.9% financing, so ask the dealer.

Should I buy the 2.7L V6?

Probably not. Edmunds clocked a V6 Ranger at 6.2 seconds to 60 mph against 6.7 for a four-cylinder Lariat 4x4, and the EPA rates it 20 mpg combined against 21. Both engines tow 7,500 pounds with the Trailer Tow Package.

Is the Ranger Raptor worth it?

Only if you will use it off pavement. It starts at $57,270 against $43,955 for a Lariat, is rated 17 mpg combined and tows 5,510 pounds against 7,500. It gets none of Ford’s November cash.

Is a used Ranger a better deal than new?

Rarely. A used 2024 asks a median $37,075, while a new XLT starts at $36,075 before Ford’s $2,500 cash. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Ford’s 4.9%. A Gold Certified 2024 or 2025 with its recalls done is the used route we’d take.
