The best used Accords are the 2023 and 2024 model years, which draw 120 and 146 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against an Accord yardstick of 146. Skip the 2018 and 2019: the 2018 draws 695 per 100,000 on 2,023 complaints, the most of any year, with the engine named in 614, the fuel system in 501 and automatic emergency braking in 358, and the 2019 draws 298. The 2020 to 2022 are the years to ask about, because NHTSA’s open analysis EA24002 covers 2018 to 2022 Accords that brake hard on their own. The 2025 rates lowest at 85, but 55 of its 132 complaints name steering and no recall covers them, so we rate it Check first.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across the Accord, Accord Hybrid and Accord Sedan listings, by U.S. sales. The 2022 and 2025 sales, 154,612 and 150,196, are the lowest of the run, which lifts those two rates. Our Accord price guide makes 2023 the floor for a used one, which this page matches.

Accord generations: what changed

Ninth generation 2016–2017

The 2013 to 2017 Accord got a 2016 refresh with a new front end and lights. It came with a 2.4-liter four-cylinder (185 hp), a 3.5-liter V6 (278 hp) and a hybrid, with a CVT on the four, and a six-speed manual was offered on the four-cylinder and V6 coupe (Wikipedia).

Tenth generation 2018–2022

The sedan went on sale in October 2017 as a 2018 with a 1.5-liter turbo four (192 hp) and, from December 2017, a 2.0-liter turbo (252 hp) that replaced the V6; the hybrid (212 hp) followed in March 2018. A 2021 refresh dropped the manual gearbox and made Apple CarPlay and Android Auto standard (Wikipedia).

Eleventh generation 2023–

Unveiled in November 2022 for the 2023 model year, with the 1.5-liter turbo kept, the 2.0-liter dropped and a 212-hp hybrid. The SE replaced the EX for 2025, and the 2026 model added a 9-inch screen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto on LX and SE (Wikipedia).

Common Accord problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 5,681 Accord complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Steering373
  • Transmission and drivetrain344
  • Speed control274
  • Engine, 2018 and 2019 2018–2019 model years837 complaints naming engine, 150 per 100,000 sold. The biggest part count on the two worst years. NHTSA’s field doesn’t say what owners describe, and none of the recalls in these years names the engine. Reddit owners report head gasket failures and oil dilution on the 1.5-liter turbo, which became the Accord’s base engine in 2018.
  • Fuel system, 2018 and 2019 2018–2019 model years713 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 128 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 20V-314 and 21V-215 cover an in-tank low-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, and 23V-858 extends the fuel pump fix across 2013 to 2023 Accords. Dealers replace the pump assembly free; the VIN lookup shows what is open.
  • Automatic emergency braking, 2018 to 2022 2018–2022 model years800 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 72 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s analysis EA24002, still open, covers cars that brake hard on their own with nothing in the lane, with 1,294 complaints across 2018 to 2022 Accords and 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs. No recall covers it yet.
  • Electrical system, 2016 and 2017 2016–2017 model years368 complaints naming electrical system, 55 per 100,000 sold. Recall 17V-418 covers a battery sensor case on 2013 to 2016 cars that can let water in and cause a short, which raises fire risk. Dealers replace the sensor, with an interim adhesive repair, free.
  • Steering, 2024 and 2025 2024–2025 model years130 complaints naming steering, 42 per 100,000 sold. Our Accord price guide counted 128 steering complaints on these two years, many describing a clicking or sticky steering rack. No recall or investigation covers it.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Accord: 352 mentions in 143 threads, 159 positive and 136 negative, a net of +8 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Accord is 53 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/accord and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Honda Accord price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners praise the Accord for reaching 200,000 miles on routine maintenance, and call the four-cylinder, V6 and hybrid systems durable. What they report going wrong is the 1.5-liter turbo’s head gasket and oil dilution, transmission failures on some older cars and older four-cylinders burning oil. Those threads line up with the 2018 engine count above, while the NHTSA steering and automatic braking counts don’t appear among the reliability themes.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report Accords passing 200k miles with only routine maintenance
  • K24 four-cylinder and J35 V6 engines last with regular fluid changes
  • Hybrid system and battery are described as durable by owners

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report head gasket failures and oil dilution on the 1.5T engine
  • Owners report automatic and CVT transmission failures on some V6 and four-cylinder cars
  • Owners report older four-cylinder cars burning oil between changes

Other complaints that come up

  • Dealers and markups. Honda dealer service quotes and fees are called inflated versus independent shops
  • Price and value. Used asking prices are seen as too high for age and mileage

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Passenger weight sensor 2016–2022Recall 26V-332. The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can set off the air bags unintentionally in a crash. Dealers replace the sensor free, and 24V-064 covers the same part on 2020 to 2022 cars.
  • In-tank fuel pump 2013–2023Recall 20V-314, 21V-215 and 23V-858. The low-pressure fuel pump inside the tank can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump assembly free.
  • Body control module software 2018–2020Recall 20V-771. A software error can interrupt communication between the body control module and other parts, which can disable the wipers, defroster, rearview camera or exterior lights. Dealers update the software free.
  • Seat belt buckle 2018–2019Recall 23V-158. The front seat belt buckle channel can interfere with the release button and keep the buckle from latching. Dealers replace the button or buckle free.
  • Battery sensor and V6 fuel pump 2013–2017Recall 17V-418 and 19V-060. Recall 17V-418 covers a battery sensor case that can let water in and short, and 19V-060 a V6 fuel pump that particulates can slow, which can stall the engine. Both repairs are free.
  • High-pressure fuel pump 2023–2024Recall 24V-763. The pump may crack and leak fuel, which raises fire risk. Dealers inspect and replace it as needed, free.
  • Driver seat cushion frame 2023–2024Recall 24V-859 and 26V-054. The driver’s seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, which can leave the seat loose in a crash. Dealers replace the frame free.
  • Hybrid control software 2023–2025 HybridRecall 25V-785. A software error can reset the integrated control module while driving and cause a loss of drive power. Dealers reprogram it free.
  • Tire repair kit 2023–2026 HybridRecall 26V-366 and 26V-663. Pressure can build in the sealant bottle and detach its cap like a small projectile. Dealers replace the nozzle or bottle free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Inadvertent automatic emergency braking 2018–2022EA24002, open. NHTSA opened the analysis on April 16, 2024, upgrading preliminary evaluation PE22003 from February 2022. It covers 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs and 2018 to 2022 Accords whose automatic emergency braking can brake hard with no obstruction ahead; NHTSA counts 1,294 complaints, and Honda has said some owners may misunderstand the system’s limits. Our verdicts treat the 2021 as Check first because of it.

What to check before you buy a used Accord

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Honda dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Automatic braking on a 2018 to 2022.Open analysis EA24002 has no recall yet. Ask the owner whether the car has ever braked hard on its own, and drive a highway stretch with cruise control on before you sign.
  • Fuel pump recalls.On a 2018 to 2020, confirm 20V-314 and 21V-215; on any 2016 to 2023, that 23V-858 is closed; on a 2023 or 2024, that 24V-763 is.
  • The passenger weight sensor on a 2016 to 2022.Confirm recall 26V-332 (and 24V-064 on a 2020 to 2022). The repair is free at a Honda dealer.
  • Steering on a 2024 or 2025.Owners report a clicking or sticky steering rack and no recall exists. Turn the wheel lock to lock when you test-drive it and have a shop check the rack.
  • Oil and coolant on a 2018 or later 1.5-liter turbo.Reddit owners report oil dilution and head gasket leaks. Check the dipstick for a high level or a fuel smell and the coolant level.
  • Hybrid software and seat recalls on a 2023 to 2025.Confirm 25V-785 on a Hybrid, and 24V-859 and 26V-054 for the driver’s seat on a 2023 or 2024.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine, transmission and brakes before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Accord has 16 NHTSA complaints and 2 recalls so far, both for the Hybrid’s tire repair kit, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2023 or 2024, the used cars carry the fuel pump, seat and Hybrid software recalls, and the 2026 does not.

Common questions

What is the worst Honda Accord year?

The 2018, at 695 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold and 2,023 complaints, the most of any year. The 2019 is next at 298.

What is the best used Accord year?

The 2023 and 2024, at 120 and 146 complaints per 100,000. The 2025 has the lowest rate, 85, but 55 of its 132 complaints name steering, so we rate it Check first.

Is the Honda Accord reliable?

Reddit owners report Accords reaching 200,000 miles on routine maintenance. NHTSA’s counts say it depends on the year: 146 complaints per 100,000 for the 2021 and 120 for the 2023, against 695 for the 2018.

Does the Accord brake hard on its own?

NHTSA’s open analysis EA24002 covers 2018 to 2022 Accords and 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs whose automatic emergency braking can brake hard with nothing ahead. There is no recall, so a VIN lookup won’t show it.

How do I check a used Accord for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Honda dealer.
