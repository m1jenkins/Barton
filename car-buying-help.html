Car buying help: three ways to get it, two of them free

By Mason · Updated November 15, 2026

Short answer: If you want help buying a car, you have three options here. Use our free worksheet and guides and do it yourself. Ask Mason a question, which costs nothing. Or pay a flat $395 or $695 and have an advisor find the car and negotiate with the dealers for you. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing, whichever you pick.

Do it yourself · Ask a question · Hand it off · Help paying for a car · Questions

1. Do it yourself, with better tools

Most of what goes wrong at a dealership comes down to not having the whole price in front of you. These are free and don’t ask for your email:

  • Quote comparison worksheet. Type in two dealer quotes and see which one really costs less. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
  • Comparing complete new-car quotes. How to ask for a written out-the-door price and what should be on it.
  • Dealership add-ons. What to ask about each product before it lands in your contract.
  • Used-car inspection checklist. What to look at yourself and what to ask an independent mechanic.

If you have the time and don’t mind the phone calls, this is enough to buy a car well.

2. Ask Mason a question

Not sure which plan you need, or whether you need one at all? Tell us your situation and we’ll tell you which plan fits. No payment needed to ask, and we reply within one business day. Email hello@steermeright.com or use “Talk to Mason” on the pricing page.

3. Hand the dealers to us

This is the paid part. One advisor finds the car, contacts the dealers that have it, gets written out-the-door offers, negotiates the price and checks the fees. You get written recommendations and make the final call. Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal.

If you meant help paying for a car

Some people searching for car buying assistance are looking for money: a grant, a loan or a program that helps cover the cost of a vehicle. That is not us. Steer Me Right is not a lender and does not offer financial assistance. We help with finding the car and negotiating the deal; the financing is yours to arrange.

Common questions

Can I pay someone to help me buy a car?

Yes. Steer Me Right finds the car and negotiates with dealers for a flat $395 (one model) or $695 (up to three models). You make the final decision and sign the paperwork.

Is there free help buying a car?

Yes. Our quote worksheet and guides are free, and asking Mason a question costs nothing. You only pay if you want us to do the search and the negotiating.

Do you help first-time buyers?

Yes. You deal with one advisor from start to finish, and the written recommendations explain what we found and what we’d do.

Do you help with financing?

No. You pick the financing and insurance. We are not a lender, and lenders pay us nothing.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel before negotiations begin and you get a full refund. During negotiations, refund eligibility depends on the work already done. Read the refund policy.
