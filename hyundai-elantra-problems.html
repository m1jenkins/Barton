The best used Elantras are the 2021 to 2025 model years, which draw 130, 136, 130, 47 and 24 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against an Elantra yardstick of 130. Skip the 2017: it draws 389 per 100,000 on 771 complaints, the most of any year, with the engine named in 294. The 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2020 are the years to ask about. The engine leads the complaints on the 2018, 2019 and 2020 (146, 138 and 113), and the 2016 carries Hyundai’s May 2026 recall for an air bag inflator that may rupture. The 2018’s middling rate of 142 hides the engine count: no recall covers that year.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across the Elantra, Elantra Hybrid and Elantra N listings, by U.S. sales. The Elantra’s yardstick is high, so a Buy year here still draws far more complaints than a Buy year on a Corolla; our Corolla vs Elantra page sets the two side by side. The 2020 rate is lifted by Hyundai’s low 2020 sales of 105,475. Our Elantra price guide tells used shoppers to start at the 2023, and this page starts a little earlier.

Elantra generations: what changed

Fifth generation 2016

The 2011 to 2016 U.S. Elantra was built in Alabama, with the sedan updated for 2014 and further changes for 2015 and 2016 (Wikipedia).

Sixth generation 2017–2020

Hyundai launched it for 2017 with a 2.0-liter four-cylinder (147 hp) and a 1.4-liter turbo Eco (128 hp, seven-speed dual-clutch), and added the 201-hp 1.6-liter turbo Sport in mid-2017. A 2019 refresh brought optional LED headlights and made advanced safety features standard on SEL and above, and the 2020 paired the 2.0-liter with a CVT (Wikipedia).

Seventh generation 2021–2025

It arrived in late 2020 as a 2021, with a 139-hp hybrid and, from July 2021, the 276-hp Elantra N. A refresh for 2024 simplified the hybrid to one trim and made rear side-impact air bags standard (Wikipedia).

  • Engine, 2016 to 2020 2016–2020 model years806 complaints naming engine, 91 per 100,000 sold. Recall 21V-301 covers piston oil rings on 2019 and 2020 cars with the 2.0-liter engine that may not have been heat-treated properly, which can damage the engine. Dealers fix it free. No recall covers the 2016 to 2018 engines, and Reddit owners report older Elantras burning oil or failing around 100,000 to 150,000 miles.
  • Body and structure, 2017 2017 model years134 complaints naming body and structure, 68 per 100,000 sold. The 2017’s second-largest part count after the engine. NHTSA’s field names the part and not what the owner describes, and no recall covers it.
  • Air bag inflators, 2016 and 2017 2016–2017 model years58 complaints naming air bags, 14 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 16V-232 and 16V-956 cover 2017 driver air bag modules built in 2016, and 26V-307 (May 2026) a driver air bag inflator on 2015 and 2016 cars that may rupture. Dealers inspect and replace the part free; Hyundai said the final remedy was due in August 2026.
  • Seat belts, 2021 and 2022 2021–2022 model years56 complaints naming seat belts, 23 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 21V-796, 22V-123, 22V-218 and 22V-354 cover front seat belt pretensioners that can burst on deployment and throw metal fragments. NHTSA closed its recall query RQ22003 in May 2023 after Hyundai widened the recalls. Dealers replace the part free.
  • Electrical system, 2021 to 2023 2021–2023 model years126 complaints naming electrical system, 34 per 100,000 sold. The biggest part count on the newer seventh-generation cars. No recall of its own covers it, and NHTSA’s field doesn’t say what owners describe.
  • Automatic emergency braking, 2021 to 2023 2021–2023 model years93 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 25 per 100,000 sold. NHTSA’s field names the driver-assist system and not the cause, and no recall covers it. Reddit owners report the forward safety warning light coming on at random and disabling cruise control.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Elantra: 533 mentions in 409 threads, 184 positive and 297 negative, a net of −24 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Elantra is 58 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/Elantra and r/ElantraN and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Hyundai Elantra price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners say 2021 and newer Elantras hold up better than older ones, which fits the NHTSA split above, and some cite Hyundai’s long powertrain warranty as reassurance on a newer car. What they report going wrong is older engines burning oil or failing around 100,000 to 150,000 miles, white paint that chips or peels, and warranty claims that dealers refuse. Some call a used Elantra a riskier long-term buy than a Corolla.

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Driver air bag inflator 2015–2016Recall 26V-307. The driver’s air bag inflator may rupture during deployment and throw sharp metal fragments. Dealers inspect and replace it as needed, free; ask whether the final remedy is in stock.
  • Driver air bag modules 2017Recall 16V-232 and 16V-956. On 2017 cars built in early to late 2016, a missing second-stage enhancer or a badly installed end seal can leave the driver’s air bag under-inflated. Dealers replace the module free.
  • Brake booster and power steering connector 2017Recall 17V-063 and 17V-213. The brake booster may fail and cut power brake assist (17V-063), and a damaged connector may cut power steering assist (17V-213). Dealers replace the booster or the steering motor free.
  • Piston oil rings 2019–2020 2.0-literRecall 21V-301. Oil rings that were not heat-treated properly can damage the engine. Dealers repair the engine as needed, free.
  • Lug nuts and ball joint 2020Recall 19V-720 and 19V-721. Right rear lug nuts may be under-tightened and let a wheel detach (19V-720), and lower control arm ball joint fasteners may be loose (19V-721). Dealers tighten them free.
  • Seat belt pretensioners 2021–2022Recall 21V-796, 22V-123, 22V-218 and 22V-354. A front pretensioner can explode on deployment and send metal fragments into the cabin. Dealers replace it free.
  • Windshield bonding 2020–2021Recall 21V00M000. The windshield may not have been bonded properly and can detach in a crash. Dealers remove and reinstall it free.
  • Air bag emblem and oil pump controller 2022–2023Recall 22V-632 and 23V-526. An air bag cover emblem may detach on deployment (22V-632), and the idle-stop oil pump controller may overheat and raise fire risk (23V-526). Both repairs are free.
  • Front struts and lug nuts 2026Recall 26V-692 and 26V-693. Front strut fasteners may be loose and cost steering control (26V-692), and lug nuts may loosen and let a wheel detach (26V-693; Hyundai replaces the hub bearings). Both repairs are free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Air bag inflator rupture 2016EA16003, open. NHTSA upgraded its evaluation of ARC Automotive air bag inflators to an engineering analysis on August 4, 2016, after ruptures in older Chrysler, Kia and Hyundai cars, including a fatal 2009 Elantra crash in Canada. It remains open. Hyundai filed recall 26V-307 for 2015 and 2016 Elantras in May 2026; NHTSA’s summary does not say whether that inflator is part of this analysis.
  • Seat belt pretensioners 2021–2022RQ22003, closed. NHTSA opened a recall query on August 8, 2022 after three injuries from front pretensioners that ruptured in Hyundai and Kia cars, including 2021 and 2022 Elantras. It closed it in May 2023 once Hyundai widened its recalls.
  • Throttle control vulnerabilities 2007–2016DP21003, closed. NHTSA denied a petition on electronic throttle systems in 2007 to 2016 Elantras and other Hyundai and Kia models in June 2023, saying a formal investigation wasn’t warranted.

What to check before you buy a used Elantra

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Hyundai dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The air bag recall on a 2015 to 2017.Ask the dealer to show 26V-307 is done on a 2016, and 16V-232 or 16V-956 on a 2017. The repair is free.
  • Engine history on a 2016 to 2020.Ask for service records and the oil-change history. Read the dipstick, look for oil leaks and confirm 21V-301 is closed on a 2019 or 2020 2.0-liter car.
  • Seat belt and camera recalls on a 2021 or 2022.Confirm the pretensioner recalls (21V-796, 22V-123, 22V-218, 22V-354) and the camera recall 24V-879 are closed.
  • Hybrid recalls on a 2024 to 2026.Confirm 26V-308, the hybrid control unit software, is done.
  • Strut and lug nut recalls on a 2026.Ask the dealer to show 26V-692 and 26V-693 are done before you take delivery.
  • Warranty status.Ask a Hyundai dealer what powertrain warranty still covers the exact car; Hyundai’s terms change with the owner and the mileage.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the engine, oil level and coolant before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Elantra has 6 NHTSA complaints and 4 recalls so far, including strut fasteners and lug nuts that can let a wheel detach, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024 or 2025, the 2024 and 2025 have 2 recalls each to confirm, and the 2026 has two fastener recalls to confirm before you take delivery.

Common questions

What is the worst Hyundai Elantra year?

The 2017, at 389 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold on 771 complaints, the most of any year; 294 name the engine. The 2020 is next at 243.

What is the best used Elantra year?

The 2021 to 2025 model years, at 130, 136, 130, 47 and 24 complaints per 100,000. Ask for proof the 2021 and 2022 pretensioner recalls are done.

Do Hyundai Elantras have engine problems?

NHTSA’s counts name the engine in 294, 146, 138 and 113 complaints on the 2017 to 2020 model years, and Reddit owners report older Elantras burning oil or failing around 100,000 to 150,000 miles. Recall 21V-301 covers piston oil rings on 2019 and 2020 cars with the 2.0-liter engine.

Is the 2026 Elantra reliable?

It is too new to rate. It has 6 NHTSA complaints and 4 recalls so far, including front strut fasteners and lug nuts, so ask the dealer to show both repairs are done.

How do I check a used Elantra for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Hyundai dealer.
