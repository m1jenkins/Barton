The bottom line

Used, a 2023

A used Model 3 is the cheapest way into a Tesla, and the 2023 is where the money is. It averaged $27,800 at dealers in September, against $37,300 for a 2024 and $38,630 for a new Standard with fees. Shop a 2023 Long Range AWD (358 miles of EPA range), aim about 1–3% under asking, and check recalls by VIN.

  • 18 to 22 recall campaigns each on 2019–2022 cars
  • Hardware 3 on every 2017–2023 car

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Open recalls. Run the VIN at NHTSA. The headlight repair for 2017–2023 cars (26V507) isn’t available yet. 2025 cars need a service visit for battery-pack contactor recall 25V690.
  • Hardware 3 or 4. Every 2017–2023 Model 3 has Hardware 3, which Tesla says can’t run unsupervised FSD. The 2024 has Hardware 4. Check under Settings, Software, Additional Vehicle Information.
  • FSD in the listing. Tesla says FSD bought outright stays with the car, and now sells it only as a subscription. Check it’s on the car’s screen before paying extra.
  • The “warranty” on the window. A dealer’s warranty is its own or a third party’s. Ask for the first delivery date to see how much battery cover is left.
  • Suspension, brakes and lights. Germany’s TÜV failed 13.1% of two- to three-year-old Model 3s, against about 6.5% for that age group. Have an independent shop inspect the car first.

How much is a used Tesla Model 3?

An average of $21,100 for a 2019 up to $38,200 for a 2025 at dealers in September 2026, by Recurrent’s count. Each 10,000 miles takes about $1,000 off a car of the same year. A new Model 3 starts at $36,990 before fees.

How much under asking can you get a used Model 3?

On a 2024 or 2025, 1–2% under asking is fair and 3–4% strong. On a 2019 to 2023, about 1% is fair and 2–3% strong. That’s our estimate: nobody publishes used Model 3 sale prices. Tesla doesn’t negotiate on its own used cars.

What is the best year for a used Model 3?

The 2023 Long Range AWD, in our view: about $9,500 below the average 2024 asking price, with 358 miles of EPA range. Buy a 2024 or later if you want Hardware 4, the quieter cabin and ventilated front seats.

Does the Tesla battery warranty transfer to a used buyer?

Yes, at no cost, from any seller. It runs 8 years or 120,000 miles on a Long Range or Performance and 8 years or 100,000 miles on a Standard or Standard Range Plus, counted from first delivery, and covers capacity that falls below 70%.

Does a used Model 3 from a dealer come with Tesla’s warranty?

Not Tesla’s used warranty. Its 1-year/10,000-mile cover applies only to used cars Tesla sells and delivers itself. The remainder of the original 4-year/50,000-mile basic warranty and the battery warranty do carry over.

Does Full Self-Driving transfer with a used Model 3?

FSD bought outright stays with the car, Tesla says. A subscription, the only way Tesla sells it now, doesn’t carry over. Check FSD shows on the car’s screen before paying extra. Tesla says Hardware 3 cars can’t run unsupervised FSD.

Is a used Model 3 reliable?

Mixed. U.S. News predicts 82 out of 100 for the 2026. Owners of three-year-old Teslas reported 226 problems per 100 vehicles in J.D. Power’s 2026 dependability study, against 204 for the industry. Germany’s TÜV failed 13.1% of two- to three-year-old Model 3s at inspection.
