The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The RAV4 is hybrid-only now and still easy to recommend: 43 mpg combined with front-wheel drive, and it loses value about half as fast as the average vehicle. The catch is price. Toyota offers no cash back, and TrueCar’s averages put most trims within 2% of sticker. Price a 2026 XLE Premium and aim for 1–2% under MSRP.

New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. Toyota’s 4.99% and the $500 rebates apply only to the 2026.

Check: That the SUV is a 2026 and not a 2027, and that the offer runs in your region.

New 2027

Best when: You want a plug-in with DC fast charging, or the Woodland’s retuned suspension.

Check: The $400 to $755 price rise, and that there are no offers yet.

CPO (Toyota Certified Gold)

Best when: You want a 2023 to 2025 hybrid with a warranty, and you’re paying cash.

Check: That it’s Gold Certified, not Silver. Gold adds a 12-month/12,000-mile comprehensive warranty and 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage; Silver gives 12 months of powertrain cover only. The hybrid battery stays covered for 10 years/150,000 miles from first sale.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $28,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2019 to 2022 AWD hybrid, the high-voltage cables under the floor.

If buying used, make 2023 the floor. The 2019 to 2021 RAV4s carry the most recalls of the last generation, AWD hybrids from 2019 to 2022 can corrode their high-voltage cables, and some hybrids’ fuel tanks won’t fill to the rated 14.5 gallons.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Toyota dealers put add-ons on 37.7% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,637. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • Markups. Some dealers added $1,104 to $4,700 over sticker when the 2026 launched. Supply is still tight, so walk away from a markup and ask the next dealer.
  • The Toyota Financial Services requirement. The 4.99% and the $500 rebates need Toyota’s own financing. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • Fees. Documentation fees on RAV4 quotes ran from $180 in Iowa to $1,094 in Florida in CarEdge’s study. Compare out-the-door prices.
  • The plug-in recall. Ask the dealer to show that recall 26V659’s software update is done before you take delivery.

Is the Toyota RAV4 a good SUV?

Most reviewers think so. Edmunds rates the 2026 an 8.1 out of 10, third of 12 small hybrid SUVs, and J.D. Power’s owner score of 85 ties it for first among compact SUVs. U.S. News is cooler, at 8.3 and 14th of 19 hybrid SUVs.

How much under MSRP can you get a Toyota RAV4?

On a 2026 LE, SE or XLE Premium, 1–2% under MSRP is fair and 3–4% strong. The Woodland, XSE and Limited sell at about sticker. That’s our read of TrueCar’s and Edmunds’ figures, so a dealer may land a point either side.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 RAV4?

The 2026, for most buyers. It costs $400 to $755 less, and Toyota’s 4.99% financing and $500 rebates apply only to the 2026. Wait for the 2027 if you want a plug-in with DC fast charging or the Woodland’s retuned suspension.

What is the destination charge on a RAV4?

$1,595 on the 2026 and the 2027, hybrid or plug-in. Toyota’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and Toyota says it may differ in AL, AR, FL, GA, LA, MS, NC, OK, SC and TX.

Does Toyota offer cash back on the RAV4?

No. In Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York on November 15, 2026, Toyota offered 4.99% financing for 48 months and leases, with no customer cash. The only money off is a $500 military or college rebate on a Toyota Financial Services loan or lease.

Is the RAV4 plug-in hybrid worth it?

Only if you can charge at home and most of your trips are short. The SE is EPA-rated for 54 miles on electricity, and Edmunds got 58 in testing. It costs $5,400 more than an AWD hybrid SE, and the federal tax credit is gone.

Is a used RAV4 a better deal than new?

Rarely. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2025 is $32,299, and TrueCar’s average paid for a new 2026 LE is $32,951. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Toyota’s 4.99%. A Gold Certified 2023 to 2025 hybrid is the used route we’d take.
