The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The Tacoma is the midsize pickup that holds its value best: iSeeCars puts its five-year depreciation at 19.5% against 30.2% for the class. Edmunds and U.S. News both fault the tight back seat. It still sells under sticker. Price a 2026 TRD Sport, aim for 5–6% under MSRP, and take Toyota’s 3.99% financing if your bank can’t beat it.

  • Reliability. Owners report Tacomas passing 200,000 miles with only routine maintenance

What owners complain about

  • Price and value. Used Tacomas carry a Toyota premium over similar trucks

How we set these discount targets

Tacomas sell quickly but not at sticker. Edmunds says new ones are selling in about 18 days against 34 for the average new vehicle, and Toyota had 39 days of supply at the end of October against 87 for the industry. Buyers still get real money off: CarWhere’s 31 verified 2026 buyers paid a median 7.9% under MSRP, Edmunds’ suggested prices, based on Texas sales, run 6–7% under, and TrueCar’s averages put the SR5, PreRunner and TRD Sport 5–6% under. New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. Toyota’s financing, leases and the SR5 cash apply to the 2026, and it costs $180 to $520 less than a 2027.

Check: That the truck is a 2026 and not a 2027, and that the offer runs in your region.

New 2027

Best when: The 2026 you want is gone, or you want a 2027-only color.

Check: The $180 to $520 price rise, and that its 4.99% runs only in Southern California and the New York area.

CPO (Toyota Certified Gold)

Best when: You want a 2024 or 2025 with a warranty, and you’re paying cash.

Check: That it’s Gold Certified, not Silver. Gold adds a 12-month/12,000-mile comprehensive warranty and 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage; Silver gives 12 months of powertrain cover only. An i-FORCE MAX hybrid battery stays covered for 10 years/150,000 miles from first sale.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $33,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2022 or 2023, that the rear axle recall is done.

Buying a 2024 or 2025? Get the recall work in writing: they were recalled for rear brake hoses, shock-absorber reservoirs and the dashboard warning display. On a 2022 or 2023, the rear axle shafts were recalled because they could separate.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Add-ons. In Florida, Georgia, Alabama and the Carolinas, Toyota’s regional distributor fits its own packages. One buyer reported $2,857 of them plus $1,599 from the dealer. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • Dealer stickers above Toyota’s. Some dealers print their own price over Toyota’s: one buyer saw $2,838 added to a TRD Off-Road. Negotiate down from Toyota’s window sticker.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 look alike on the lot. In Chicago and Dallas, only the 2026 has offers.
  • The Toyota Financial Services requirement. The 3.99%, the leases and the $500 rebates need Toyota’s own financing. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • Fees. Documentation fees in owner reports ran from $225 to $899. Compare out-the-door prices.

Is the Toyota Tacoma a good truck?

Most reviewers think so. Kelley Blue Book rates the 2026 a 4.8 out of 5 and first among midsize trucks, and U.S. News gives it 8.8. Edmunds is cooler at 6.8, faulting the cramped back seat and engine noise under hard acceleration.

How much under MSRP can you get a Toyota Tacoma?

On a 2026 SR5, TRD PreRunner, TRD Sport or TRD Off-Road, 5–6% under MSRP is a fair deal and 7–8% a strong one, by our read of TrueCar, Edmunds and CarWhere figures. The SR and the hybrids have less room, around 2–4%.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Tacoma?

The 2026, for most buyers. It costs $180 to $520 less, and nearly all of Toyota’s offers are on it: 3.99% financing, the SR5’s $500 finance cash and the leases. The 2027 gets 4.99% in Southern California and the New York area only.

What is the destination charge on a Tacoma?

$1,745 on the 2026. Toyota’s advertised starting prices leave it out, so a $32,545 SR is $34,290 on the window sticker.

Does Toyota offer cash back on the Tacoma?

No. On November 16, 2026, Toyota offered financing from 2.99% and lease deals, but no customer cash. The only money off is $500 on a 2026 SR5 financed through Toyota in three regions, and a $500 military or college rebate.

Is the Tacoma i-FORCE MAX hybrid worth it?

Only if you want its power or its 2,400-watt outlet. It makes 326 hp and 465 lb-ft against 278 hp and 317 lb-ft, but it costs $3,720 more on a TRD Sport 4x4 and is rated 23 mpg combined against 21.

Is a used Tacoma a better deal than new?

Rarely. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2024 is $38,119, while TrueCar’s average paid for a new SR5 starts at $36,274. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Toyota’s 3.99%. A Gold Certified 2024 or 2025 with its recalls done is the used route we’d take.
