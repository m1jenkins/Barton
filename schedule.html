Questions before
you choose.

Can you get me a price below MSRP?

Usually, yes. On new cars, getting you a discount off MSRP is the goal of every negotiation. How much depends on the model, local supply and timing. Some high-demand models sell at or above sticker, and we’ll tell you up front if yours is one of them.

Which plan is right for me?

Full Service fits most buyers: you know roughly what you want, and you want someone else to do the searching and the negotiating. Choose Ultimate Concierge for a hard-to-find, enthusiast or luxury car, or when you want a wider search with priority communication and coordination.

What happens after I pay?

You tell us what you’re looking for right after checkout. Your advisor confirms scope and expected start time after payment and intake verification, then gets to work.

Do I have to buy the car you find?

No. We negotiate; you decide. You review the vehicle, the deal and the final paperwork, and you decide whether to buy. A specific price or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the U.S. We’re based in Austin and work with buyers nationwide, remotely. Search reach, seller participation and delivery options depend on your plan and vehicle.
