More about Steer Me Right

Questions before you choose.

Can you get me a price below MSRP?

Usually, yes. On new cars, getting you a discount off MSRP is the goal of every negotiation. How much depends on the model, local supply and timing. Some high-demand models sell at or above sticker, and we’ll tell you up front if yours is one of them.

Which plan is right for me?

Steer Me Right if you know the car you want: one model, any trim, color or package. Steer Me Right Pro if you’re torn between a few, say a RAV4 Hybrid and a CR-V Hybrid. We get offers on up to three models, including out-of-state dealers, and tell you which deal we’d take. Switching models isn’t included on Steer Me Right, but you can upgrade to Pro later by paying the $300 difference.

What happens after I pay?

You tell us what you’re looking for right after checkout. We reply within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts, then get to work.

How long does it take?

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.

How do you compare with CarEdge or Delivrd?

See our side-by-side comparison of car buying services. It lists each company’s published price, who does the negotiating and the refund terms, with links to their own pages so you can check.

Do I have to buy the car you find?

No. We negotiate; you decide. You review the vehicle, the deal and the final paperwork, and you decide whether to buy. A specific price or vehicle outcome isn’t guaranteed.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the U.S. We’re based in Austin and work with buyers nationwide, remotely. Search reach and shipping options depend on your plan and the car.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. or email hello@steermeright.com, Monday–Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central.
