New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. The runout discounts and Chevrolet’s cash and 0% are on the 2026, and the RST and LTZ exist only as 2026s.

Check: That the truck is a 2026, which engine it has (the $6,000 needs the 5.3L), and that the offer runs in your region.

New 2027

Best when: You want the new 5.7L or 6.6L V8, the 16.3-inch screen and the redesigned cabin, or the 13,700-pound tow rating. Edmunds scores it 7.7 against 6.1 for the 2026.

Check: The price rise ($8,500 on the High Country, $900 to $1,400 on the Custom and WT), and that Chevrolet lists no 2027 offers yet.

CPO (GM CarBravo)

Best when: You want a 2023 to 2025 truck with some cover, and you’re paying cash.

Check: CarBravo’s certified warranty is 12 months/12,000 miles bumper to bumper on trucks under 10 model years and 100,000 miles. Chevrolet’s powertrain warranty runs 5 years/60,000 miles from first sale.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $37,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 6.2L V8, proof that recall 25V274’s engine inspection was done.

Buying used? Start at 2023. The 2019 and 2020 trucks carry 10 and 12 recalls, and 2021 to 2024 trucks with the 6.2L V8 were recalled in 2025 for defects that can wreck the engine. Owners are suing GM over lifter failures on its V8s.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Which engine. The $6,000 applies only to the 5.3L V8 and the $2,250 only to an LT with the TurboMax. Make sure a quote names the engine.
  • Cash or 0%. You get one or the other. Work out the total cost of each with your bank’s rate before you choose.
  • Dealer add-ons. Chevrolet dealers put them on 32.4% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,443. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products: refuse or negotiate them.
  • The 6.2L V8. On a 2021 to 2024 truck, ask for proof that recall 25V274’s inspection was done. NHTSA’s open investigation also covers 2025 and 2026 engines.
  • The transfer case recall. A few 2026 4WD trucks are under recall 26V289, and GM tells owners not to drive them until fixed. Have the dealer check the VIN.

Is the Chevrolet Silverado 1500 a good truck?

Reviewers are lukewarm on the 2026 and warmer on the 2027. U.S. News scores the 2026 an 8.8 out of 10 and Kelley Blue Book 4.3 out of 5. Edmunds gives it 6.1 and the redesigned 2027 a 7.7.

How much under MSRP can you get a Chevrolet Silverado 1500?

On a 2026 LT, RST, Custom Trail Boss, LTZ or LT Trail Boss, 5–7% under MSRP is a fair deal and 8–10% a strong one. That’s our read of TrueCar’s averages and iSeeCars’ invoice figures. Chevrolet’s cash comes on top.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Silverado 1500?

The 2026 if price matters most. It costs less (the High Country starts $8,500 lower), and Chevrolet’s cash and 0% are on the 2026. The 2027 brings new V8s and a redesigned cabin, and Edmunds scores it 7.7 against 6.1 for the 2026.

What is the destination charge on a Silverado 1500?

$2,795 on the 2026 and the 2027. Chevrolet’s advertised starting prices leave it out.

Does Chevrolet offer cash back on the Silverado 1500?

Yes, on the V8. On November 16, 2026, Chevrolet offered $6,000 on a 2026 Silverado 1500 with the 5.3L V8 and $2,250 on an LT with the TurboMax, or 0% for 60 months. All of it ends with delivery by November 30.

Is the Silverado’s 6.2L V8 reliable?

It has a record. GM recalled nearly 600,000 2021 to 2024 trucks and SUVs with the 6.2L in 2025, and NHTSA has since opened a wider investigation covering 2021 to 2026 engines. Until that closes, we’d take the 5.3L V8 or the diesel.

How much can a Silverado 1500 tow?

Chevrolet lists up to 13,300 pounds on the 2026 and up to 13,700 on the 2027 with the new 5.7L V8. A specific truck’s limit depends on its engine, cab, axle ratio and hitch, so check the trailering rating on its own label.

Is the Silverado 1500 the same truck as the Sierra?

They’re closely related and offer the same four engines. The Silverado costs less (the 2026 WT starts at $37,200, against $38,600 for a Sierra Pro) and is finished a step lower inside. Price both and let the better deal decide.
