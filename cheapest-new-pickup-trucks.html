Cheapest new pickup trucks for 2026

By Mason · Updated November 21, 2026

  • Our pick
  • Prices
  • At a glance
  • The ranking
  • Plans
  • Questions

The Ford Maverick XL is the cheapest new pickup in the US at $29,990 with destination, and it’s a 38-mpg hybrid. The Hyundai Santa Cruz is next at $31,950. The cheapest midsize trucks sit within $230 of each other: the Nissan Frontier S King Cab ($34,265), Toyota Tacoma SR ($34,290) and Chevrolet Colorado WT ($34,495). The cheapest full-size is the Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Work Truck at $39,995. We’d buy a step up from the base on most of them, and the table shows which.

How we ranked them

Trucks are ordered by the base trim’s MSRP plus the maker’s destination charge, which is $1,495 on a Ridgeline and $2,795 on a Silverado, so ordering by sticker alone would put the wrong trucks first. Each truck is priced as its maker sells it now: a 2026 where our price guide covers it, and a 2027 where the maker lists only that year. Heavy-duty and electric pickups are left out.

Cheapest to dearest

  1. 1 Ford Maverick Compact · 2027

    The cheapest new pickup you can buy, and no other truck here is close: the next one costs $1,960 more.

    Base trim
    2027 XL hybrid, FWD SuperCrew $28,145 $29,990 with $1,845 destination
    The trim we’d buy
    XL hybrid, with AWD if you tow or see snow the base trim
    EPA

    What the base lacks. AWD ($3,395 more with destination), and the tow package that raises the 2,000-pound rating to 4,000 pounds on AWD trucks.

    Why that trim. U.S. News suggests most buyers stick with the XL or XLT. The 2027 XL is a hybrid; U.S. News listed the 2026 gas XL at $27,145.

  2. 2 Hyundai Santa Cruz Compact · 2027

    2027 is the Santa Cruz’s last model year, and U.S. News ranks it second of three compact pickups (8.8 out of 10) behind the Maverick (9.1).

    Base trim
    2027 SE, FWD $30,300 $31,950 with $1,650 destination
    The trim we’d buy
    SE, with AWD if you see snow the base trim
    EPA
    25 combined (24 with AWD) mpg

    What the base lacks. AWD ($1,500 on the SE and SEL) and the turbo engine, which only the XRT gets. Blind-spot and lane-keeping aids are standard.

    Why that trim. The SE already has the safety aids, and the next trim up, the SEL Activity, is $34,700.

    One engine, a 310-hp 3.8-liter V6, on every Frontier, and U.S. News ranks it sixth of seven midsize pickups.

    Why that trim. The SV adds the 12.3-inch screen and wireless CarPlay and Android Auto, and U.S. News lists it only as a crew cab.

Common questions

What is the cheapest new pickup truck?

The 2027 Ford Maverick XL, at $28,145 MSRP or $29,990 with destination. It’s front-wheel drive with a hybrid engine; AWD adds $3,395.

What is the cheapest full-size pickup?

The 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Work Truck, at $37,200 MSRP or $39,995 with destination, then the GMC Sierra 1500 Pro at $41,395 and the Ford F-150 XL at $42,380.

What is the cheapest truck with 4WD?

A Maverick XL with AWD, at $33,385 with destination, $65 under a Santa Cruz SE with AWD ($33,450). The cheapest midsize 4WD we priced is a Colorado WT at $37,795, because 4WD adds $3,300.

Does destination count in these prices?

Yes. Every price here is MSRP plus the destination charge, which is on every window sticker.
