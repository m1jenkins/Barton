Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $27,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2023 to 2025, that steering recall 24V744 is done.

If you buy used, make 2023 the floor. That year began the current CR-V. NHTSA has an open investigation into 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs that brake hard on their own (1,294 owner complaints), and 2017 and 2018 turbo engines could dilute their oil with fuel.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Honda dealers put add-ons on 35.1% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,159. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 look the same on the lot. Honda’s rates and leases apply only to the 2027, and the 2026 should cost less.
  • The Honda Financial Services requirement. The low rates and every $500 or $750 offer need Honda’s own financing. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • First payment in 90 days. Interest still builds during those 90 days, so it isn’t a discount.
  • Fees. CarEdge puts the national median for dealer fees at $498. Compare out-the-door prices.

Is the Honda CR-V a good SUV?

Most reviewers think so. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.0 out of 10 and Kelley Blue Book 4.8 out of 5. Edmunds is cooler, at 7.4 (7.7 for the hybrid), and faults the slow gas engine and short list of driver aids.

How much under MSRP can you get a Honda CR-V?

On a 2026 LX, EX or EX-L, 3–4% under MSRP is fair and 5–6% strong. Sport and TrailSport hybrids get 2–3%, the Sport-L and Sport Touring 4–5% or more. That’s our read of TrueCar, Edmunds and CarWhere figures, give or take a point.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 CR-V?

Price both. Honda changed only the price for 2027: $600 more on the LX, $400 on the rest and $100 more for destination. A leftover 2026 should cost less, but Honda’s 3.49% to 4.99% financing and its leases are only on the 2027.

Should I wait for the 2028 CR-V?

Only if you want the new one. Honda says an all-new, hybrid-only CR-V arrives in 2027, but it hasn’t given a price or a date. The current CR-V is a known car that dealers are discounting now.

What is the destination charge on a CR-V?

$1,450 on the 2026 and $1,550 on the 2027. Honda’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and premium paint adds $455.

Does Honda offer cash back on the CR-V?

No. In the four regions we checked on November 16, 2026, Honda offered financing from 3.49%, leases from $299 a month and $500 for Honda owners, all on the 2027, with no customer cash. Military members and recent graduates get $500 on either year.

Is the CR-V Hybrid worth it?

For most buyers, yes. The Sport Hybrid costs $230 more than a gas EX-L and is rated 40 mpg combined against 30 with front-wheel drive. You give up the EX-L’s leather and power tailgate, and it loses value a little faster.

Is a used CR-V a better deal than new?

Rarely. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2025 is $33,066, more than TrueCar’s $31,517 average paid for a new 2026 LX. Used loans averaged 11.19% in the second quarter of 2026. A HondaTrue Certified 2023 to 2025 is the used route we’d take.
