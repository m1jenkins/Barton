Honda says the CR-V is America’s best-selling SUV this year, and it’s one of the easier small SUVs to buy below sticker. A 2026 starts at $30,920 with the gas engine and $35,630 as a hybrid, and the 2027 now on Honda’s site is the same car for $400 to $600 more. What people praise

  • Reliability. Owners report older CR-Vs passing 200,000 miles with only routine maintenance

The trim we’d buy

A 2026 Sport Hybrid, with AWD if you see snow. At $35,630 it costs $230 more than a gas EX-L and has everything the EX does, including heated front seats, a power moonroof and blind-spot monitoring. In place of the 190-hp turbo it has the 204-hp hybrid, with 247 lb-ft of torque against 179, and it’s rated 40 mpg combined against 30. AWD adds $1,500 and drops it to 37 mpg.

Want leather and a power tailgate? The Sport-L Hybrid adds both, plus parking sensors that brake for you, for $3,095 more, and it’s Edmunds’ pick among the hybrids. Skip the TrailSport unless you want its all-terrain tires: it’s AWD only and rated 35 mpg. If you’d rather have gas, Edmunds picks the EX-L, but the Sport Hybrid costs almost the same.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Dealer add-ons. Honda dealers put add-ons on 35.1% of the quotes CarWhere studied, averaging $1,159. Paint protection, nitrogen and etched VINs are the dealer’s products. Refuse or negotiate them.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 look the same on the lot. Honda’s rates and leases apply only to the 2027, and the 2026 should cost less.
  • The Honda Financial Services requirement. The low rates and every $500 or $750 offer need Honda’s own financing. Compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • “No payments for 90 days.” Interest still builds during those 90 days, so it isn’t a discount.
  • Fees. CarEdge puts the national median for dealer fees at $498. Compare out-the-door prices, not monthly payments.
  • Recalls on a used one. On a 2023 to 2025 CR-V, ask for proof that steering recall 24V744 is done.

Common questions

Is the Honda CR-V a good SUV?

Most reviewers think so. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.0 out of 10 and Kelley Blue Book 4.8 out of 5. Edmunds is cooler, at 7.4 (7.7 for the hybrid), and faults the slow gas engine and short list of driver aids. J.D. Power owners give it 82 out of 100. A three-year-old CR-V has lost 13.7% of its value by iSeeCars’ figures, against 24.8% for the average vehicle, and Kelley Blue Book named it the 2026 best resale value compact SUV.

How much under MSRP can you get a Honda CR-V?

On a 2026 LX, EX or EX-L, 3–4% under MSRP is a fair deal and 5–6% a strong one. The Sport and TrailSport hybrids sell closer to sticker, at 2–3%, and the Sport-L and Sport Touring have the most room, at 4–5% or more. Those ranges are our read of TrueCar’s and Edmunds’ November figures and CarWhere’s buyer reports, so a given dealer may land a point either side.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 CR-V?

Price both. Honda changed only the price for 2027: $600 more on the LX, $400 on the rest, and $100 more for destination. A leftover 2026 should cost less, but Honda’s 3.49% to 4.99% financing and its leases are only on the 2027, so compare the total cost of each.

Should I wait for the 2028 CR-V?

Only if you want the new one. Honda says an all-new, hybrid-only CR-V arrives in 2027, but it hasn’t given a price or a date. The current CR-V is a known car that dealers are discounting now.

What is the destination charge on a CR-V?

$1,450 on the 2026 and $1,550 on the 2027. Honda’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and premium paint adds $455.

Does Honda offer cash back on the CR-V?

No. In Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York on November 16, 2026, Honda offered financing from 3.49%, leases from $299 a month and $500 for current Honda owners, all on the 2027, but no customer cash. Military members and recent graduates get $500 off a 2026 or 2027 when they finance or lease through Honda.

Is the CR-V Hybrid worth it?

For most buyers, yes. The Sport Hybrid costs $230 more than a gas EX-L, makes 204 hp against 190, and is rated 40 mpg combined against 30 with front-wheel drive. You give up the EX-L’s leather and power tailgate. It loses value a little faster: 15.9% in three years against 13.7% for the gas CR-V.

Is a used CR-V a better deal than new?

Rarely. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2025 is $33,066, more than TrueCar’s $31,517 average paid for a new 2026 LX. Used loans averaged 11.19% in the second quarter of 2026. A HondaTrue Certified 2023 to 2025 is the used route we’d take.
