Camry generations: what changed

Seventh generation 2016–2017

The 2012 to 2017 Camry ended its U.S. run in May 2017. It came with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder (177 to 181 hp), a 3.5-liter V6 (268 hp) and a hybrid, with a six-speed automatic on the gas cars, and Toyota facelifted it for 2015 (Wikipedia).

Eighth generation 2018–2024

It went on sale in late July 2017 as a 2018, with a 2.5-liter four (203 to 207 hp), a 300-hp V6 and a 208-hp hybrid. The four and V6 got an eight-speed automatic. All-wheel drive returned for 2020 along with a V6-only TRD trim, and a 2021 refresh added Android Auto and Toyota Safety Sense 2.5+. U.S. production ended in April 2024 (Wikipedia).

Ninth generation 2025–

Every U.S. Camry is now a hybrid: a 2.5-liter system making 225 hp with front-wheel drive and 232 hp with all-wheel drive, which is optional on every trim. Production began in Kentucky in April 2024, and the 2026 model year added the Nightshade Edition (Wikipedia).

Common Camry problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 2,377 Camry complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Transmission and drivetrain422
  • Engine355
  • Brakes318
  • Air bags280
  • Electrical system271
  • Fuel and hybrid system270
  • Speed control187
  • Steering139
  • Power train, 2018 and 2019 2018–2019 model years326 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 48 per 100,000 sold. The biggest single part count on the two worst years. NHTSA’s field names the part and not what the owner describes, and no recall covers it, so a test drive and an inspection are the only check.
  • Brakes, 2018 and 2019 2018–2019 model years220 complaints naming brakes, 32 per 100,000 sold. Recall 18V-211 covers a brake booster vacuum pump rotor that may have been machined wrong, on 2018 cars, and 21V-890 covers a vacuum pump vane cap that can break on 2018 and 2019 cars. Both can mean a sudden loss of brake assist, and dealers repair or replace the pump free.
  • Fuel system, 2018 to 2020 2018–2020 model years235 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 24 per 100,000 sold. Recalls 20V-012 and 20V-682 cover a low-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine on 2018 to 2020 cars, and 25V-028 repeats the fuel pump fix for some 2018s. Dealers replace the pump assembly free.
  • Engine, 2018 and 2020 2018–2020 model years292 complaints naming engine, 30 per 100,000 sold. Recall 18V-200 covers 2018 four-cylinder engines whose pistons may be oversize and stall the engine, and 20V-064 covers 2020 2.5-liter engines with casting porosity that can leak coolant. Dealers inspect and replace the engine as needed, free.
  • Air bag sensor, 2020 and 2021 2020–2021 model years143 complaints naming air bags, 24 per 100,000 sold. Recall 23V-865 covers a short circuit in the passenger occupant sensor that can stop the front passenger air bag from deploying, on 2020 to 2022 Camrys and Camry Hybrids. Dealers inspect and replace the sensor free; the VIN lookup shows whether it is open.
  • Body and structure, 2025 2025 model years33 complaints naming body and structure, 10 per 100,000 sold. Our Camry price guide counted about 27 of the 2025’s complaints describing a front bumper cover that comes loose, and about 21 a cracked windshield. Neither has a recall.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Camry: 247 mentions in 153 threads, 102 positive and 119 negative, a net of +6 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Camry is 52 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/Camry and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Toyota Camry price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit owners praise the Camry’s staying power and describe the used ones as a safe buy when inspected first. The reliability complaints are small ones, such as paint that chips and older four-cylinders that burn oil. The 2018 and 2019 power train, brake and fuel pump counts above don’t appear among those themes, so the NHTSA figures carry that story.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report Camrys passing 200,000 miles on routine maintenance alone
  • Current and hybrid Camrys draw few reported problems from owners
  • Used Camrys are seen as a safe bet when inspected first

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report paint chipping easily, often within the first year
  • Owners report four-cylinder Camrys from the late 2000s burning oil
  • Owners report the 2025 driver seat lumbar making noise after repairs

Other complaints that come up

  • Dealers and markups. Dealers pile on prep, protection and other fees buyers say to remove
  • Price and value. Used Camrys are priced close to new ones, so buyers call it overpaying
  • Interior and tech. Older model years lack CarPlay and have a poor backup camera

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Inverter bolt 2025–2026 HybridRecall 25V-869. A bolt inside the inverter may not have been tightened, which can cut drive power or cause a fire. Dealers replace the inverter free.
  • Instrument cluster and hazard lights 2025–2026 HybridRecall 26V-511. The instrument cluster display may fail at startup, which turns off the hazard lights. Dealers fix it free.
  • Vacuum pump vane cap 2018–2019Recall 21V-890. The vane cap inside the brake vacuum pump may break and the pump fail, causing a sudden loss of brake assist. Dealers repair or replace the pump free.
  • Brake booster vacuum pump 2018Recall 18V-211. The oil galley in the pump rotor may have been machined wrong, which can cause a sudden loss of brake assist. Dealers replace the pump free.
  • Oversize pistons 2018 2.5-literRecall 18V-200. Pistons larger than specified can stall the engine. Dealers check the production date codes and replace the engine if a piston is from the affected period, free.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump 2018–2020Recall 20V-012 and 20V-682. The in-tank pump can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump assembly with an improved one, free; 25V-028 covers some 2018 cars again.
  • Engine casting porosity 2020 2.5-literRecall 20V-064. Porosity in the engine castings can crack and leak coolant, which can overheat or stall a gas car and damage the engine. Dealers inspect and replace the engine as needed, free.
  • Passenger occupant sensor 2020–2022Recall 23V-865. A short circuit in the sensor can keep the front passenger air bag from deploying. Dealers inspect and replace the sensor free.
  • Lug nuts and rear head restraints 2023–2024Recall 23V-432 and 24V-128. Recall 23V-432 covers loose lug nuts on 2023 cars that can let a wheel detach, and 24V-128 insufficiently welded rear head restraint brackets on 2023 and 2024 cars. Both repairs are free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Brake actuator valve wear 2016–2019 HybridDP19004, closed. A petitioner asked NHTSA in September 2019 to investigate Prius, Camry Hybrid and Avalon Hybrid brake actuator valve wear, which Toyota covers under customer support programs ZJB and ZKK. The petitioner withdrew in August 2020 and NHTSA denied the petition as moot without a finding on the defect.

What to check before you buy a used Camry

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Toyota dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Inverter and display recalls on a 2025 or 2026.Ask the dealer to show 25V-869 (inverter) and 26V-511 (hazard lights and display) are done.
  • Brake and fuel pump recalls on a 2018 or 2019.Confirm 18V-211 (2018 only), 21V-890, 20V-012 and 20V-682 are closed, and on a 2018 that 25V-028 is too.
  • Engine recalls on a 2018 or 2020 four-cylinder.Ask for proof that 18V-200 (2018) or 20V-064 (2020) was done, and check the coolant level and for oil leaks.
  • The air bag sensor on a 2020 to 2022.Confirm 23V-865 is closed. The passenger sensor repair is free at a Toyota dealer.
  • Bumper cover and windshield on a 2025.Our price guide counted loose front bumper covers and cracked windshields in the 2025’s complaints with no recall. Look at the bumper seams and the glass.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s check the brakes, the coolant level and the transmission before you sign, especially on a 2018 or 2019.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Camry has 0 NHTSA complaints and 3 recalls so far, including inverter recall 25V-869 and display recall 26V-511, and it is too new to rate. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2024, the 2024 carries 1 recall (24V-128, rear head restraints) against the 2026’s 3 to confirm.

Common questions

What is the worst Toyota Camry year?

The 2018, at 230 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold and 789 complaints, the most of any year. The 2019 is next at 124.

What is the best used Camry year?

The 2021 to 2025 model years, at 53, 23, 24, 17 and 57 complaints per 100,000. The 2016 and 2017 also rate Buy, at 49 and 40.

Is the 2025 Camry reliable?

It draws 57 complaints per 100,000, near the Camry yardstick of 53, with 181 complaints and six recalls. Ask for proof that inverter recall 25V-869 and display recall 26V-511 are done.

Did Toyota recall the 2018 and 2019 Camry for the brakes and fuel pump?

Yes. Recalls 18V-211 and 21V-890 cover the brake vacuum pump and 20V-012 and 20V-682 the low-pressure fuel pump. The repairs are free at a Toyota dealer, and a VIN lookup shows which are still open on a given car.

How do I check a used Camry for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Toyota dealer.
