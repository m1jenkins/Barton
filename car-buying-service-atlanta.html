The Atlanta car market right now

On November 15, 2026, dealers within 50 miles of downtown Atlanta listed about 69,000 new cars and 80,000 used ones, across roughly 340 franchised dealerships. Atlanta was the only one of the six metros we checked with more used cars listed than new. Ford had the most new cars on the lot, then Chevrolet and Toyota. The median asking price was about $49,000 new and $24,000 used.

Buying a car around Atlanta

Let the used inventory narrow the search

Atlanta has more used cars listed than new ones, so you can be picky. Give us the trims you’d take, the most miles you’d accept and the colors you’d skip, and the search can pass over cars that are almost right and wait for one that is. When one dealer won’t move on price, the same car is usually listed at another store. Tell us what you’d pass on and we build the list from there.

Find out where a used car came from

In a market this large, used cars arrive as lease returns, trade-ins and former rental and fleet cars. Ask the dealer which one a car is, get the VIN and run a history report on it. Two cars at the same price can have very different mileage and service records, so line them up on those before you pick one to drive.

How it works from Atlanta

  1. You pay the flat fee and tell us the car. Model, trims and colors you’d accept, budget, and how far you’d go to pick it up. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.
  2. We find the cars. Your advisor searches new, used and CPO dealer inventory for the model.
  3. We get written offers and negotiate. Each dealer is asked for an out-the-door price in writing. Offers go against each other, and add-ons and padded fees get questioned before you see a contract.
  4. You pick. We send written recommendations and our pick of the best deal. You make the final call, sign the paperwork and pick up the car from the dealer.

Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal. Rare specs and factory orders take longer, and we tell you that up front. We can’t promise a specific discount: the market for a given car sets what a dealer will do. The full process is on how it works.

What stays with you

You decide which car, if any, to buy. You arrange the inspection, pick the financing and insurance, read the contract and sign it. Our fee does not include the car, taxes, registration, inspection, financing, insurance or delivery. We don’t give tax or registration advice for your state; the dealer’s paperwork and your state’s motor vehicle agency are the sources for that.

Common questions

Do you have an office in Atlanta?

No. We’re based in Austin, Texas, and we work with Atlanta-area dealers by phone and email. You deal with one advisor the whole way, and you pick up the car from the dealer.

Do you negotiate used cars in Atlanta?

Yes. Used and CPO cars from dealers are part of both plans, at the same flat fee as a new car.

Can you search for an exact trim, color and mileage?

Yes. Tell us the trims and colors you’d accept and the most miles you’d take. With this many used cars listed, there is room to wait for the right one.

Will you compare used cars across different dealers?

Yes. Each dealer with a fitting car gives a written out-the-door price, and we put the offers side by side with each car’s mileage and trim, so a lower price on a higher-mileage car shows up as exactly that.
