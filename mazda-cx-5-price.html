The bottom line

New, as a 2026

The redesigned 2026 CX-5 is a roomier, calmer compact SUV, and dealers discount it: plan on 4–5% under sticker. U.S. News ranks it first of 14 compact SUVs. There’s no hybrid yet, so it’s rated 26 mpg combined, and Edmunds clocked 9.0 seconds to 60 mph. Price a 2026 Preferred, and choose between Mazda’s 0% financing and its $1,000 cash.

Best when: You want a CX-5 from the last several years with a warranty.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Offers that don’t stack. Mazda’s $1,000 customer cash can’t be combined with its special APR or lease. Ask for the price with each and compare the totals.
  • The model year. A 2026 and 2027 look alike on the lot. The 2027 costs $150 to $550 more above the base, and the 0% is on the 2026.
  • Touchscreen controls. Touchscreen controls replaced the physical buttons, Edmunds says. Try the climate and cruise controls on a test drive.
  • The data plan. Google built-in is free for one year, then needs a monthly subscription. Edmunds says $15.
  • The destination charge. Mazda’s is $1,595. Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book print $1,495, so check the window sticker against any quote.

Is the Mazda CX-5 a good car?

Reviewers split. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.3 out of 10, first of 14 compact SUVs. Edmunds rates it 6.5, 14th of 20 small SUVs, and calls it less engaging to drive than before. IIHS lists it as a Top Safety Pick+.

How much under MSRP can you get a Mazda CX-5?

On an S, Select or Preferred, 4–5% under MSRP is a fair deal and 6% or more a strong one. On the Premium and Premium Plus, 3–4% is fair. That’s our read of Edmunds, Kelley Blue Book and CarWhere figures. Mazda’s $1,000 cash is separate.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 CX-5?

The 2026, for most buyers. It has the same engine and equipment for $150 to $550 less on every trim above the base, plus Mazda’s 0% APR and customer cash. Buy the 2027 if Mazda’s rate covers only the 2027 where you live.

Is there a CX-5 hybrid?

Not yet. Every 2026 and 2027 CX-5 has a 2.5-liter four-cylinder. Mazda says additional powertrain options will be announced in 2027, and Edmunds expects a hybrid to arrive then.

What mpg does the CX-5 get?

The EPA rates every 2026 CX-5 at 26 mpg combined (24 city/30 highway). All-wheel drive is standard, so there’s no front-drive version to compare.

What is the destination charge on a CX-5?

$1,595 ($1,640 in Alaska) on the 2026 and 2027. Mazda’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book still print $1,495.

Does Mazda offer cash back on the CX-5?

Yes. On November 18, 2026, Mazda offered $1,000 customer cash on a 2026 S, Select or Preferred in Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York. It can’t be combined with Mazda’s special APR or lease.

Is a used CX-5 a better deal than new?

Only if you want to spend under about $24,000. A used 2025 asks a median $26,669 at about 30,500 miles, and Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 2.5 S is $29,875 with destination. The used 2025 is also the smaller, previous-generation CX-5.
