Dealers and lenders pay us nothing.

The CX-5 was redesigned for 2026, and it’s a roomier, calmer compact SUV that dealers are willing to discount. Edmunds found the back seat expansive, the ride smooth and the cabin quiet, U.S. News ranks it first of 14 compact SUVs, and IIHS lists it as a Top Safety Pick+. The catches: there’s no hybrid yet, so it’s rated 26 mpg combined, and Edmunds clocked 9.0 seconds to 60 mph, among the slowest small SUVs it has timed. Edmunds scores it only 6.5 out of 10, 14th of 20, because the driving feel went soft and most controls moved onto the touchscreen. Supply is generous, too: Edmunds counts about 27,300 new CX-5s and a median 57 days to sell one, against 34 for the average new vehicle, and its Texas suggested prices run 4–5% under sticker. Price a 2026 Preferred, aim for 4–5% under MSRP, and ask whether the dealer’s quote uses Mazda’s 0% financing or its $1,000 customer cash, because you can’t have both. What to pay: how far under MSRP

CX-5s are plentiful. Edmunds counts about 27,300 new ones for sale and says they take a median 57 days to sell, against 34 for the average new vehicle. Its suggested prices, built from Texas sales through November 2, run 5.1–5.3% under sticker on the S, Select and Preferred and 3.9–4.2% under on the Premium and Premium Plus. Kelley Blue Book’s fair purchase price is $1,385 to $1,585 under MSRP (3.6–5.0% by trim), CarWhere’s 18 verified buyers paid a median 5% under (the middle half 3–7.3%), and iSeeCars puts dealer invoice 2.5% under MSRP.

The trim we’d buy

A 2026 2.5 S Preferred. At $34,250 it costs $2,260 more than a Select, and the step up buys heated front seats and a heated steering wheel, a power liftgate, a 10-way power driver’s seat with memory, 19-inch wheels and a color head-up display. Edmunds’ own pick is the Select, but it says to splurge on the Preferred if you live somewhere cold, and it lists the Preferred as the most popular trim. Mazda’s $1,000 customer cash covers the S, Select and Preferred.

Pick the Select ($31,990) if price comes first: it adds leatherette seats and wireless CarPlay to the base car. Skip the Premium: its panoramic roof, leather and Bose audio cost $2,650 more than a Preferred. Take the Premium Plus only for the 15.6-inch screen and 360-degree camera; it’s also the only trim whose headlights IIHS rates Good.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Offers that don’t stack. Mazda’s $1,000 customer cash can’t be combined with its special APR or lease. Ask the dealer for the price with each, and compare the total cost with your bank’s rate.
  • The model year. A 2026 and a 2027 look alike on the lot. The 2027 costs $150 to $550 more on every trim above the base, and Mazda’s 2.9% rate in Chicago, Dallas and New York is on the 2027 only, while the 0% is on the 2026.
  • Touchscreen controls. Edmunds says the physical buttons were replaced by touchscreen controls and that the steering wheel’s touch-sensitive panels didn’t always register presses. Try the climate and cruise controls on a test drive, with the screen in use.
  • The data plan. Google built-in is free for one year through Mazda Connected Services, then needs a monthly subscription (Edmunds says $15).
  • The destination charge. Mazda’s is $1,595. Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book print $1,495, so check the window sticker against any quote.
  • Headlights below the top trim. IIHS rates the Premium Plus headlights Good and every other trim’s Acceptable.
  • Early owner complaints. NHTSA has 20 on the 2026 so far, mostly about the electrical system (6), forward collision avoidance (4) and wipers or visibility (3). Edmunds’ owner summary mentions screen problems and warning lights.
  • Small-item storage and towing. Edmunds finds no extra shelves or cubbies and a small center bin, and rates towing at only 1,500 pounds.

Common questions

Is the Mazda CX-5 a good car?

Reviewers split. U.S. News scores the 2026 a 9.3 out of 10, first of 14 compact SUVs, while Edmunds rates it 6.5, 14th of 20 small SUVs, and calls it less engaging to drive than before. Kelley Blue Book’s owners give it 4.2 out of 5 (6 reviews), IIHS lists it as a Top Safety Pick+, and iSeeCars counts 20.0% of its value lost in three years, against 24.8% for the average vehicle.

How much under MSRP can you get a Mazda CX-5?

On a 2026 S, Select or Preferred, 4–5% under MSRP is a fair deal and 6% or more a strong one; on the Premium and Premium Plus, 3–4% is fair. Those ranges are our read of Edmunds’ Texas prices (5.1–5.3% under on the lower trims), Kelley Blue Book’s fair purchase prices and CarWhere’s 18 verified buyers (a median 5% under), so a given dealer may land a point either side. Mazda’s $1,000 customer cash is separate.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 CX-5?

The 2026, for most buyers. It’s the same engine and equipment for $150 to $550 less on every trim above the base, and it carries Mazda’s 0% APR and customer cash. Buy the 2027 if Mazda’s rate covers only the 2027 where you live (the 2.9% rate in Chicago, Dallas and New York) or you want one before the 2026s run out.

Is there a CX-5 hybrid?

Not yet. Every 2026 and 2027 CX-5 has a 2.5-liter four-cylinder. Mazda says additional powertrain options will be announced in 2027, and Edmunds expects a hybrid to arrive then.

What mpg does the CX-5 get?

The EPA rates every 2026 CX-5 at 26 mpg combined (24 city/30 highway). All-wheel drive is standard, so there’s no front-drive version to compare.

What is the destination charge on a CX-5?

$1,595 ($1,640 in Alaska) on the 2026 and 2027, per Mazda’s releases. Mazda’s advertised starting prices leave it out, and Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book still print $1,495.

Does Mazda offer cash back on the CX-5?

Yes. In Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York on November 18, 2026, Mazda offered $1,000 customer cash on a 2026 S, Select or Preferred, on top of 0% financing in three regions and loyalty and military cash. The customer cash can’t be combined with Mazda’s special APR or lease.

Is a used CX-5 a better deal than new?

Only if you want to spend under about $24,000. CarWhere’s median asking price for a used 2025 is $26,669 at about 30,500 miles, and Edmunds’ Texas price for a new 2026 2.5 S is $29,875 with destination. A used 2025 is also the smaller, previous-generation CX-5, and used loans averaged 11.19% in the second quarter of 2026.
