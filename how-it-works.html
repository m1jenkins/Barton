How our car buying service works.

Drive Right is an Austin-based car buying and negotiation service supporting buyers nationwide. Choose a plan, share your buying brief, review the available vehicles and written terms, and keep control of every purchase decision. See what a car buying service can handle.

  1. 01

    Describe your car.

    A family SUV is a good starting point. Tell us the kind of car you need, your car-price budget, and your ZIP. No exact model required.

  2. 02

    Review your brief.

    Keep what matters. Edit any answer and add year, mileage, radius, color, trim, or transmission. Mark details Must-have or Prefer in the editor.

  3. 03

    Choose your plan.

    Choose Full Service for research, price negotiation, fee review and documented vehicle recommendations, or Ultimate Concierge for expanded sourcing and coordination.

  4. 04

    Make an introduction.

    Add your name and email so we can follow up. Phone is optional at checkout, and your brief comes with you.

  5. 05

    Pay the service fee.

    Pay the service fee securely with Stripe. After payment verification, complete the remaining intake details. Your advisor confirms scope and expected timing. You retain the final decision on every vehicle and deal.

How to evaluate the fee

Start with the tasks you want to hand over to a car negotiator: finding candidates, comparing written quotes, or communicating about price and fees with participating sellers. Compare the $295 Full Service scope with the $895 Concierge plan before choosing. You decide which vehicle to buy and arrange any independent inspection, financing, insurance and final signing.

Remote service does not imply a local office or an in-person visit. Seller participation, vehicle availability and delivery options vary; confirm engagement-specific logistics with your advisor.

Compare the listed fee and scope with the work you want to delegate. Drive Right does not promise a specific discount, time saving, vehicle outcome, loan approval, interest rate, or return on the fee. Ask what deliverables you will receive, what happens if the search changes, and which expenses remain your responsibility.

Compensation and independence disclosure

Drive Right's statements about dealer referrals, commissions, kickbacks, or other third-party compensation are pending owner attestation and supporting documentation. This page does not represent that those relationships do or do not exist. Prospective clients should request a current written disclosure before paying if compensation relationships could affect their decision.

Scope and limitations

  • Drive Right is not identified on this site as a lender, insurer, vehicle inspector, law firm, tax adviser, or government agency.
  • Vehicle condition, seller representations, financing terms, taxes, registration, warranties, and contracts require separate review with the relevant qualified provider or official source.
  • Inventory, pricing, incentives, lending offers, and transaction timing can change. Confirm material terms when making the purchase decision.
