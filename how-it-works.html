How our car buying service works.

Drive Right is an Austin-based car buying and negotiation service supporting buyers nationwide. Choose a plan, share your search details, review the available vehicles and written terms, and keep control of every purchase decision. See what a car buying service can handle.

  1. 01

    Pick your plan.

    Choose Full Service for research, price negotiation, fee review and documented vehicle recommendations, or Ultimate Concierge for expanded sourcing and coordination. Pay securely with Stripe.

  2. 02

    Tell us the car.

    Right after checkout, tell us the kind of car you need, your car-price budget and where you are. No exact model required. Mason replies within one business day to confirm the search and when it starts.

  3. 03

    We do the legwork. You decide.

    We search, contact dealers, compare offers and negotiate. You review the vehicle, the deal and the paperwork, and you keep the final decision on every vehicle and deal.

How to evaluate the fee

Start with the tasks you want to hand over to a car negotiator: finding candidates, comparing written quotes, or communicating about price and fees with participating sellers. Compare the $395 Full Service scope with the $695 Concierge plan before choosing. You decide which vehicle to buy and arrange any independent inspection, financing, insurance and final signing.

Remote service does not imply a local office or an in-person visit. Seller participation, vehicle availability and delivery options vary; confirm engagement-specific logistics with your advisor.

Compare the listed fee and scope with the work you want to delegate. Drive Right does not promise a specific discount, time saving, vehicle outcome, loan approval, interest rate, or return on the fee. Ask what deliverables you will receive, what happens if the search changes, and which expenses remain your responsibility.

Compensation and independence disclosure

Drive Right does not take commissions, kickbacks, or other payments from dealers or sellers. You pay the service fee, and you decide whether to buy.

Scope and limitations

  • Drive Right is not identified on this site as a lender, insurer, vehicle inspector, law firm, tax adviser, or government agency.
  • Vehicle condition, seller representations, financing terms, taxes, registration, warranties, and contracts require separate review with the relevant qualified provider or official source.
  • Inventory, pricing, incentives, lending offers, and transaction timing can change. Confirm material terms when making the purchase decision.
