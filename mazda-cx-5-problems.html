The best used CX-5s are the 2022 to 2025 model years, which draw 46, 44, 35 and 21 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a yardstick of 46 for this car. Skip the 2016 (456), 2017 (118), 2019 (179) and 2021 (90). The 2019 and 2021 have the engine at the center: 137 of the 2019’s 277 complaints and 84 of the 2021’s 152 name it, and in the complaint text we read most of those mention oil. The 2018 (84) and 2020 (75) sit in between and are Check first.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints by Mazda’s U.S. CX-5 sales. The CX-5 is a small-count car in NHTSA’s files, with no year above 512 complaints, so one recall or one part can move a rate a long way; a daytime-running-light recall explains 161 of the 2016’s 512 complaints. CX-5 generations: what changed

First generation 2013–2016

The first CX-5 came with a 2.0-liter engine for 2013, added an optional 2.5-liter for 2014 and made it standard on almost every trim at a 2016.5 refresh (Wikipedia). The 2016 on this page is the last year of that design.

Second generation 2017–2025

The second generation made the 2.5-liter the only engine for 2017, added cylinder deactivation (shutting some cylinders off to save fuel) as standard for 2018, brought a 2.5-liter turbo and a short-lived diesel for 2019, and made all-wheel drive standard for 2022 (Wikipedia). A facelift was revealed in September 2021.

Third generation 2026–

The redesigned CX-5 reached North America in January 2026 with the naturally aspirated 2.5-liter as its only U.S. engine (Wikipedia).

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the CX-5: 606 mentions in 580 threads, 357 positive and 206 negative, a net of +30 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the CX-5 is 62 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/CX5 and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Mazda CX-5 price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Reddit’s engine talk centers on cold-start ticking and rarely mentions the oil complaints that lead the 2018, 2019 and 2021 rows, so the complaint counts here carry that part of the record. The electrical and software reports on the 2026 match its early NHTSA complaints.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners report 100,000 miles and more with only routine maintenance
  • Owners rate it about as dependable as the RAV4 and CR-V
  • Conventional six-speed automatic and naturally aspirated engine avoid CVT worries

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report electrical faults and software bugs on the 2026 redesign
  • Owners report cold-start engine ticking that dealers often call normal
  • Owners report torque converter and transmission failures on 2017 and newer automatics

Other complaints that come up

  • Fuel economy. Owners report roughly 18 to 22 mpg in the city with the turbo
  • Space and practicality. Owners report the rear seat is tight for tall adults and rear-facing child seats

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Engine stall software 2018–2019Recall 19V-497. A software error in the powertrain control module may stall the engine. Dealers reprogram it, free. It also covers the Mazda6 and the 2019 Mazda3.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump 2018–2019Recall 21V-875. The pump impeller may crack and deform, which can stall the engine. Dealers replace the pump, free.
  • Curtain air bags 2018Recall 18V-426. In a side impact or rollover the side curtain air bags may not unfold properly. Dealers replace them, free.
  • LED daytime running lights 2016Recall 20V-063. The lights may fail while driving, which reduces visibility to other drivers. Dealers inspect the headlights and replace the gasket or assemblies, free.
  • Fuel filler pipe 2014–2016Recall 16V-064. The filler pipe may rupture in a crash and leak fuel. Dealers change how the pipe attaches to its bracket, free.
  • Accessory trailer hitch wiring 2017 with a hitch harnessRecall 17V-744. The accessory harness may be routed too close to the muffler and get damaged, which can knock out trailer lights. It affects only cars fitted with it; dealers reroute or replace it, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

What to check before you buy a used CX-5

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Mazda dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Engine oil on a 2018 to 2021.Owners’ NHTSA complaints on these years mention oil often. Check the dipstick level and color, ask for service records showing oil changes on schedule, and have a shop look for oil leaks.
  • Engine stall and fuel pump recalls.On a 2018 or 2019 confirm 19V-497 (software) and 21V-875 (fuel pump) are closed; on a 2018, 18V-426 too.
  • Daytime running lights on a 2016.Confirm 20V-063 is done and that both lights work in the daytime.
  • Transmission behavior.Reddit owners report torque converter and transmission failures on 2017 and newer automatics. Ask for the service history and drive it cold, through stop-and-go traffic.
  • Software and screens on a 2026.Reddit owners report electrical faults and software bugs on the redesign. Ask the dealer to confirm every software update is installed and that nothing is waiting.
  • An independent inspection.NHTSA lists few recalls for the CX-5 after 2019, so the VIN lookup says little about condition. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look at the engine and the transmission before you sign.

Common questions

What is the worst Mazda CX-5 year?

The 2016 has the highest rate, 456 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold, partly from a daytime-running-light recall. Of the later years the 2019 is worst, at 179, with 137 of its 277 complaints about the engine.

What is the best used CX-5 year?

The 2022 to 2025 model years, at 46, 44, 35 and 21 complaints per 100,000 sold. NHTSA lists no recalls for them.

Does the CX-5 burn oil?

Owners’ NHTSA complaints on the 2018, 2019 and 2021 name the engine most often, and in the text we read most mention oil. It isn’t a recall, so a VIN lookup won’t show it; check the dipstick and the service records.

Is the 2026 Mazda CX-5 reliable?

It is too new to rate: 22 complaints and no recalls so far on a redesigned car, with Reddit owners reporting electrical faults and software bugs.

How do I check a used CX-5 for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Mazda dealer.
