The best used Tucsons are the 2020, 2021, 2023 and 2024 model years, which draw 146, 56, 86 and 77 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold against a yardstick of 146 for this car. Skip the 2016 to 2019 and the 2022. The 2016 and 2017 are far above anything else (1,812 and 956), with the engine named in 430 and 619 complaints; the 2018 (259) and 2019 (282) fall under the Avoid line on rate but carry the same engine pattern, so we rate them Avoid too. The 2022 draws 293, a point over the line, with the engine (161) and the fuel system (158) leading. The 2025 (197) is Check first: 222 of its 461 complaints name automatic braking, which Hyundai’s free recall 26V-316 covers.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints, counted across the gas, Hybrid and Plug-in Hybrid listings, by Hyundai’s U.S. Tucson sales; the release has one Tucson row and doesn’t say whether it includes the hybrids, whose complaints are in the count; if it doesn’t, the 2022 rate is overstated. The 2022 was the first year of the current generation and the 2016 the first of the one before. The 2026 is too new to rate and has six recalls. Each rate divides a model year’s complaints by the U.S. sales of the same calendar year. Model years and calendar years don’t line up, since a model year’s cars sell across two calendar years, so the rate compares years with each other. Sales come from Hyundai’s year-end releases and Good Car Bad Car’s Tucson table, the same figures our Tucson comparison pages use; 2016 to 2018 come from Good Car Bad Car alone. Our verdicts use this car’s own yardstick, the median of 146 complaints per 100,000 across its 2019 to 2025 model years: Buy up to 1.25 times it, Check first up to twice it, Avoid above that. A recall, free or not, never moves a verdict. A year can move when NHTSA has an open investigation on it or one part carries most of its complaints,; the reason column says so.

Tucson generations: what changed

Third generation 2016–2021

The TL Tucson went on sale in July 2015 as a 2016 with a 164-hp 2.0-liter and a 175-hp 1.6-liter turbo with a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission. The 2019 refresh dropped the dual-clutch and the manual for a six-speed automatic and replaced the turbo in most trims with a 181-hp 2.4-liter (Wikipedia).

Fourth generation 2022–

The redesigned Tucson is built in Montgomery, Alabama (Wikipedia). Hybrid and plug-in hybrid complaints appear in NHTSA’s files from 2022, and our Tucson price guide covers the current lineup.

Common Tucson problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 5,197 Tucson complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Engine, 2016 to 2019 2016–2019 model years1,503 complaints naming engine, 303 per 100,000 sold. The engine is the leading part in 2017 (619), 2018 (249) and 2019 (205). In the text we read, most mention oil, many mention knocking or stalling, and some mention a fire. Recall 21V-727 covers connecting rod bearing wear on 2017 cars; Hyundai inspects the engine, replaces it if bearings are damaged and installs knock-detection software, free. No recall covers the other years.
  • Transmission, 2016 2016 model years518 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 577 per 100,000 sold. The 2016 has 518 power train complaints, and about half the ones we read mention the transmission or clutch. Recall 16V-628 covers seven-speed dual-clutch software that can leave the car unable to move after repeated pedal presses; dealers update it, free.
  • Brake control unit fire risk, 2016 to 2021 2016–2021 model years316 complaints naming brakes, 41 per 100,000 sold. Recall 20V-543 covers an anti-lock brake control unit that can corrode inside and short, which can start an engine compartment fire. Dealers replace a fuse, and on 2019 to 2021 cars update the stability control software, free. Our Tucson price guide says to confirm it is done.
  • Fuel injectors on the 2022 2022 model years158 complaints naming fuel and hybrid system, 90 per 100,000 sold. The 2022 has 158 fuel system complaints, and most we read name fuel injectors and a warning light. No recall or investigation covers it, and NHTSA’s field says nothing about cause.
  • Automatic braking, 2025 on 2025–2026 model years267 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 114 per 100,000 sold. Recall 26V-316 covers front camera software that can make the forward collision system brake unexpectedly on 2025 and 2026 Tucsons, Hybrids and Plug-in Hybrids; dealers update it, free. It accounts for most of the 2025’s 222 braking complaints, and the 2026 has 45 so far.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Tucson: 554 mentions in 503 threads, 194 positive and 314 negative, a net of −28 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Tucson is 62 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/HyundaiTucson and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The oil-burning and engine-failure reports match the 2016 to 2019 engine counts in the table. The 2025 braking recall barely shows up, and the 2025 keyless and software reports fit its 131 electrical complaints.

What owners praise about reliability

  • Owners of 2022 and newer Tucsons report few or no problems at 20k to 60k miles
  • Newer generation is said to be proving reliable after earlier engine trouble
  • Some high-mileage older Tucsons reached 100k to 180k miles with few repairs

What owners report going wrong

  • Owners report older 2.0 and 1.6 turbo engines burning oil or failing near 100k miles
  • Owners report keyless entry faults, warning codes and software glitches on 2025 cars
  • Owners report the rear window trim panel separating and needing repeat warranty repairs

Our own reading of public Reddit comments from the Arctic Shift archive, January 2025 onward, collected November 18, 2026; the method is in the price guide. Complaints are what owners report, not confirmed defects.

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Connecting rod bolts 2025–2026Recall 25V-549. Bolts that were tightened wrong can cost drive power and damage the engine, with an oil leak that can reach hot parts. Dealers replace the engine, free.
  • Automatic braking software 2025–2026Recall 26V-316. Front camera software may make the forward collision system brake early and unexpectedly. Dealers update the software, free. It covers the Hybrid and Plug-in Hybrid too.
  • Front strut fasteners 2026 HybridRecall 26V-692. Strut fasteners that aren’t tight enough can cost steering control. Dealers tighten them and align the wheels, free.
  • Brake control unit 2016–2021Recall 20V-543. The anti-lock brake control unit can corrode and short, which can start an engine compartment fire. Dealers replace a fuse and, on 2019 to 2021 cars, update the stability control software, free.
  • Connecting rod bearings 2017Recall 21V-727. Bearings can wear early and damage the engine, which can stall it or start a fire. Hyundai inspects the engine, replaces it if bearings are damaged and adds knock-detection software, free.
  • Power steering circuit board 2024Recall 24V-412. The circuit board can short and leave the car without power steering assist. Dealers replace the electric power pack, free.
  • Transmission control software 2016 with the dual-clutchRecall 16V-628. Repeated accelerator presses in heat can leave the car unable to move. Dealers update the transmission control software, free.
  • Idle stop-and-go oil pump 2023–2024Recall 23V-526. The controller for the electric oil pump can overheat and start a fire. Owners are told to park outside until it is fixed; dealers replace the controller as needed, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

NHTSA’s public investigation list showed no investigation naming the Tucson on November 21, 2026.

What to check before you buy a used Tucson

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Hyundai dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • Brake control unit recall on a 2016 to 2021.Confirm 20V-543 is closed. It is a fire recall and the fix is a fuse, so a dealer can print the history in minutes.
  • The engine on a 2016 to 2019.Ask for service records showing regular oil changes, check the dipstick, and listen for knocking on a cold start. On a 2017, confirm 21V-727 was done and ask whether the engine was ever replaced.
  • Transmission on a 2016.Confirm 16V-628 on a dual-clutch car and ask the seller about hesitation or lurching in traffic.
  • Engine replacement recall on a 2025 or 2026.Ask whether 25V-549 has been done; Hyundai replaces the engine on affected cars.
  • Braking and display software on a 2025 or 2026.Confirm 26V-316 (braking) and 26V-400 (instrument display) are installed, and on a 2026 Hybrid 26V-692 too.
  • An independent inspection.Reddit owners report oil burning on older turbo and 2.0-liter engines, keyless entry faults on 2025 cars and rear window trim that separates. Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look at the engine and the trim before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

The 2026 Tucson has 106 NHTSA complaints and 6 recalls so far, among them engine replacement recall 25V-549, so it is too early to call. If you are choosing between a new 2026 and a used 2023 or 2024, the used years have the lower complaint counts, and the 2024 has four recalls to confirm.

Common questions

What is the worst Hyundai Tucson year?

The 2016, at 1,812 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold, then the 2017 at 956. The engine and power train lead both, and the dual-clutch 2016 has a transmission software recall (16V-628).

What is the best used Tucson year?

The 2021 has the lowest rate, 56. The 2020, 2023 and 2024 are also Buy, at 146, 86 and 77. Confirm recall 20V-543 on the 2020 and 2021.

Is the 2022 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

It draws 293 complaints per 100,000 sold, a point over our Avoid line, with the engine (161) and the fuel system (158) leading. It’s the first year of the current generation, and we’d choose a 2023 or 2024 instead.

Does the Hyundai Tucson have engine problems?

On the 2016 to 2019 it does: the engine is named in 430, 619, 249 and 205 complaints. Recall 21V-727 covers one 2017 failure, and Hyundai replaces the engine on affected 2025 and 2026 cars under 25V-549.

How do I check a used Tucson for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that car, and recall repairs are free at a Hyundai dealer.
