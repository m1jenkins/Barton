What a car buying concierge does

The word comes from hotels, and the idea is the same. You say what you want and someone else makes the calls. For a car that means four jobs: finding dealers that have the car, getting each one to put an out-the-door price in writing, negotiating those prices and the fees against each other, and telling you plainly which offer to take.

At Steer Me Right one advisor does all four, from the first message to the signed deal. We search new, used and CPO dealer inventory, work the dealers by phone and email, check the fee lines and send written recommendations: what we found and what we’d do. You never sit in a sales office waiting on a manager.

What a concierge doesn’t do, at least here

Some people hear “concierge” and picture a car arriving in the driveway with a bow on it. That isn’t what you’re buying from us, and it’s better to know now. You still choose the car, arrange your own financing and insurance, read the contract and sign it. You pick the car up from the dealer.

Two things on Steer Me Right Pro get close to the white-glove picture. We handle the paperwork remotely where we can, so you mostly just pick up the car. And when the best deal is far away, we get shipping quotes. You pay the carrier; shipping is not part of our fee.

We also only work with dealers. Private sellers and auctions are not part of any plan.

Which plan is the concierge one

All of them, really. On the Steer Me Right plan ($395) you already know the model, and we do the finding and haggling for that one car at dealers within 200 miles of you. Steer Me Right Pro ($695) is closer to what most people mean by a concierge: we search dealers nationwide, get shipping quotes on long-distance deals, work your trade-in into the deal, price up to three models side by side and tell you which deal we’d take, judged on out-the-door price plus resale and running costs. For a car with an MSRP of $100,000 or more, Steer Me Right Private Client ($1,995) adds allocation and factory-order work, negotiation of market adjustments and a paperwork check before you sign.

Ours is a flat fee, paid once: $395 or $695, or $1,995 for Private Client on a car with an MSRP of $100,000 or more. It does not change with the price of the car, and we don’t take a cut of anything. Common questions

What is a car buying concierge?

A person or service you pay to shop for a car for you: find it, get written offers, negotiate and guide you to the signing. Steer Me Right does this for a flat $395 or $695.

Is a car concierge worth it?

It depends on what your time and patience are worth, and we can’t promise a specific discount. What you get for certain is the hours back, written offers from several dealers and someone checking the fees. Most searches take one to two weeks from payment to a signed deal.

Do you deliver the car to my house?

No. You pick up the car from the dealer. On Steer Me Right Pro, we get shipping quotes when the best deal is far away. You pay the carrier; shipping is not part of our fee.

Do you work with used cars?

Yes. Used and CPO cars from dealers are part of every plan, at the same flat fee as a new car. See our used car buying service page.

Do dealers pay you?

No. Dealers and lenders pay us nothing. You pay the flat fee, and you’re the only one we work for.
