How we set these discount targets

Rams don’t sell at sticker. Cox counted 148 days of supply for Ram at the end of October against 87 for the industry, and Edmunds says new 2026 Ram 1500s are taking 126 days to sell against 34 for the average new vehicle. Edmunds’ November tips put the average buyer about $4,400 under MSRP (7–8%), CarWhere’s 59 verified 2026 buyers paid a median 9.1% under, and Kelley Blue Book’s fair purchase prices run 5–10% under. Edmunds’ suggested prices, based on Texas sales through November 2 and including destination, run 17–20% under its MSRP on every trim except the RHO (10.6%), because Ram’s cash and employee pricing are large this month.

  • Tradesman, Express, Warlock. Kelley Blue Book’s fair purchase price is 7–8% under. Ram’s cash is $1,500 to $4,000 on these trims. Dealer invoice sits 4% under.
  • Big Horn, Lone Star. Kelley Blue Book puts the fair price 8–9% under. Ram’s cash is $1,750 to $6,000, the most on any trim for a Crew Cab 4x4.
  • Laramie, Rebel, Limited, Limited Longhorn, Tungsten. Kelley Blue Book is 5–10% under and Edmunds says the Tungsten sells 8.3% under. Ram’s cash is $5,250, or $6,750 in Los Angeles. Invoice sits about 6–7% under.
  • RHO. Ram lists no cash on the RHO. Kelley Blue Book is 6% under and invoice 6.4% under, while Edmunds’ Texas price is 10.6% under.

New 2026

Best when: Most buyers. Employee pricing, bonus cash, 0% financing and the 10-year powertrain warranty offer all apply to the 2026, and it costs $400 to $1,200 less than a 2027.

Check: That the truck is a 2026, that the cash is for that exact trim and cab in your ZIP, and that you take delivery by November 30.

New 2027

Best when: The 2026 you want is gone, or you want a 2027-only version such as the Rumble Bee or TRX SRT.

Check: The price rise ($400 to $550, $1,200 on the RHO), and that Ram shows only employee pricing and 5.9% financing on it in Chicago, with no cash.

CPO (Ram Certified Pre-Owned)

Best when: You want a 2023 to 2025 with a warranty and don’t mind buying through a dealer.

Check: Ram’s terms are a 125-point inspection, a 3-month/3,000-mile Maximum Care warranty and 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage with a $100 deductible. Ram’s page gives no age or mileage limit, so ask the dealer.

Used, not certified

Best when: Your budget is under about $33,000.

Check: Open recalls by VIN, an independent inspection, and on a 2019 to 2022 whether the seat belt anchorage recall 26V495 and the tailgate hinge recall 22V904 are done.

Buying used? We’d take a certified 2023 to 2025 with its recalls done, starting with 26V495 (second-row seat belt anchorage). The 2019 drew 1,505 NHTSA complaints and the 2020 to 2022 trucks 645 to 692 each, against 211 for 2023 and 79 for 2024.

What to watch for at the dealer

  • Which cash you get. It differs by trim, cab, bed and region. The Big Horn’s $6,000 covers only the oldest 30% of eligible stock. Ask which tier yours is in.
  • Delivery by November 30. Every 2026 offer needs delivery by that date. A 2027 looks the same on the lot, costs more and shows no cash.
  • 0% or cash. Ram’s 0% can’t be combined with other offers. Price the truck both ways and compare the total.
  • Reliability. Edmunds scored it 3 out of 10 after its one-year test truck’s electronic problems. J.D. Power’s owners rate quality and reliability 73 of 100, which it calls average.
  • Recalls. Ask the dealer to show every open recall is done, especially 26V495 and the 2026 headlight, air bag and tire pressure recalls (26V421, 26V622, 26V623).

Is the Ram 1500 a good truck?

For comfort, yes. Edmunds gives the 2026 a 7.5 out of 10 and says it rides more comfortably than any rival. U.S. News gives it 9.5, second of six full-size pickups. Edmunds scored its reliability 3 out of 10.

How much under MSRP can you get a Ram 1500?

On most 2026 trims, 8–10% under MSRP is a fair deal and 12% or more a strong one. The Laramie to Tungsten group runs 7–9%, and the RHO about 5–6%. That’s our estimate, so a dealer may land a point either side.

Should I buy a 2026 or a 2027 Ram 1500?

The 2026, for most buyers. It costs $400 to $1,200 less, and Ram’s cash and 0% financing apply to it through November 30. The 2027 gets employee pricing and a 5.9% rate in Chicago.

What is the destination charge on a Ram 1500?

$2,795 on both years. Ram’s advertised starting prices leave it out, so a $42,025 Tradesman is $44,820 on the window sticker.

Does Ram offer cash back on the 1500?

Yes, on the 2026. In Chicago, Dallas and New York it’s $1,500 to $5,250 depending on trim, with $6,000 on a Big Horn Crew Cab 4x4. Los Angeles pays up to $6,750. The 0% financing replaces the cash, and the 2027 shows none.

Is the V8 or the turbo six better?

The six. In Edmunds’ tests the 420-hp six reached 60 mph in 5.2 seconds and the 395-hp V8 in 6.9, and Edmunds calls the six quieter and cheaper. Kelley Blue Book calls the V8 the gem of the line on towing and price.

Is a used Ram 1500 a better deal than new?

Rarely. A used 2024 asks a median $37,898 and a 2025 $40,566, while Edmunds’ Texas price for a new Big Horn is $42,883. Used loans averaged 11.19% against Ram’s 0%. A certified 2023 to 2025 with its recalls done is the used route we’d take.
