The best used Colorados are the 2018 to 2022 model years and the 2024 and 2025, which draw from 26 to 136 NHTSA owner complaints per 100,000 sold. Skip the 2016 and 2017. The 2016 draws 660, with 466 of its 718 complaints naming steering and no steering recall on NHTSA’s list for that year, and the 2017 draws 193 with half its complaints on the transmission and drivetrain. The 2023 is Check first at 204, and NHTSA’s open brake investigation EA26006 names 2023 to 2026 Colorados.

The rates divide each model year’s NHTSA complaints by U.S. Colorado sales. They compare years with each other; they don’t predict one truck. Chevrolet redesigned the Colorado for 2023, so the 2016 to 2022 rows are the older truck. The 2026 is too new to rate; our Colorado price guide covers what it costs new.

Colorado generations: what changed

Second generation 2015–2022

A mid-size pickup built at Wentzville, Missouri. It launched with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder and a 3.6-liter V6, a 2.8-liter Duramax turbodiesel joined for 2016, and the ZR2 off-road trim for 2017 (Wikipedia). The GMC Canyon is its mechanical twin; the counts here are Chevrolet’s only.

Third generation 2023–

Built at Wentzville and offered only as a crew cab with one bed length. Its only engine is a 2.7-liter turbo four-cylinder with an eight-speed automatic (Wikipedia).

Common Colorado problems by component

NHTSA files each complaint under the parts the owner names, and one complaint can name more than one. Across all 1,821 Colorado complaints for the 2016 to 2026 model years, these parts come up most:

  • Steering575
  • Transmission and drivetrain429
  • Electrical system352
  • Engine226
  • Brakes190
  • Automatic emergency braking89
  • Fuel and hybrid system81
  • Speed control49
  • Steering 2016 model years466 complaints naming steering, 429 per 100,000 sold. Steering is the 2016’s top complaint by a wide margin (466 of 718). NHTSA’s closed recall query RQ19001 looked at loss of power steering assist on Colorado and Canyon trucks; the recalls behind it, 16V-054 and 21V-213, list only 2015 trucks. A 2016 buyer should ask whether the steering was ever repaired.
  • Transmission and drivetrain 2017–2020 model years286 complaints naming transmission and drivetrain, 61 per 100,000 sold. Power train leads the 2017 to 2020 counts (108, 78, 67 and 33) and has no recall on NHTSA’s list for those years. NHTSA’s label spans the transmission, axles and driveline, so it doesn’t say which part. Owners on Reddit report transmission shudder and hard shifts, most often on eight-speed trucks, which means the 2023 and later.
  • Forward collision avoidance 2023–2024 model years69 complaints naming automatic emergency braking, 41 per 100,000 sold. Recall 24V-133 covers 2023 trucks whose front camera may falsely detect an obstacle and brake on its own (automatic emergency braking). Dealers update the camera software, free. The count is 47 on the 2023 and 22 on the 2024.
  • Brakes 2023–2025 model years75 complaints naming brakes, 27 per 100,000 sold. Brake complaints run 33, 31 and 11 on the 2023 to 2025 trucks. NHTSA’s engineering analysis EA26006 is studying loss of brake assist in GM’s eBoost brake system, which the Colorado shares with several other GM models.

What owners report on Reddit

Reliability is the topic Reddit raises most about the Colorado: 474 mentions in 332 threads, 128 positive and 288 negative, a net of −29 on a scale from −100 to +100. Our Reddit Sentiment Index for the Colorado is 57 out of 100 (50 is even). The threads run from January 2025 to November 2026 and come from r/ChevyColorado and the rest of Reddit, so they say little about a car that owners stopped discussing before 2025. The Chevrolet Colorado price guide has the full topic breakdown.

Owners on Reddit report older Colorados and 2.8 Duramax diesels past 200,000 miles on routine maintenance, and call the 2.7 turbo four tough so far. What they say goes wrong clusters on the newer truck: transmission shudder and hard shifts, a steering column rattle that returns after repair on 2024 to 2026 trucks, and upper radiator hose and coolant leaks on 2023 and 2024 trucks. NHTSA’s complaint labels don’t isolate the rattle or the hose, so Reddit adds problems the table can’t show.

Recalls worth knowing about

  • Driver air bag 2015–2016Recall 17V-621. The driver’s frontal air bag may inflate improperly in the second stage of a high-speed crash. Dealers replace the module, free.
  • High-pressure fuel pump 2016–2018Recall 18V-358. The pump may detach from its mounting flange and damage the high-pressure fuel line. Dealers replace the pump and the fuel pipe, free.
  • Manual passenger seat 2022Recall 22V-375. A hook attachment on a manual front passenger seat may not have been welded properly and can separate from the seat track. Dealers inspect the seat and replace the cushion frame if necessary, free.
  • Unexpected automatic braking 2023Recall 24V-133. The front camera module may falsely detect an obstacle and engage automatic emergency braking. Dealers update the software, free.
  • Front wheel hub bolts 2023Recall 24V-237. The front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged during assembly. Dealers replace the left and right bolts, free.
  • Seat belt buckle bolt 2023–2024Recall 24V-703. A front seat may have a seat belt buckle attachment bolt that was not tightened properly. Dealers tighten the bolts on both front seats, free.
  • Wrong tires 2024Recall 24V-366. Some trucks may have tires that don’t match the tire label, and the tire pressure monitor may then give inaccurate information. Dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free.
  • Fuel pump lock ring 2024Recall 24V-491. The ring that secures the fuel pump to the tank may not have been fully locked, which can leak fuel in a crash. Dealers inspect and lock it, free.

Open and recent NHTSA investigations

  • Loss of brake assist (eBoost) 2023–2026EA26006, open. NHTSA upgraded its preliminary evaluation PE24011 to an engineering analysis on August 21, 2026. It began with 2023 Cadillac Lyriq complaints and now covers GM vehicles with the eBoost brake system, a list that includes the 2023 to 2026 Colorado. No recall had been issued when we checked.
  • Loss of power steering assist 2015RQ19001, closed. NHTSA opened a recall query in 2019 after 50 complaints of lost steering assist on trucks outside the first recall (16V-054), and closed it after GM’s 2021 recall 21V-213 for 2015 Colorado and Canyon trucks.

What to check before you buy a used Colorado

  • Open recalls by VIN.Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall fix is free at a Chevrolet dealer, and the lookup shows which ones are still open on that exact car.
  • The 2016’s steering.NHTSA lists no steering recall for the 2016, yet 466 owners named steering. Ask whether the steering unit was ever replaced and have a shop check it.
  • The transmission on a 2017 to 2020.Power train is the top complaint part on those years with no recall to explain it. Ask for service records and have a shop scan for transmission codes.
  • The recalls on a 2023 or 2024.Confirm 24V-133 (automatic braking software), 24V-237 (hub bolts) and 24V-703 (seat belt bolts) on a 2023, and 24V-366 (tires), 24V-491 (fuel pump lock ring) and 24V-703 on a 2024. The VIN lookup shows what is open.
  • The brake assist on a 2023 to 2026.EA26006 is open and no recall existed when we checked. Read the brake complaints on NHTSA for the model year and ask the dealer about any brake warning messages in the truck’s history.
  • Coolant hoses on a 2023 or 2024.Owners on Reddit report upper radiator hose and coolant leaks. Look for coolant stains and a low reservoir.
  • An independent inspection.Have a shop that isn’t the seller’s look over the steering, transmission and brakes before you sign.

Is the 2026 better?

NHTSA lists 22 complaints and 1 recall for the 2026 Colorado, an owner’s manual notice (26V-114), and the open brake investigation EA26006 names the 2026. With that little on record we can’t say it is more reliable than the 2024 and 2025.

Common questions

What are the worst Chevrolet Colorado years?

The 2016 and 2017 model years. They draw 660 and 193 NHTSA complaints per 100,000 sold against 65 for the 2022, and 466 of the 2016’s 718 complaints name steering.

What is the best used Colorado year?

The 2022 draws 65 and the 2025 26, and the 2018 to 2022 and 2024 trucks all rate Buy. The 2025 is still collecting complaints, so its rate will rise a little.

Is the 2023 Colorado reliable?

It draws 204 complaints per 100,000, the highest since the 2016, and 3 recalls, including one for automatic emergency braking that can engage by mistake (24V-133). We rate it Check first.

Does the GMC Canyon have the same problems?

The two trucks are mechanical twins, but NHTSA counts them under separate names and these figures are the Colorado’s alone. EA26006 and recalls such as 24V-133 name both.

How do I check a used Colorado for recalls?

Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It lists open recalls for that truck; recall repairs are free at a Chevrolet dealer.
